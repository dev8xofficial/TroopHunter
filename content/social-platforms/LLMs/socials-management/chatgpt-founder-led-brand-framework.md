# PILLAR 1 — THE AUTHORITY PROFILE
**Goal:** Cold visitor → Trust → Credibility → Action

**Formula:**
```

RESULT
PROOF
POSITIONING
CTA
```

**Example:**
```
Founder @ Dev8X

```

## Highlight Structure (Trust Funnels)

**Required Highlights:**
- **Start Here** – Who you help, what you solve, why you
- **Proof** – Results, metrics, testimonials
- **Process** – Frameworks, systems, methodology
- **Media** – Podcasts, features, interviews
- **Life** – Founder lifestyle, team, behind-the-scenes

## Grid Strategy (Perception Engineering)
**Ratio Model:**
- 40% Authority
- 30% Human/Affinity
- 30% Assertion/Opinion

**First 9-Post Objective:**
1. Who is this founder?
2. Is he legit?
3. Is he credible?
4. Is he strategic?
5. Is he experienced?
6. Does he operate at scale?
7. Can I trust him?
8. Is he human?
9. Is he worth following/connecting with?

2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Soft CTA

## Outbound (PR-Style Warm DMs)

**Founder Connection Sequence:**

**Message 1:**
"Came across your work at [Company]. Love how you're approaching [specific detail]."

"I run a web systems agency focused on scaling tech platforms — always good to connect with serious builders."
