Southwest Customers can now enjoy easier ways to manage their account while using their mobile devices. 

With this improvement, now you can access mobile friendly versions of the following account features directly in the Southwest app and mobile website:

- View Points Activity
- Enroll and and View Your Loyalty Promotions
- View Ways to Earn Points
- View Ways to Use and Manage Points
- Add and View your Companion (not edit, yet) 
- Request Missing Points

No more needing to pinch and zoom to view Points Activity! No more needing to toggle over and sign in again to our desktop website in order view or enroll in a loyalty promotion! Huzzah!

All of these newly made mobile friendly features are currently behind the “View Points Activity” button in the Quick Links section of your account on our mobile website and app. We’ll work to optimize those links as we monitor usage in the near term.

Learn more about ways Southwest is investing in the Customer Experience at https://lnkd.in/gXag2Vzi

Behind the scenes, this new area of your account is built on our next generation Responsive My Account platform that is much more mobile friendly. 

This is our first phases of launching that new platform, so for now, those features will be linked through this quick link section. We’re still working on adding some niceties like deep linking to specific account sections like Points Activity, so pardon our dust for this first launch. We prioritized getting more account features into your mobile experiences as fast as possible to make it easier to travel with us. We’ll keep adding improvements as we go to make it even easier to use. 

Next we’ll be adding Payment Management, then Trip & Profile Management. We hope to have our next release of added mobile friendly features later this year.
