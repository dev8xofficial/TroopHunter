The Impact of Diabetes on an Individual’s Lifestyle

Nov 2024 – Dec 2024


Associated with University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill

Our project investigated how diabetes influences lifestyle choices, focusing on its physical, mental, and social impacts. Using data from the CDC’s Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS), we analyzed over 250,000 responses to explore relationships between high blood pressure, general health, and diabetes. We developed a decision tree model to predict diabetes outcomes and identified critical indicators such as BMI and self-reported health. By addressing sampling biases and building a balanced dataset, we improved the model's reliability and achieved a 70% accuracy rate. This project highlights how data-driven insights can inform better healthcare systems and early diabetes detection strategies.
