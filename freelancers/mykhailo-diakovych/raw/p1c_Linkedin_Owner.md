As a developer, technical improvement is expected. But stepping into a CTO role expands the horizon. 

You need to continuously grow: not just technically, but also as a manager and a business leader. 

It’s about mastering the technical aspects while also learning to steer the company's strategy and operations. 

Never stop learning. Grow. Adapt. Lead.

The key point is not about having skills. It's about applying them WHERE they make the biggest difference.
