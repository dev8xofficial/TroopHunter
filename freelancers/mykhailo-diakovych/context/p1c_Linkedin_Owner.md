3 lessons that help me run a business as a CTO:
(We started 1 year ago as a company and grew to 12 people in our team)

1) We never start coding without a plan.
In a team, we break big projects into manageable parts.
Then, on team syncs, we review each thoroughly.
This way, we avoid wasted efforts on ineffective solutions.

2) Less is more.
It’s easy to get caught up in adding new tools or complex features to the product.
But that often just leads to headaches and inefficient use of resources.
Instead, we stick to what’s necessary. We focus on what our client needs right now.
Keeping it simple saves time and keeps things running smoothly.

3) Monorepo and Turborepo for Scalability
I use a monorepository enhanced with Turborepo to simplify development processes, improve scalability, and streamline testing. While it can add minor complexity to CI/CD, centralizing code ensures consistency, efficiency, and reusability across projects.

Delivery receipts:

Invoice processing.

Customs documentation.

Use pre-filling forms with stored information and make sure your processing through customs is fast.

Audit preparation. 

Gathering necessary documents for audits can be labor-intensive.
