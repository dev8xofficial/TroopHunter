**I ask a lot of questions.** Not because I'm unsure — because assumptions kill projects. And not just on day one. Assumptions get invalidated constantly as a product evolves, so I keep asking. Tight feedback loops beat big upfront answers every time.

I build web apps that ship. My default is SvelteKit (faster for 99% of projects), but I've shipped plenty of Next.js/React and will happily work in your existing stack without trying to convert you.

**Here's how I'm different:**

Most devs defend one framework. I defend outcomes. I use LLMs to move fast — but I override them constantly because most AI is trained on average developer output. You get AI speed + human judgment.

**What working with me looks like:**

- I ask questions throughout the project, not just at the start. New information changes things. I'd rather adjust constantly than build the wrong thing perfectly.
- Daily Loom videos or written updates — no disappearing acts
- Tight feedback loops — we validate before we build too far
- "Break stuff fast" (metaphorically) to find what users actually love
- No perfectionism. Just working software your users want to use.

**My goal for your project:** Simple, Lovable, Complete (SLC). Not perfect. Just done and loved.

**Best fit for:**
- MVPs that need to launch yesterday
- Internal tools no one wants to maintain
- Existing apps that feel over-engineered and slow
- Greenfield projects where you want someone who picks the right tool, not their favorite
