Full-Stack Dev: AI Integration & SaaS MVP Specialist | NextJS, Laravel
$175.00/hr

Hey, I'm Virgil, a full-stack developer based just outside NYC. I specialize in building SaaS MVPs and integrating AI into existing apps, websites, and workflows. Over the past 5 years, I've helped numerous founders turn their ideas into beautiful and functional applications.

What I can help with:

AI Integration & Development
I work with OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Replicate, Fal.ai, Eleven Labs, and other AI providers to build any workflow you can think of, from simple chatbots, to multi-step complex chains of calls between content generation tools (text, image, audio, video), multimodal LLMs, and external APIs. I also set up advanced automations, either in code within your app, or using n8n (which I can help you self-host) and when appropriate to your needs I can fine tune models.

Full-Stack SaaS & App Development
I handle everything from scoping through deployment, with a focus on building MVPs that look good, function well, and get you to market quickly. On the frontend, I build with React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS. For backends, I typically prefer Laravel or Node.js, along with PostgreSQL, Supabase, or other database solutions depending on what fits your project best.

Getting Your Vibe-Coded Apps to Work
I help a large number of founders who come to me with apps that they built in Lovable, v0, Replit, etc. and help get them fully functional and ready for production.

Deployment & Cloud Services
I deploy on Vercel, DigitalOcean, AWS, Hetzner, Coolify, or whatever infrastructure makes sense for your project. I'm experienced with AWS, Cloudflare, and GCP services for storage, edge computing, and messaging.

Multi-Platform Development
I build custom web apps, cross-platform desktop apps (Electron), and native iOS apps (Swift).

How I work:

I'm a clear, straightforward, and proactive communicator, and I don't use AI to communicate with you! I'll consistently work with you to ensure I understand your vision, that you're kept up to date on the state of the project, and I'll offer suggestions along the way to improve the product.

A bit about me:

I'm originally from Southern California and now live in Beacon, NY with my wife. When I'm not working, you'll find me taking day trips into NYC to get the best bagels on Earth, exploring Hudson Valley towns and trails, snowboarding in winter when I can, or traveling abroad doing the digital nomad thing, sometimes for years at a time. We tend to gravitate towards Europe, Southeast Asia, and Latin America.

If you've got an idea you want to build, let's talk about how I can help make it happen!
