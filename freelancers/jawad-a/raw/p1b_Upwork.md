Early detection is crucial in preventing visual impairment, yet the manual diagnostic process is time-consuming, costly, and prone to human error.

To streamline diagnosis, I developed a CNN-based automated DR detection system trained on a real-world dataset. The dataset was categorized into five labels representing four stages of DR (Mild, Moderate, Severe, and Proliferative) along with a label for No DR. Augmentation techniques such as color and angle augmentation were applied to enhance dataset generalization and balance.

Multiple pre-trained CNN models were trained and evaluated, with the best-performing model selected based on various accuracy metrics. Model testing was conducted on a dataset sourced from ophthalmologists at a private hospital in the city.

To facilitate ease of use for medical professionals, a user-friendly Flask-based User Interface was designed, providing a seamless interaction platform for accessing and utilizing the DR detection system.
