About
Hello! My name is Ryan Brown. Check out my work and learn more about me at ryanbrownmedia.com

I am a Top Rated freelancer on Upwork who is passionate about bringing websites to life for my clients that reflect their brand, vision, and personality. One of the things I take most seriously is being dependable, and providing support and solid communication through your entire site build. 

My typical workflow involves my client's designer, or one of the many talented designers in my network to create the prototype of the site (on Figma, or XD, etc), and then I build it out pixel for pixel. 

I have built a wide range of websites related to the following industries: NFTs, crypto, psychology, authors, Sass, financial institutions,  basketball leagues, fitness studios, coffee shops, equipment rentals, home defense, spirituality, etc. Any chance I have an opportunity to work in a new field, I consider it a chance to become a better version of myself as I get to learn about something new!

I started out building my client's sites from scratch:  Node, Express, Javascript, React, and PostgreSQL was my typical stack that I used. Lately though, I have been specializing in building my client's sites on Webflow! Webflow is an incredible solution that lets me build out any design that my client could want, while incorporating a flexible but powerful CMS into this design. This gives my sites both creative freedom and a way for clients to keep it up date with their own content without having to hire a developer for every change. 

Reach out if you want to partner up and begin the journey of developing a new website with me!
