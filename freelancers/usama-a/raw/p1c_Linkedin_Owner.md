You try again.

LinkedIn User: "Hi, what can you do for me?"

1. You immediately demonstrate empathy, showing you value their time.

2. You establish your value by addressing your persona's title and challenge — a clear sign you understand their world.

3. You create a path forward. You've piqued their interest by addressing a challenge they face daily.

Bottom line - Focus on them, not you.

Next time you send that connection request, remember to tell them how YOU can HELP them.
