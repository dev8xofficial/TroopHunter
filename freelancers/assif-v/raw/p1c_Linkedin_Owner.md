Keep it simple, stupid (KISS) is a design principle which states that designs and/or systems should be as simple as possible.

When programming or acting as a product manager, your job is to mitigate for different possible scenarios and sometimes so many of them. This is how I was taught to "complicate" things.

Now, with Blend By Vitality26 we invented a beauty product that provides customers with fresh, potent and tailor-made creams. Though challenged by the production process, assembly line, customization etc., at its core, Blend is created through a "simple" process.

When applying a "complicated" thought process to a "simple" environment, I created frustration around me. I heard things such as "You need to let things flow..."

Learn to recognize when you over complicate things. Simplify and let things flow. Simplicity adds "air pockets" that are later fueled with a greater power. Power that pushes things forward naturally. Do not try to control and mitigate every possible scenario. Just let things flow…
