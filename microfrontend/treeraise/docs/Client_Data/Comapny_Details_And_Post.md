## About the Company
TreeRaise is a modern fundraising platform built for schools, nonprofits, churches, and community organizations that are ready to move beyond outdated, burnout driven fundraising models.

TreeRaise helps organizations raise real funds while creating verified environmental impact. Every TreeRaise campaign delivers two outcomes:
- Communities keep the funds they raise.
- Forests are restored through transparent, data backed reforestation partnerships.

Traditional fundraisers rely on door to door sales, cash envelopes, and products families do not need. These models create fatigue, inequity, and donor distrust. TreeRaise replaces these systems with a digital first, values aligned approach designed for modern donors and families.

The platform enables organizations to easily launch, share, and manage fundraising campaigns while providing supporters with clear proof of impact. Every campaign includes transparent reporting and verified tree planting data so donors can see exactly what their support creates.

TreeRaise operates at the intersection of fundraising, sustainability, and trust. The company believes communities should never have to choose between raising money and doing good. Each campaign is designed to grow both financial resources and environmental restoration.

TreeRaise works with the following groups across the United States:
- Schools
- PTAs
- Nonprofits
- Churches
- Civic groups
- Mission driven partners

- This is TreeRaise.
