🌿 How we work together

This isn't a bill.
It's a partnership agreement.

TreeRaise only earns money when you earn money. Our platform fee is deducted automatically from campaign proceeds — you never write us a check. We succeed when your community succeeds. That's not a policy, it's a design choice.

92% Goes to your org Starter plan · $10k raised
8% TreeRaise platform fee Only when you raise funds
$0 Upfront cost, ever Free to start · Free to try
🏦
You get paid first
When a donor gives, funds are collected and split instantly. Your share deposits to your bank every 7 days. No waiting for "month-end settlement."
📊
Complete transparency
Every fee deduction is itemized in your invoice history. You'll always know exactly what TreeRaise earned and what your organization received — down to the cent.
📉
The more you grow, the less we take
Our fee drops from 8% to 5% to 3% as your organization scales. Our incentive is to help you raise as much as possible — then get out of the way.
❌
No surprise charges. Ever.
We will never charge your card without a donation event triggering it. No monthly minimums, no "platform maintenance fees," no penalties for low-activity months.
💬
A note from the TreeRaise team
"We designed revenue share because we believe fundraising platforms should be partners, not vendors. When you run a $50,000 campaign, we earn $4,000 and you keep $46,000. We think that's fair. And we think you should always know exactly what you're paying and why."

Destructive Actions & Owner Protection
Any action that deletes data, changes banking, or removes team members requires email confirmation to the Owner — even if the Owner is the one making the change.
🗑️
Delete Campaign
Only the Owner can initiate. When triggered, an email is sent: "Someone requested to delete [Campaign Name]. Was this you?" with a 24-hour Cancel window before deletion executes.
Owner only · Email confirmation required
👤
Remove Team Member
