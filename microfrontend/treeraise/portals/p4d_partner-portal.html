🌿 How we work together
This isn't a bill.
It's a partnership agreement.
TreeRaise only earns money when you earn money. Our fee is deducted from campaign proceeds — you never write us a check. We succeed when your community succeeds.
50%Goes to your org50/50 split · $10k raised
50%TreeRaise shareOnly when you raise funds
$0Upfront cost, everFree to start · Free to try
🏦
You get paid first
When a donor gives, funds are collected and split instantly. Your share deposits to your bank every 7 days.
📊
Complete transparency
Every fee deduction is itemized. You'll always know exactly what TreeRaise earned and what your org received.
🤝
A true 50/50 partnership
Every dollar raised is split equally — 50% goes directly to your organization, 50% to TreeRaise. No hidden fees. Our success is entirely tied to yours.
❌
No surprise charges. Ever.
No monthly minimums, no platform maintenance fees, no penalties for low-activity months.
💬
A note from the TreeRaise team
"We designed revenue share because we believe fundraising platforms should be partners, not vendors. When you run a $50,000 campaign, we earn $25,000 and you keep $25,000. We think that's fair — and you should always know exactly what you're paying and why."
