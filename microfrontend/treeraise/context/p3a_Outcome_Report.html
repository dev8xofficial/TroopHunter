Campaign Pipeline CRM & Lead Tracking System
🏗️ Platform
Current problem: No named CRM exists — leads from "Begin Your Impact" clicks enter a black box. Coaches manually track each prospect, there's no pipeline visibility, and dropped leads are only noticed when it's too late.
What gets built: A custom CRM portal (or configured HubSpot integration) with pipeline stages — Prospect → Inquiry → Coach Contacted → Setup Started → Campaign Live → Campaign Closed → Renewal Due — with automated task creation, lead source tagging, and real-time conversion dashboards for leadership.

Automated Email Nurture & Annual Renewal Engine
⚡ Automation
Current problem: No automated email sequences exist. New sign-ups receive no drip nurture. Past campaign organizers receive no renewal prompt — the "Legacy Forest" repeat-campaign strategy is entirely passive, depending on organizers choosing to return on their own.
What gets built: Three automated email sequences — a 5-email new lead nurture (10 days), a 4-email post-sign-up onboarding flow (7 days), and a 3-email annual renewal sequence triggered 11 months after each campaign close — all connected to the CRM and requiring zero manual intervention per campaign.

Automated Impact Report Generator
⚡ Automation
Current problem: It's unclear whether the Verified Impact Report delivered at campaign close is auto-generated or manually assembled. Victor Junco is the sole owner of monthly veritree data reconciliation — GPS photos, CO2 data, tree counts. One person, no backup, and the process doesn't scale past current volume.
What gets built: A direct data pipeline from veritree into a branded report template that auto-populates the moment a campaign closes — trees planted, CO2 offset, GPS locations, project photos — and delivers the report to the organizer's email instantly, with zero manual steps required from the team.

BDR Outbound Prospect Portal & Partner Management System

There's no system for a BDR to build prospect lists of school principals, nonprofit development directors, or church pastors — and no portal for managing B2B partnership conversations with district admins or denomination networks.

Campaign Milestone Alert System & Internal Admin Dashboard
📊 Reporting
Current problem: Staff manually check active campaign dashboards daily to confirm data is updating correctly. The mid-drive check-in is also a manual coach task. There are no automated alerts when an organizer hits a fundraising milestone — meaning 20–30% of total contributions that arrive in the "Final Push" window are not being maximised by proactive organizer nudges.
What gets built: An internal Admin Console (building on the p4d_admin.html interface in your files) with automated campaign health monitoring — plus an organizer-facing milestone alert system that sends automated email/SMS at 25%, 50%, and 75% of goal, prompting them to share the campaign link at exactly the right moment to boost Final Push contributions.
