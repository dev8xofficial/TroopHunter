Section 2

What TreeRaise Is Built to Do

Desired Outcome
Generate significant unrestricted revenue for mission-driven organizations
TreeRaise replaces product-sales fundraising with a zero-friction digital model — 50% of every contribution goes directly to schools, nonprofits, and faith communities, with no inventory, no hidden fees, and no logistics burden.
Current Reality
The operational model doesn't match the ambition yet
TreeRaise launched in February 2026 with a team of one and is hiring its first dedicated client-support role. Every organization that joins today requires manual setup, personal coaching, and hand-crafted marketing content — a model that hits its ceiling well before 500 clients.
Why this matters now: TreeRaise's 2026 goals — 500+ organizations, 125,000+ verified trees, and 2.5M+ lbs of CO₂ sequestered — are already on the calendar. The window to build scalable infrastructure before the organization pipeline outgrows the team is Q2–Q3 2026. Each month of delay narrows the margin.
