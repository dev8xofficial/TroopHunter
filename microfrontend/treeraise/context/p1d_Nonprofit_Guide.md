### Welcome to TreeRaise
#### A modern fundraising platform for mission-driven organizations.

Thank you for choosing TreeRaise. This guide was created to help nonprofits raise funds in a way that builds donor trust and aligns with their mission. Instead of selling products or relying on pressure-based tactics, TreeRaise allows your supporters to contribute digitally while funding verified environmental restoration.

Our goal is to help you launch a fundraiser that is ethical, transparent, and easy to manage. Inside this guide, you will find:
- A quick-start roadmap for launching your campaign
- A clear explanation of how TreeRaise works
- Impact options you can share with confidence
- Simple planning checklists for nonprofit teams
- Ready-to-use communication templates
- Guidance for keeping supporters informed and engaged

### Quick Start Roadmap
#### Launch your TreeRaise drive in four simple steps.

1. **Step 1: Create Your Campaign**
   Set your fundraising goal and campaign details.
2. **Step 2: Share With Supporters**
   Distribute your digital campaign link via email, social media, or newsletters.
3. **Step 3: Engage Your Community**
   Share progress updates and impact milestones.
4. **Step 4: Close and Report**
   Wrap up your campaign and share verified results with contributors and stakeholders.

### How TreeRaise Works
#### A transparent model nonprofits can trust.

- **Mission Support**
  Fifty percent of every contribution directly supports your nonprofit’s programs and operations.
- **Verified Environmental Impact**
  The remaining funds support tree planting through independently verified reforestation projects.
- **Real-Time Transparency**
  Your dashboard updates with funds raised and impact data as it becomes available.
- **Simple Participation**
  Supporters give digitally. No products, no logistics, no follow-up burden.

### Choose Your Impact
#### Your community can support your fundraiser in the way that matters most to them.

Supporters can also give directly toward your fundraising goal. This model allows your group to raise meaningful funds while contributing to global restoration.

#### Keep 50% of Every Contribution to Support Your Organization’s Mission.

**Impact Highlights:**

- Real-time, transparent reporting

### Launch Checklist
#### Key Milestones

Use this checklist to guide your team through a simple and successful TreeRaise drive.

- **Phase 1: Set Up Your Drive**
  Create your TreeRaise account, add your organization name.
- **Phase 2: Personalize Your Page**
  Upload your logo or banner and set your fundraising goal to make the drive feel connected to your community.
- **Phase 3: Share With Members**
  Send the launch email, post your unique link, and display posters in high-traffic areas.
- **Phase 4: Keep Contributors Engaged**
  Post updates, highlight early contributions, and remind supporters that every dollar makes a real impact.
- **Phase 5: Track Your Progress**
  Monitor your dashboard as contributions come in and watch your impact grow in real time.
- **Phase 6: Celebrate Your Impact**
  Announce your totals, thank supporters, and share your verified impact report with your community.

#### Quick Start: Full Walkthrough

1. **Set Up Your TreeRaise Drive**
   Log into your account, add your organization name, set your goal, and choose your impact option.
   > TIP: Keep your goal simple and realistic.
2. **Prepare Your Materials**
   Download posters, review templates, and decide on your communication schedule.
   > TIP: One or two people can run a successful drive.
3. **Launch Your Drive**
   Send the Launch Email, share your link across channels, and display posters.
   > TIP: Launch day messaging should be warm, simple, and mission-focused.
4. **Keep Families Engaged**
   Share mid-week updates and celebrate early progress.
   > TIP: Small updates help supporters feel connected.
5. **Final Push**
   Send the Final Push Email 24–48 hours before the drive ends.
   > TIP: A friendly reminder often brings in 20 to 30 percent of total contributions.
6. **Celebrate & Share Impact**
   Check final totals, thank supporters, and share your verified impact report.
   > TIP: Celebrating impact builds trust and inspires future participation.
