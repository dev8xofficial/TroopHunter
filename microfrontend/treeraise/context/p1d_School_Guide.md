## Welcome to TreeRaise

This kit was created to make your fundraiser simple, transparent, and meaningful from the very first step. Instead of selling products families do not need, your community raises money while helping restore forests and ecosystems around the world.

Inside this guide, you will find everything required to run a successful drive:

- A clear Quick Start Roadmap
- A step-by-step walkthrough of how the program works
- Impact options you can present to your group
- Simple checklists for teams and volunteers
- Posters, email templates, and social content
- Tips for keeping families engaged and excited

Our goal is to help you launch quickly and confidently, while creating a fundraiser your organization can be proud of.

---

## Quick Start Roadmap

Launch your TreeRaise drive in four simple steps.

- **Step 1: Create Your Account** — Name your drive, add your school or group info, and set your fundraising goal.
- **Step 2: Share Your Drive** — Use the prebuilt posters, social graphics, and email templates to invite families.
- **Step 3: Engage Your Community** — Post updates and reminders. Highlight progress and invite families to join in.
- **Step 4: Celebrate Together** — Wrap up your drive, announce your totals, and share your verified impact story.

---

## How TreeRaise Works

- **Step 1: Choose Your Impact** — Supporters can choose where to focus your reforestation and give toward your school's fundraising goal.
- **Step 2: Transparent Tracking** — Your dashboard updates in real time with dollars raised, trees funded, and verified impact.
- **Step 3: Verified Tree Planting** — Tree reforestation projects are carried out by trusted partners and validated through veritree.
- **Step 4: Impact Your Community Can See** — You receive real impact reporting that can be shared with students, families, and staff.

## Choose Your Impact

Your community can support your fundraiser in the way that matters most to them.

Keep 50% of every contribution to support your school's mission. Supporters can also give directly toward your fundraising goal. This model allows your group to raise meaningful funds while contributing to global restoration.

**Impact Highlights**
- Fifty percent of every contribution supports your organization's programs
- No selling, no inventory, no pressure on families
- Simple participation for parents and supporters
- Real-time, transparent reporting

## Brand Guidelines

TreeRaise uses a five-color palette and two font families to create a warm, approachable visual identity. These brand elements help ensure everything you create feels consistent, friendly, and unmistakably TreeRaise.

### Colors

TreeRaise's palette is inspired by nature and supports a calm, trustworthy, eco-friendly tone across all materials.

**Primary Greens**
- Forest Green: #18492E
- Canopy Green: #3C6F46
- Leaf Green: #7BAF73

**Accent Colors**
- Sunrise Gold: #E4B659
- Soft Earth Neutral: #F4EFE5

Use these colors for backgrounds, accents, headlines, and illustration elements to maintain a unified visual identity.

### Fonts

TreeRaise pairs a warm editorial serif with a clean sans serif to balance personality and readability.

- **Heading Font:** Recoleta (Editorial Serif style or similar)
- **Body Font:** Roboto (Regular, Medium or similar)

### Icons

Use simple thin-line eco icons with rounded corners for a modern and minimal look. Keep icons lightweight and consistent in line width to match the TreeRaise illustration style.

## Quick Start: Full Walkthrough

### 1. Set Up Your TreeRaise Drive
- Log into your TreeRaise account.
- Add your school or organization name.
- Set your fundraising goal or tree-planting target.
- Choose your impact location.

> TIP: Keep your goal simple and realistic. Clear goals increase participation.

### 2. Prepare Your Materials
- Download your posters from the appendix.
- Review the email and social templates included in this kit.
- Decide your communication schedule (Launch, Mid-Drive, Final Push).
- Identify who will help share the drive (teachers, coaches, PTA, volunteers).

> TIP: You do not need a large team. One or two people can run a successful drive.

### 3. Launch Your Drive
- Send the Launch Email to families and supporters.
- Share your TreeRaise link across your school or group channels.
- Post your first set of social media updates.
- Display printed posters in hallways, offices, and event spaces.

> TIP: Launch day messaging sets the tone. Keep it warm, simple, and mission-focused.

### 4. Keep Families Engaged
- Share one update mid-week to maintain momentum.
- Celebrate early progress: dollars raised, trees funded, supporters participating.
- Remind families that sharing the link helps multiply impact.
- Use the Mid-Drive templates included in this kit for quick posts and emails.

> TIP: Small updates create excitement and help supporters feel connected.

### 5. Final Push
- Send the Final Push Email 24–48 hours before your drive ends.
- Post a countdown reminder on social media.
- Highlight your progress to inspire last-minute participation.
- Keep messaging positive and gratitude-driven.

> TIP: A friendly reminder often brings in 20 to 30 percent of total contributions.

### 6. Celebrate & Share Impact
- Check your TreeRaise dashboard for final totals.
- Share your results with your community.
- Post a Thank You message using the templates in the kit.
- Download or share your impact summary.

> TIP: Celebrating impact builds trust and inspires future participation.

## TreeRaiser Printable Checklist

### Before You Launch
- Set up your TreeRaise account.
- Add your school or organization name.
- Personalize your dashboard.
- Confirm your posters and social templates are ready.
- Set up competition between classes, grades, or organization departments.

### Launch Week
- Send your Launch Email to families and supporters.
- Post your TreeRaise link on your school or group website.
- Share your drive on social media.
- Display printed posters in high-traffic areas.
- Remind teachers and group leaders to spread the word.
- Highlight why your community chose TreeRaise.

### Mid-Drive
- Share a mid-week reminder email or message.
- Post a social update celebrating early progress.
- Encourage families to share the link with relatives and friends.
- Announce a small milestone and competition updates.
- Thank your supporters publicly.

### Final Push
- Send your Final Push Email.
- Share a countdown ("2 days left").
- Highlight your impact so far to build excitement.
- Encourage one last round of sharing and participation.
- Keep messaging positive, simple, and gratitude-driven.

### Drive Wrap-Up
- Share your final totals and celebrate success with your community.
- Thank all supporters, families, teachers, and organizers.
- Review your impact report inside the TreeRaise dashboard.
- Download or share the verified impact summary.
- Note any learnings or ideas for next time.

### Optional Extras
- Post a "Thank You" graphic on social media.
- Print certificates for students, classrooms, or departments.
- Celebrate your drive at an assembly, meeting, or event.
