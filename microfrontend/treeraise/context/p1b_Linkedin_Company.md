# TreeRaise — LinkedIn Profile

## About

TreeRaise is a modern fundraising platform built for schools, nonprofits, churches, and community organizations that are ready to move beyond outdated, burnout-driven fundraising models. We help organizations raise real funds while creating verified environmental impact. Every TreeRaise campaign delivers two outcomes: communities keep the funds they raise, and forests are restored through transparent, data-backed reforestation partnerships.

Traditional fundraisers rely on door-to-door sales, cash envelopes, and products families do not need. These models create fatigue, inequity, and donor distrust. TreeRaise replaces all of that with a digital-first, values-aligned approach designed for today's donors and families. Our platform makes it easy to launch, share, and manage a fundraiser while giving supporters something they increasingly demand: clear proof of impact. Every campaign is backed by transparent reporting and verified tree planting data so donors can see exactly what their support creates.

TreeRaise exists at the intersection of fundraising, sustainability, and trust. We work with schools, PTAs, nonprofits, churches, civic groups, and mission-driven partners across the United States to modernize fundraising and restore confidence in giving.
