### Business Operations Manual

#### 1. Company Overview
##### 1.1 Business Name
**TreeRaise**, LLC.

##### 1.2 Industry
**Fundraising** and **Environmental Sustainability**.

##### 1.3 Core Products or Services
TreeRaise provides a **digital-first fundraising platform** that allows organizations to raise unrestricted funds while contributing to **verified environmental restoration** (reforestation). Every contribution supports both the organization's mission and the planting of trees globally.

##### 1.4 Target Customers
The business serves **schools (PTAs/PTOs)**, **nonprofits**, **faith-based communities (churches)**, and **civic/youth groups** (e.g., sports teams, clubs).

##### 1.5 Value Proposition
TreeRaise replaces outdated, "heavy" fundraising models (product sales, inventory, cash handling) with a **zero-friction, digital-first approach** that aligns with modern values of stewardship and transparency. It eliminates volunteer burnout and donor fatigue by offering **verified proof of impact** instead of unwanted physical goods.

##### 1.6 Business Model
*   **How the company makes money:** TreeRaise operates on a **50/50 revenue-share model**.
*   **Pricing model:** Contributions are tiered for supporters: **Seed ($15/1 tree)**, **Sapling ($45/3 trees)**, **Grove ($84/6 trees)**, and **Legacy ($156/12 trees)**, though custom amounts are accepted.
*   **Revenue streams:** Income is generated through a percentage of the digital contributions processed through the platform.

#### 2. Customer Segments
##### 2.1 Ideal Customer Profile
The ideal customers are **mission-driven organizations** seeking a sustainable, low-labor way to generate revenue without burdening their volunteer base or supporters.

##### 2.2 Customer Industries
Primarily **Education**, **Philanthropy**, **Religious Organizations**, and **Civic Leadership**.

##### 2.3 Customer Size
Ranges from **local chapters** and small PTAs to **national nonprofits** and large religious denominations.

##### 2.4 Common Customer Problems
*   **Volunteer Burnout:** Parents and staff are exhausted by managing physical inventory and logistics.
*   **Safety Concerns:** Modern schools want to avoid door-to-door sales for students.
*   **Donor Fatigue:** Supporters are tired of being treated like "ATMs" and buying unhealthy or unneeded products.
*   **Lack of Transparency:** Organizations struggle to prove to donors where their money actually goes.

##### 2.5 Typical Customer Goals
*   Generate **unrestricted operational capital** for programs.
*   Engage their community through a **universally positive, eco-friendly mission**.
*   **Modernize fundraising** to fit the digital schedules of busy families.

#### 3. Product / Service Breakdown
##### 3.1 Core Services
*   **Digital Fundraising Platform:** A hosted dashboard where organizations can launch and track campaigns.
*   **Verified Reforestation:** Coordination of tree planting through global partners like **veritree**.

##### 3.2 Optional Services
*   **Educational Curriculum:** K-12 activity packs and lesson plans that turn the fundraiser into a biology or stewardship lesson.
*   **Seasonal Campaigns:** Specialized tools for holidays or service weeks.

##### 3.3 Product Features
*   **Real-Time Impact Dashboard:** Live tracking of funds raised, trees funded, and **GPS-verified planting locations**.
*   **Digital Toolkit:** Pre-built social media templates, email scripts, and printable posters.
*   **Blockchain Verification:** "Proof of planting" for every contribution to ensure transparency.

##### 3.4 Deliverables Clients Receive
*   **Fundraising Revenue:** 50% of all gross contributions.
*   **Verified Impact Report:** A summary of environmental outcomes (CO2 offset, trees planted) to share with stakeholders.
*   **Branded Campaign Page:** A personalized digital destination for their drive.

##### 3.5 What Makes the Service Different
Unlike "buy and sell" models, TreeRaise is **zero-inventory** and **verified**. It shifts the transaction from "extraction" (taking money) to "regeneration" (planting a legacy).

#### 4. Customer Journey
##### 4.1 Lead Generation
TreeRaise attracts leads through **educational content marketing** (blogs on burnout and donor trust), **LinkedIn outreach**, and leveraging the founder's existing reputation in the fundraising industry.

##### 4.2 Lead Qualification
The company focuses on organizations expressing **"fundraising fatigue"** or those looking to align their revenue generation with **environmental values**.

##### 4.3 Sales Process
1.  **Inquiry:** Lead requests a demo or signs up via the website.
2.  **Consultation:** A **TreeRaiser Coach** reaches out to guide the organization.
3.  **Account Setup:** Organization defines their goal and chooses their impact location.

##### 4.4 Onboarding Process
Clients are provided with a **Digital Download Kit** and an **Appendix-based walkthrough** to personalize their dashboard and prepare communication schedules.

##### 4.5 Service Delivery
1.  **Launch:** Organization sends the pre-built "Launch Email" and shares their link.
2.  **Tracking:** Platform automatically processes contributions and updates the dashboard in real time.
3.  **Verification:** Tree planting is executed and verified by third-party partners.

##### 4.6 Client Communication
Communication is structured around **milestone updates**: Launch, Mid-Drive (momentum check), Final Push (urgency), and Wrap-Up (impact celebration).

##### 4.7 Retention & Upselling
TreeRaise encourages organizations to build **"Legacy Forests"** through recurring annual drives, moving from a single transaction to an ongoing environmental stewardship partnership.

#### 10. Delivery System
##### 10.1 Project Kickoff
Begins with the organization setting a goal and uploading their logo to the TreeRaise platform.

##### 10.2 Task Allocation
The organization's leader (PTA President or Director) allocates the task of **sharing the unique link** to their members/families.

##### 10.3 Work Execution
TreeRaise handles the **financial transaction processing** and the **reforestation logistics** through verified third parties.

##### 10.4 Quality Control
Independent verification is provided for every tree planted via **on-site photos and GPS coordinates** uploaded to the dashboard.

##### 10.5 Final Delivery
At the end of the drive, the organization receives **50% of funds** and a finalized **Verified Impact Summary**.

#### 14. Summary of Business Operations
TreeRaise functions as a **highly automated, digital bridge** between mission-driven organizations and global reforestation. The business operates by removing the "sludge" of traditional fundraising—logistics, inventory, and physical labor—and replacing it with a **streamlined digital dashboard**. Organizations sign up, share a link, and collect 50% of contributions while TreeRaise handles the automated reporting and **independently verified tree planting**. This end-to-end system is designed to build **donor trust through transparency**, ensuring that every dollar raised creates a visible, trackable environmental legacy.
