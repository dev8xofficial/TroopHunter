Desired Outcome

What TreeRaise is building — and why operational speed matters.

TreeRaise aims to generate significant unrestricted revenue for mission-driven organizations by replacing product-sales fundraising with a zero-friction digital model that delivers 50% of every contribution directly to the cause — with no inventory, no hidden fees, and no logistics burden.

Buying Reason: The 2026 goal is 500+ organizations and 125,000+ verified trees. TreeRaise just launched (Feb 2026) with a team showing as 1 employee on LinkedIn and is hiring its first Client Specialist to handle onboarding and social-media coaching manually. To deliver meaningful revenue at scale for hundreds of organizations, the current manual model will collapse without automation and operational tooling.

Current Problem Register

These are the operational realities we extracted from TreeRaise's public-facing materials — not generic assumptions.

Current ChallengeBusiness Impact
Manual client onboarding: a TreeRaiser Coach reaches out within 1–2 business days to walk every new organization through setup, social strategy, and digital outreach. Blocks the 500-org 2026 goal. Each new client requires human hours before revenue generation begins.
Broken campaign infrastructure: donation archive returns 404, 4 campaign pages are empty shells, and 2 dashboard UIs have no content. Donor trust and conversion suffer. Orgs cannot self-manage campaigns. Internal team must handle pages manually.
Clients manually create all marketing content using static PDF templates from the School/Nonprofit/Faith-Based starter guides. Campaign engagement depends on client design skill and time. Low engagement = low donations = low revenue.
No self-serve campaign tools: organizations cannot create, customize, or launch campaigns without human-assisted setup. Speed to launch is bottlenecked by human availability. Each day of delay is lost revenue and lost impact.
Extremely small team (1 employee on LinkedIn) attempting to support 500 organizations with one newly hired contract Client Specialist. Organizational scalability and quality control are at risk. The 500-org goal requires a 500x scale-up in operational capacity.
FAQ questions exist on the website but answers are collapsed and not rendered, forcing users to contact support for basic questions. Self-service resolution is impossible. Support ticket volume grows with every new organization.
