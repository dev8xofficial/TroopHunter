# Sickle Cell 101 — LinkedIn Profile

## About

Sickle Cell 101 (SC101) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization that specializes in sickle cell education. We are the largest online platform dedicated solely to sickle cell education. The SC101 team consists of Sickle Cell Educators certified by the Department of Public Health, sickle cell patients, caregivers, advocates, researchers, and an expert sickle cell and thalassemia physician. SC101 also works closely with some of the top medical physicians and researchers within the field of hematology.

SC101's mission is to educate all people affected by sickle cell primarily through the use of social media. Our methods allow us to reach and educate people by the tens of thousands around the world. What makes SC101 unique is our Social Media Education Program. Through the use of patient-friendly graphics and quick and simple facts, we educate the sickle cell community and the general public on sickle cell disease and trait. On social media, our information is liked, commented on, shared, and reposted, and historically our posts have reached and engaged up to 85,000 people. Sickle Cell 101 has yielded the largest following of sickle cell patients and their caregivers on Instagram, with a growing 45k followers on combined platforms. Our information is accessed by people from over 110 countries on six continents.

Do you know your sickle cell status?

Sickle cell trait (SCT) means you carry one sickle cell gene and can pass it to your children. If both parents have SCT, there's a chance their child could have sickle cell disease.

The only way to know for sure is to get tested. From newborn screening to lab tests, options vary depending on where you live — but testing matters. Knowing your status helps you make informed health and family planning decisions. Share this and encourage others to get tested too.

Think you know sickle cell? Let's break it down.

Sickle cell is a genetic blood disorder that causes red blood cells to change shape and block blood flow, leading to pain, complications, and serious health issues.
