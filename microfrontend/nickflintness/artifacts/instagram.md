# Rideit — LinkedIn Profile

## About

Rideit is a mobility solutions company focused on expanding access to transportation for communities of all sizes. Rideit works with both rural and urban communities to deliver reliable and flexible transportation options.

We provide more than just rideshare, offering on demand and scheduled rides, microtransit solutions for local governments, and customized transportation programs for healthcare, workforce, and community access.
