1. **Company Overview:** Mission, vision, products/services, market positioning, and key stakeholders.

6. **Implementation Recommendations:** Prioritize web solutions and outline a high-level roadmap for adoption.
7. **Conclusion & Next Steps:** Summarize key findings and actionable recommendations.

- Use the attached files as the primary source of information.
- Make the report structured, clear, and professional.
- Where information is missing or vague, make logical assumptions based on industry best practices.
- Ensure the report is suitable for decision-makers evaluating web-based transformations.

Do not suggest third-party platforms or off-the-shelf SaaS tools that replicate, compete with, or could substitute the company's proprietary web service offerings. Recommendations should be custom-built, internally developed, or API-integrated enhancements that keep ownership and control within the company's digital ecosystem.**

Output the report in a readable, structured format (e.g., docx).
