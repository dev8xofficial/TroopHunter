# Skillity Ltd — LinkedIn Profile

## About
Skillity builds AI-powered interview and assessment technology designed around real decision-making. The platform conducts structured, adaptive interviews that respond to how people answer, not to fixed scripts. It analyses spoken responses, communication patterns, and behavioural signals to generate clear, role-relevant insight into capability, motivation, and judgement. Unlike traditional hiring tools that rely on CVs or surface indicators, Skillity focuses on observable performance in realistic interview conditions. The result is signal grounded in how people actually think and respond under pressure. Skillity provides the infrastructure to do that, at scale.
