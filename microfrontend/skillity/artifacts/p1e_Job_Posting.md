# Instagram Reel / TikTok Video Editor & Creator — Skillity — Job Posting

## Role Summary

Skillity (skillity.ai) is an AI-powered interview practice platform. They are seeking a video editor to transform raw interview footage — filmed with university students discussing their job search and interview experiences — into 10–20 polished, engaging Instagram Reels and TikToks to promote the platform. The primary outcome is a set of professional-quality, Gen Z-targeted short-form videos ready for social distribution.

## Responsibilities

- Edit raw interview footage into complete, publish-ready Instagram Reels and TikToks.
- Optimise content for high engagement with a Gen Z audience.
- Deliver a paid sample reel within 3 days of project confirmation.
- Complete remaining 9–19 reels within 2 weeks of sample approval.

## Requirements

##### Must Have
- Proven short-form video editing experience (Instagram Reels / TikTok).
- Ability to produce professional-quality output suitable for a branded product promotion.
- Ability to meet tight turnaround deadlines (3-day sample, 2-week full delivery).

##### Nice to Have
- Experience creating content targeting Gen Z audiences.
- Familiarity with SaaS or tech product promotion content.
- Understanding of interview, careers, or university student content niches.

## Client / Company Profile

- Website: https://www.skillity.ai
- Industry: AI / HR Tech / Interview Preparation
- Product: AI-powered adaptive interview practice platform

## Compensation & Terms

- Type: Fixed
- Rate: $5 for paid sample reel; $105 for remaining content upon sample approval ($110 total)
- Deliverables: 10–20 Instagram Reels / TikToks
- Sample deadline: 3 days from confirmation
- Full project deadline: 2 weeks after sample approval
- Project type: One-time (potential ongoing if relationship develops)
