## 7 — About the FactFind
url: https://skillity.ai/about-factfind
type: utility
meta: Learn how Skillity's FactFind personalizes your practice interview experience. Discover tips for an effective introduction and why mastering your career narrative is essential.

### About The FactFind

The FactFind is a 120-second opening moment in every Skillity practice interview that acts as the foundation for how relevant and personalised the AI practice session will be.

#### Tips for an Effective FactFind

1. **Be Specific About Role** — Go beyond generic labels like "Finance". Try "Investment Banking Associate" or "Strategic Analyst". More detail allows the AI to calibrate to the role's seniority and expectations.
2. **Mention Key Experiences** — Reference specific achievements, e.g. "I led a team of 5 on a product launch," to give the AI context to probe deeper.
3. **State Career Goals** — Where you want to be in 2–3 years shapes the Will dimension questions received.
4. **Include Industry Context** — If pivoting industries, explain why. If staying, mention what specifically excites you about continuing.
5. **Use the Full Time** — Many candidates finish in 30 seconds and wonder why questions feel generic. The full 2 minutes makes a difference.
6. **Practice Your Narrative** — Think of it like an elevator pitch covering who you are, what you've done, and where you're going.

#### How It Works

##### General Approach (explorers and generalists)
If you say "I'm interested in consulting" without specifying a firm, the AI tests general competencies: broad industry knowledge, transferable skills, and behavioral probes.

##### Specific Approach (focused candidates)
If you say "Product Manager at Google," the AI researches actual Google interviews and values: company-specific values, role technical scenarios, and cultural fit indicators.

##### Company-Specific Intelligence
When a specific company is named, the AI conducts real-time research to understand: core values and mission, common interview formats, key competencies, recent company developments, industry challenges, and cultural fit indicators.

#### Why This Matters

- Every networking event or LinkedIn message requires succinctly communicating who you are.
- Interviewers can sense when motivations don't align with stated goals.
- Resumes get you in the door; the ability to tell your story gets you the offer.
- Research shows interviewers form initial impressions within the first 2 minutes.

#### Why Skillity's Approach Is Better

Traditional interview prep asks you to passively upload a job description. Skillity's FactFind encourages thinking about the role from the onset. Benefits: flexibility to pivot your narrative for any opportunity, confidence in making a strong professional impression, a realistic "tell me about yourself" simulation, and reflective thinking on what makes you unique.

### The Keyword Illusion: Why Modern ATS is Failing the AI Era

The golden rule of job hunting used to be simple: optimize for the machine. For decades, candidates meticulously wove keywords from job descriptions into their experience just to pass algorithmic gatekeepers.

Today, that system is fundamentally broken. The widespread adoption of generative AI has turned resume tailoring from a painstaking, hours-long task into a ten-second automated prompt. Recruiters are now drowning in thousands of applications that are mathematically perfect matches for the job description.

The Keyword Illusion: in a landscape where AI can turn average bullet points into flawless corporate prose, it is nearly impossible to separate a genuine industry expert from a candidate who simply wrote a great prompt. When every resume is a 99% match, the resume itself ceases to be a signal of quality — it merely proves the candidate knows how to use modern tools.

#### From "Matching" to "Authenticating"

Modern hiring tools are trapped in the matching phase — scanning, parsing, and scoring based on static text. The hiring paradigm has to evolve. If the resume is the marketing brochure, the interview is the live product demonstration.

The traditional hiring funnel has flipped:

- Yesterday's filter: Getting past the ATS.
- Today's filter: Surviving the live interview.

Because getting past the ATS is easier than ever, the real competition has shifted entirely to the conversation. A candidate relying solely on an AI-generated resume will quickly falter when pressed for depth, nuance, and situational reasoning.

#### The Ultimate Truth is Live

Preparation cannot rely on static scripts or rehearsing the STAR method. Candidates must be ready to defend their "perfect" resumes under pressure.

Skillity's Adaptive Flow technology strips away predictable, rehearsed scenarios and asks the fluid, unpredictable follow-up questions that elite hiring managers use to authenticate true capability. It probes Skill, Will, and Attitude to reveal how a candidate actually thinks when the script runs out.

The ATS might get you in the door, but mastering the real interview is how you prove you belong in the room.

## 16 — Signal vs Noise: Why Static Interviews Fail
url: https://skillity.ai/posts/signal-noise
type: article
author: The Skillity Team
meta: The Performance Paradox: Why Modern Interview Prep is Breaking. The hiring landscape is currently locked in a digital arms race that is failing both sides of the table.

### Signal vs Noise: Why Static Interviews Fail

The hiring landscape is locked in a digital arms race failing both sides of the table. As candidates increasingly use generative AI to optimize resumes and rehearse answers, the result is a landscape of identical candidates — polished mediocrity where everyone sounds perfect but no one sounds like themselves. The interview has been turned into a predictable exchange of rehearsed lines that rarely predicts actual success in a role.
