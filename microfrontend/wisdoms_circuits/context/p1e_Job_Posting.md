# Create a Series of 30 Second Animated Product Explainer Videos for EdTech Platform — Wisdom Circuits — Job Posting

## Role Summary
Wisdom Circuits (wisdomcircuits.com) is an EdTech platform seeking a freelancer or small studio to produce a series of short animated product explainer videos. Each video will be approximately 30 seconds and highlight a specific platform feature. The primary outcome is a cohesive, publish-ready video series in a modern SaaS motion design style — featuring screen movement, angled screen perspective, animated UI elements, and smooth transitions.

## Responsibilities
- Produce a series of ~30-second animated explainer videos, each focused on a distinct Wisdom Circuits feature.
- Maintain a consistent design style and visual language across all videos in the series.
- Apply modern SaaS motion design techniques including animated UI elements, angled screen perspectives, and smooth transitions.

## Requirements

##### Must Have
- Portfolio of similar short-form animated or motion design explainer work.
- Ability to propose a production approach suited to a SaaS product series.
- Ability to provide rough cost-per-video and estimated timeline upfront.
