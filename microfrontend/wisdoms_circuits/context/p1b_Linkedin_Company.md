# Wisdom Circuits — LinkedIn Profile

## About
Wisdom Circuits builds AI learning companions designed for students. The platform offers adaptive, curriculum-aligned support that guides thinking rather than replacing it — emphasizing understanding, integrity, and safe learning. Tools include instant flashcard generation, essay guidance, warm-up prompts for teachers, and a gamified practice mode called Circuit Rush. Parents receive clear visibility into their child's progress and learning activity. Named one of Tech National's "17 Companies to Watch in 2026."
