- Early LinkedIn presence (6 followers) — content is being produced but not yet reaching decision-makers at scale
- Product differentiation is real but nuanced — hard to communicate "we guide thinking, not give answers" in 30 seconds to a skeptical buyer

- Dev8X operates in B2B SaaS, which means Abdul understands the challenge of selling to institutional buyers with long decision cycles — the same friction Peter faces selling to districts and universities
- Both founders are navigating the AI product landscape and making deliberate choices about how AI should and should not be used — a genuine shared intellectual position
- Peter's post on NYC's AI policy patchwork ("1,700+ schools, each improvising") is exactly the kind of fragmented market that creates demand for technology partners who understand the institutional layer
- Dev8X's technical capabilities are precisely what a 4-person EdTech company needs as it scales — organic fit for a future partnership or referral conversation

#### Shared Ground

Both Abdul and Peter are CEOs who have made a deliberate bet that AI should augment human capability rather than replace it — and both are operating in a market where most competitors are doing the opposite. That shared conviction is the invisible thread. Every comment should feel like it comes from someone who has already reached the same conclusion Peter has, just from a different angle.
