# A Human Sanctuary — LinkedIn Profile

## About

At A Human Sanctuary, we are tending something more than individual therapy alone — we are cultivating an island of coherence: a living refuge of connection, resilience, and shared humanity within a rapidly changing world. Here, you will find innovative individual therapy, trauma healing intensives, and supportive spaces for both personal and collective healing. Alongside this, we offer community-rooted experiences — through circles, gatherings, and guided pathways of Journeywork — that invite you back into relationship with yourself, others, and the natural world.

We are deeply attuned to the reality that many are feeling overwhelmed by the future, by environmental and societal stressors, and by the profound changes unfolding in our world. You are not alone in this. Our work is grounded in nervous system healing, relational safety, and the quiet rebuilding of inner and outer resilience. Through spaces like The Acorn Circle: Where Wild Hearts Find Deep Roots, we are nurturing small, intentional pockets of connection and skill-building that ripple outward — restoring a sense of belonging, agency, and meaning. This is a place of true refuge. A place to remember the inherent wholeness in all of your humanity.
