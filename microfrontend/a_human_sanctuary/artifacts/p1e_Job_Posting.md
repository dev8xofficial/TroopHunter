# Website Compliance & Optimization Specialist (HIPAA + SEO) – Ongoing Support — A Human Sanctuary — Job Posting

## Role Summary

A Human Sanctuary is a California-based trauma-informed psychotherapy practice (www.ahumansanctuary.com) seeking an experienced web professional to audit, optimize, and provide ongoing support for their existing Wix website. The specialist is responsible for delivering a HIPAA-conscious, ADA-accessible, SEO-optimized site in Phase 1, then serving as a long-term monthly collaborator for content updates, new clinician onboarding, and security oversight in Phase 2.

## Responsibilities

**Phase 1 — Audit & Optimization**

- Conduct a HIPAA-conscious review of forms, integrations, and data flow.
- Audit for California privacy compliance (CCPA/CPRA).
- Perform an ADA accessibility review.
- Execute technical SEO optimization.
- Improve site speed and mobile performance.
- Identify and fix current issues.
- Review Wix app integrations and tracking tools.

**Phase 2 — Ongoing Support**

- Add new clinicians (bios, photos, specialties).
- Add new offerings (courses, programs, intensives).
- Update pages and landing content.
- Implement ongoing SEO improvements.
- Oversee security and site maintenance.

## Requirements

##### Must Have

- Wix platform expertise (not WordPress or other CMS).
- Demonstrated experience with healthcare or therapy websites.
- Working understanding of HIPAA-conscious web practices.
- Strong technical and on-page SEO knowledge.
- ADA accessibility awareness.
- Available for ongoing monthly support.

##### Nice to Have

- Prior Wix work on healthcare sites (2–3 examples required with proposal).
- Documented approach to HIPAA-conscious website structure.
