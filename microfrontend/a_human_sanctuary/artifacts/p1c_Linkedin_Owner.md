# Julie Anne Steiger, MSW, LCSW, SEP — LinkedIn Profile

## Summary

Julie Anne Steiger is the Founder of A Human Sanctuary, a Somatic Psychotherapist, and Clinical Supervisor based in Idyllwild, CA. She has dedicated her career to cultivating the tools and practices required for healing and growth after trauma. She is a Somatic Experiencing Practitioner, certified in Expressive Arts and Ecotherapy, and has over 10 years of experience using EMDR for transformative healing. Julie Anne blends the science of the nervous system, the creativity of the arts, and the wisdom of nature to guide people in reconnecting to their inherent wholeness and resiliency. She is currently creating coursework in Deep Adaptation Therapy and offers groups in Resiliency and Collapse Acceptance. This work supports people as they navigate eco-anxiety, societal and environmental stressors, and parenting in times of uncertainty.

At A Human Sanctuary, we believe that providing therapy and creating a therapeutic environment is nothing short of a work of art. Every human is profoundly unique and deserves to be met with true curiosity, openness, and authentic compassion. Our mission is to be a safe place for people seeking refuge, healing, and personal transformation.

Our offerings include innovative individual therapy, trauma-healing intensives, support groups for the communities we serve, and coursework designed to foster eco-resiliency for future generations. Specializations include working with women and children with complex or developmental trauma, highly sensitive or neurodivergent people, and those who have not found traditional talk therapy to be supportive in their healing. We also work with trauma, anxiety, depression, grief, relational challenges, stress management, eco-anxiety, and existential crisis.

Our approach is collaborative and strengths-based, drawing from EMDR, IFS/Parts Work, Somatic Experiencing, Motivational Interviewing, Depth Psychology, Expressive Arts, and Ecotherapy. In every session, we balance deep emotional processing with concrete, stabilizing practices that support nervous system regulation, increase resilience, and build sense of interpersonal safety.

We see this work as part of a larger vision: a world that honors the worth of all beings, nurtures the kinship between humans, animals, and plants, and recognizes our essential role as guardians of Mother Earth. Julie Anne believes that tending to our soul wounds and remembering our ecological belonging is among the most sacred and powerful work we can do — for ourselves, for one another, and for the planet we call home.
