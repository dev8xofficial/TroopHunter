#### About us

OC Architects & Design is an enthusiastic Architectural practice committed to providing clients with a professional, high quality, Architectural and Design service. We employ a creative and innovative approach to design, for all projects regardless of size. The Practice has a broad range of knowledge and experience in the building and design industry from small domestic refurbishments to large scale commercial developments. We believe that Architecture should respond to its surroundings and the characters of the people that inhabit it. It should always seek to enhance their lives on a practical and emotional level and our approach is underpinned by this belief. The Practice is currently employed in a number of projects throughout Ireland, is fully insured and is a registered practice with the RIAI, with offices in Ranelagh Village, Dublin, and in Castleisland, Co. Kerry.
