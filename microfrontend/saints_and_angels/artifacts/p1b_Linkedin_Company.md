# Saints and Angels Healing Through Sound — LinkedIn Profile

## About

Saints and Angels "Healing through Sound" is a Sound Healing ecosystem that offers live immersive individual and group Sound Baths/Meditations, fully-immersive 3D experiences in virtual reality, healing binaural and solfeggio frequencies, and masterfully produced, specifically designed healing music. The mission is to help people de-stress, rejuvenate, heal, rebalance, transcend, and improve overall well-being through the power of sound. Sessions combine the tones and harmonics of crystal singing bowls, chimes, shruti, ocean drum, solfeggio tones, and other meditative instruments, alongside aromatherapy and lighting.

The Therapeutic Sound Meditation offering is positioned as a safe, effective way to promote wellness in the workplace — helping businesses show employees their well-being is a priority, reduce stress, and improve productivity, creativity, concentration, and overall well-being, while supporting return-to-office efforts. For events, retreats, workshops, or conferences, founder Peter offers private group sound baths to individuals and workplaces; inquiries can be directed to pc@saints-angels.com or 708-228-2477.

**Primary location:** 7206 N. Wolcott Suite 2C, Chicago, IL.
