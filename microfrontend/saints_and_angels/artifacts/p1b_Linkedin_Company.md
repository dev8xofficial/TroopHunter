#### About us

Saints and Angels "Healing through Sound" is a Sound Healing ecosystem that offers live immersive individual, and group Sound Baths/Meditations, Fully-Immersive 3D experiences in Virtual Reality, Healing Binaural and Solfeggio Frequencies, and Masterfully produced and specifically designed healing music. The mission of Saints and Angels is to help people de-stress, rejuvenate, heal, rebalance, transcend, and improve overall well-being through the Power of Sound. Saints and Angels Sound Meditation sessions combine the tones and harmonics of the crystal singing bowls, chimes, shruti, ocean drum, solfeggio tones, and other meditative instruments.
