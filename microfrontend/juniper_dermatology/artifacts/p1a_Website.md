- March 1, 2024

Are you ready to shake off the winter blues and embrace the fresh vibes of spring? Well, buckle up, buttercup, because we’re about to spill all the tea on how to get that radiant springtime glow you’ve been dreaming of! In this blog, we’re dishing out some springtime skincare tips to help you slay the season and unleash your inner glow-up!

#### Spring Skincare Transition:

Alright, babes, let’s talk about the elephant in the room—transitioning from winter to spring can wreak havoc on your skin! As the temperatures rise and the air gets a little less dry, your skin might need a little extra TLC. Say buh-bye to heavy moisturizers and hello to lightweight, hydrating formulas that won’t weigh you down.

Here are some important things to know about this transition from snow queen to spring goddess:

- Hydration, hydration, hydration! We’re talking about moisturizing like your life depends on it. Swap out those heavy creams for lightweight, hydrating formulas that won’t leave you feeling like an oil slick.
- Sunscreen is your BFF, hun! With the sun shining brighter than your highlighter, you gotta protect that precious skin of yours. Slather on that SPF like it’s nobody’s business and keep those UV rays at bay.
- Exfoliation is key, but let’s keep it gentle, shall we? Say goodbye to those dead skin cells with some sweet, sweet chemical exfoliants. Just remember, less is more, babe. We don’t need to scrub our faces off.
- Time to switch up that cleansing routine, darling! Say hello to a gentle cleanser that’ll wash away the day without stripping your skin of its natural oils. Foamy or gel, it’s your call, just make sure it leaves you feeling fresh as a daisy.
- Got specific skin concerns? Ain’t no thang, boo! Whether it’s allergies acting up or the sudden oil slick on your face, we’ve got products for that. Treat yourself to some targeted treatments and watch those worries disappear.
- Keep yourself hydrated, inside and out! Guzzle that agua like you’re a desert flower in need of a drink…because in this high desert climate, you are! And don’t forget those water-rich fruits and veggies—they’re like a spa day for your skin.
- Oh, and lips? They need love too, hun! Don’t leave ’em out in the cold. Swipe on some hydrating lip balm with SPF and pucker up for spring

#### Fresh Ingredients for Spring:

Time to get fresh, honey! Spring is all about embracing nature’s bounty, so why not incorporate some fresh ingredients into your skincare routine? Look for products packed with antioxidants like vitamin C to brighten and protect your skin from environmental stressors. Here’s a list of some fun, natural ingredients to implement into your springtime skincare routine:

- Watermelon Extract: This juicy little fruit isn’t just for summer picnics! Watermelon extract is packed with antioxidants and vitamins that hydrate and brighten the skin, leaving you with a dewy, radiant glow. It’s like a tall glass of water for your face!
- Pineapple Enzymes: Time to get tropical, babe! Pineapple enzymes are like a vacation for your skin, gently exfoliating away dead skin cells and revealing smoother, more radiant skin underneath. Say goodbye to dullness and hello to that vacation glow!
- Hyaluronic Acid: Let’s plump it up, shall we? Hyaluronic acid is the holy grail of hydration, attracting moisture like a magnet and locking it into your skin for a smooth, supple complexion. It’s like a big, cozy hug for your face!
- Cucumber Extract: Cool as a cucumber, baby! Cucumber extract is like a spa day in a bottle, soothing and calming irritated skin while reducing puffiness and inflammation. It’s the perfect antidote to those springtime allergies and late nights.
- Green Tea: Sip it or slather it, honey! Green tea is chock-full of antioxidants and anti-inflammatory properties that help protect your skin from environmental damage and soothe redness and irritation. It’s like a detox for your skin!
- Vitamin C: Brighten up, buttercup! Vitamin C is the secret weapon against dull, lackluster skin, helping to fade dark spots, even out skin tone, and boost collagen production for a firmer, more youthful complexion. It’s like sunshine in a bottle!

#### Sun Protection Tips for Outdoor Activities:

Listen up, sunshine! As you start spending more time outdoors soaking up those rays, it’s essential to protect your precious skin from the sun’s harmful UV rays. Slather on that SPF like it’s nobody’s business and opt for products that offer broad-spectrum protection.

And don’t forget to reapply throughout the day, hun—ain’t nobody got time for sunburn! If sunscreen isn’t your thing, or you don’t have time to apply it before you leave the house, make sure to grab a cute sun hat for some added protection. Also, shade…when those UV rays get intense, grab as much shade as you can. Your skin will thank you later!

#### Spring Cleaning for Your Skincare Shelf:

Out with the old, in with the new, darling! It’s time to Marie Kondo your skincare shelf and declutter like a boss. Toss out anything past its prime and make room for some springtime essentials. Think lightweight moisturizers, refreshing toners, and maybe even a floral-scented face mist to keep you feeling fresh and fabulous! If you have any of the following in your skin care regime, toss ’em and leave ’em:

- Harsh Physical Scrubs: Say goodbye to those gritty scrubs that feel like sandpaper on your face, honey! Harsh physical scrubs can cause micro-tears in the skin and lead to irritation and sensitivity. Opt for gentler exfoliants like chemical exfoliants or soft cleansing brushes instead.
- Artificial Fragrances: Adios, synthetic scents! Artificial fragrances might smell nice, but they can wreak havoc on sensitive skin and cause irritation and allergic reactions. Look for fragrance-free or naturally scented products to keep your skin happy and healthy.
- Alcohol-Based Toners: Time to dry up those alcohol-based toners, darling! While they might make your skin feel squeaky clean, they can strip away your skin’s natural oils and leave it feeling tight and dry. Opt for alcohol-free toners that hydrate and balance your skin instead.
- Mineral Oil: Sayonara, mineral oil! While it might seem innocent enough, mineral oil can clog pores and lead to breakouts, especially if you have oily or acne-prone skin. Look for non-comedogenic oils like jojoba or argan oil to keep your skin moisturized without the risk of breakouts.
- Parabens: Buh-bye, parabens! These controversial preservatives have been linked to hormone disruption and other health concerns, so it’s best to steer clear of them. Look for paraben-free products to keep your skincare routine clean and safe.
- Sulfates: So long, sulfates! These harsh cleansing agents can strip away your skin’s natural oils and leave it feeling dry and irritated. Opt for sulfate-free cleansers that gently cleanse your skin without stripping away its natural moisture barrier.
- Formaldehyde-Releasing Preservatives: Adieu, formaldehyde-releasing preservatives! These sneaky ingredients might help keep your skincare products shelf-stable, but they can also cause irritation and allergic reactions. Look for products that are free of these preservatives to keep your skin happy and healthy.

From rejuvenating facials to skin-resurfacing treatments, there’s no shortage of options to help you achieve that radiant springtime glow. So, go ahead, darling—indulge yourself! Give us a call to set up an appointment to get your glow up!

Combat those pesky allergies with soothing skincare products designed to calm and hydrate your skin. Look for gentle, fragrance-free formulas that won’t exacerbate your symptoms and keep that skin feeling happy and healthy.

Time to switch up your makeup game, babe! Say goodbye to those dark, moody hues of winter and hello to fresh, spring-inspired looks that scream sunshine and rainbows. Think soft pastels, dewy finishes, and maybe even a pop of color to make those peepers pop! Get creative, experiment with different shades, and let your inner artist shine!

And there you have it, gorgeous—your guide to embracing springtime radiance like a boss! Whether you’re revamping your skincare routine, indulging in some luxurious treatments, or slaying the makeup game, remember to embrace the beauty of the season and let your inner glow shine bright!

### Sneezin’ Season: How Allergies are Messing with Your Glow!

- April 1, 2024

Hey there, gorgeous! So, picture this: you’re all set to rock that flawless complexion, but suddenly, now that the weather is starting to warm up, your skin decides to throw a tantrum. What gives? Well, hold onto your hats, folks, ’cause we’re diving headfirst into the world of seasonal allergies and how they’re messing with your glow!

At Juniper Dermatology, we’re all about keeping your skin looking fierce and fabulous, but sometimes, Mother Nature has other plans. Yep, we’re talking about those pesky seasonal allergies that turn your face into a battlefield. But fear not, dear reader, because we’re here to spill the tea on how these sneezy shenanigans can wreak havoc on your complexion.

#### Pollen: The Sneaky Springtime Saboteur

First off, let’s talk about the culprit behind all this chaos: pollen. Ah, yes, that seemingly innocent substance floating through the air, not so innocently wreaking havoc on your skin. You see, when pollen decides to make its grand entrance, your immune system goes into full-on panic mode. Cue the sneezing, itching, and watery eyes – and oh yeah, say hello to irritated, inflamed skin.

But wait, there’s more! Not only does pollen trigger a full-blown skin freakout, but it also has a knack for clogging up your pores like nobody’s business. And we all know what happens when those pores get all backed up – hello, breakouts! So if you’ve been noticing a sudden surge in zits popping up like unwanted guests at a party, you can thank pollen for crashing the party.

#### Histamines: The Mischievous Masterminds of Skin Rebellion

Now, let’s not forget about everyone’s favorite sidekick to seasonal allergies: histamines. These little troublemakers are responsible for causing all sorts of mayhem, from itching and redness to swelling and irritation. So if you’ve been feeling like your skin is staging a rebellion, histamines might just be the ringleader. Symptoms of a histamine flare-up include, but are not limited to:

- Itching: If your skin’s throwing a major itchfest, and it’s got you scratching like there’s no tomorrow, you can probably thank histamines.
- Redness: Oh, look, it’s the “I’m-blushing-but-not-in-a-cute-way” look brought to you by histamines. Get ready for some serious redness action, because these troublemakers love to paint the town red on your skin!
- Swelling: Say hello to the puffy princess look, courtesy of our dear friend histamine. Because what’s better than a little skin swelling to keep things interesting, right?
- Irritation: Feeling a bit on edge? Blame it on histamines, the ultimate skin irritators. Get ready for some tingling, burning, and general “get-me-out-of-here” vibes.
- Hives: Ah, yes, the classic histamine-induced “I-woke-up-with-this” look. Nothing screams fun like raised, itchy welts popping up out of nowhere.
- Rashes: Get ready to play “spot the rash” with our good pal histamine. Whether it’s eczema, dermatitis, or just a good old-fashioned allergic reaction, histamines love to keep things interesting with a little rash action.
- Watery Eyes: Cue the waterworks, because nothing says “I’m allergic to everything” like a constant stream of tears flowing down your face. You guessed it…thanks, histamines, for the “emotional” rollercoaster!

#### Conquer Allergies and Reclaim Your Glow

But fear not, my fellow allergy warriors, because at Juniper Dermatology, we’ve got your back – and your face! Here are a few tips to help you reclaim your glow and show those allergies who’s boss:

- Cleanse Like it’s Your Day Job: Say goodbye to pollen and pore-clogging nasties by washing your face with a gentle cleanser morning and night. Trust us, your skin will thank you.
- Moisturize, Moisturize, Moisturize: Hydration is key, honey! Keep your skin happy and hydrated with a lightweight, non-comedogenic moisturizer to soothe that allergy-induced irritation.
- Show Pollen Who’s Boss: Minimize your exposure to pollen by keeping windows closed, showering after spending time outdoors, and rocking those fabulous oversized sunglasses like the allergy-fighting superhero you are.
- Chill Out with Cool Compresses: Soothe irritated skin and reduce inflammation by applying a cool compress to your face whenever those pesky allergies start acting up. Ah, instant relief!
- Seek Professional Help: When all else fails, don’t hesitate to reach out to our skincare experts at Juniper Dermatology. Dermatology treatments come armed with powerful anti-inflammatory ingredients like corticosteroids and hydrocortisone to kick allergy-induced swelling and redness to the curb. We’ve got all the tricks up our sleeves to help you tackle those allergy-induced complexion woes like a pro.

### Glowing, Not Frying: Sun Protection Tips for Happy Skin!

- June 3, 2024

Hey there, Central Oregon sun-seekers! Are you ready to dive into the wonderful world of sun safety? As the sun cranks up the heat and our days get longer, it’s time to get real about how those UV rays can wreak havoc on our precious skin. Buckle up, because we’re about to break down the science of UV radiation and dish out some helpful tips for keeping your skin happy and healthy under the sun!

#### Understanding UV Radiation

Alright, let’s talk UV – the good, the bad, and the skin-damaging. UV radiation comes in three flavors: UVA, UVB, and UVC. While UVA rays like to dive deep into your skin, causing premature aging and stirring up trouble with skin cancer, UVB rays are more of the “ouch, I got a sunburn” type. Both can tag-team your skin cells, leaving you with a not-so-glamorous sun-kissed glow.

- UVA rays are the sneaky ninjas of the UV world. They penetrate deep into your dermis, wreaking havoc by causing premature aging and cozying up with skin cancer cells like they’re BFFs at a slumber party.
- UVB rays, on the other hand, are the more immediate troublemakers. They’re like that friend who always forgets sunscreen and ends up looking like a lobster after a day at the beach. Together, UVA and UVB rays form the ultimate tag team, assaulting your skin cells and leaving behind a not-so-flattering sun-kissed souvenir.
- But wait, there’s more! Enter UVC rays, the elusive troublemakers that rarely make it through Earth’s atmosphere to crash our skin party. They’re like the distant cousin who sends occasional shady texts but never really shows up. So, while UVA and UVB rays are busy causing chaos, UVC rays are probably chilling somewhere, plotting their next move.

Bottom line? Wear your sunscreen, babes. UV rays may come in different flavors, but they all have one goal in mind: to mess with your skin’s vibe.

#### Skin Reactions to UV Exposure:

Ever had a day lounging pool, beach, or lake side, soaking up the glorious rays, when suddenly you’re as red as a lobster? That’s the immediate reaction to UV exposure, but the real “fun” effects start later. Oh, honey, your skin’s got quite the repertoire of reactions to those UV rays. Let me break it down for you:

- Sunburn Spectacular: UV rays hit your skin like a slap in the face from the sun itself, leaving you with a fiery red complexion that screams, “I should’ve listened to my mom about sunscreen!”
- Tan Tango: Your skin decides to do a little dance with melanin, trying to play it cool with a tan, but sometimes ends up looking more like a patchy Picasso painting than a bronzed beach babe.
- Wrinkle Rumble: UVA rays sneak into your skin’s VIP section, cozying up to collagen and elastin like they own the place, resulting in fine lines and wrinkles that make you wonder if time really is on your side.
- Age Spot Showdown: UV rays play a little game of connect-the-dots on your skin, leaving behind pesky age spots that are like unwelcome guests crashing the party of your flawless complexion.
- Cancer Conundrum: When UV rays decide to get really serious, they can kickstart some shady business with your skin cells, leading to the dreaded C-word: cancer. It’s the ultimate betrayal from those sunny rays that you once thought were your friends.

#### The Importance of Sun Protection:

Listen up, sunshine aficionados – sun protection is a MUST. But don’t stop there! Seek out shade like it’s your own personal oasis, and rock those UV-blocking threads like a fashionista on a mission. Your skin will thank you later, trust us.

Let’s chat about a few reasons why sun protection is as essential as a killer pair of shades:

- Wrinkle Warfare: Sunscreen isn’t just for the beach babes; it’s your secret weapon in the battle against wrinkles. Wrinkles may be inevitable, but why not keep them guessing with SPF on your side?
- Glow-Getter: Sunscreen isn’t just about avoiding sunburn; it’s about preserving that radiant glow that makes you look like you woke up flawless. Who needs filters when you’ve got SPF?
- Skin Savior: Sunscreen is like a knight in shining armor for your skin, protecting it from the evil clutches of UV damage. Don’t let those rays rain on your skincare parade!
- Time Machine: Want to keep that youthful glow intact? Slather on some sunscreen and take a trip back in time. Who says you can’t defy aging with a little SPF magic?
- Confidence Booster: Rocking sunscreen isn’t just about protecting your skin; it’s about strutting your stuff with confidence, knowing you’re taking care of business, and looking fabulous while doing it.

#### Sun Protection Beyond Sunscreen:

We know skin protection doesn’t just come in a bottle, though we’re big fans. Sunscreen may be the star of the show, but there’s a whole entourage of sun protection tactics ready to back it up.

- Shady Business: Don’t underestimate the power of a fabulous hat and oversized sunglasses. They’re not just accessories; they’re your VIP pass to shady glamour. Shielding your face from the sun has never looked so chic.
- Cover-Up Couture: Who says you can’t rock a stylish cover-up while lounging by the pool? Throw on a breezy kaftan or a flowy beach shirt and show those UV rays who’s boss. Sun protection never goes out of style, darling.
- Seek Shelter: When the sun’s rays are at their peak, seek refuge in the shade like it’s your own personal sanctuary. Whether it’s under a parasol or nestled between some palm trees, find your shade haven and bask in the cool comfort of sun-free bliss.
- Timing is Everything: Channel your inner sun goddess and plan your outdoor escapades wisely. Avoid peak sun hours and opt for early morning or late afternoon outings instead.
- Hydration Station: Keep your skin hydrated from the inside out by guzzling down H2O like it’s your signature cocktail. Hydrated skin is happy skin, and happy skin is better equipped to fend off UV invaders.

#### Choosing the Right Sunscreen:

Not all sunscreens are created equal. You’ve got SPF, water resistance, and even different formulations to consider. Whether you’re team cream, lotion, or spray, make sure your sunscreen game is strong and that you’re reapplying regularly – especially when you’re out there living your best outdoor life.

Next time you stop by Juniper Dermatology, make sure to ask us for our sunscreen recommendations! We’re here to help set you up for sun damage prevention success!

Remember, the sun’s rays may be hot, but you’re hotter – especially when you’re rocking that sun-safe swagger. So, slather on that sunscreen, seek out some shade, and show those UV rays who’s boss. Here’s to happy, healthy skin under the sun – you’ve got this, sunshine!

### Skin Cancer Screening: What You Should Know

- June 23, 2023

Skin cancer is a serious skin condition that affects millions of people; however, it can be effectively treated if it’s detected early on. This is why scheduling regular skin cancer screenings with Juniper Dermatology is so important. We carefully screen our patients for malignant and pre-malignant lesions and moles.

If you haven’t had a checkup in a while, we recommend scheduling an appointment with Dr. Karla Pivik as soon as possible. As you prepare for your appointment, consider the following risk factors and signs to look out for.

##### Risk Factors of Skin Cancer

Skin cancer can affect all age groups. In fact, melanoma is ranked as the number one cancer detected in young adults aged 25 to 29. Not only that, but it is by far the deadliest form of skin cancer, causing more than 75 percent of all skin cancer deaths. This is why we are so passionate about knowing the risk factors and considering consistent skin cancer screenings to catch it as soon possible. No one is exempt of being exposed to possible factors of skin cancer, which is why we’ve listed a few of them below:

- Extensive sun exposure: UV rays cause damage to the DNA within our skin cells and can even result in changes. Extensive sun exposure can be caused by sun-bathing, tanning beds, etc.

- Family history: Genetic factors can contribute to an increased risk of being diagnosed with skin cancer.

- Previous history of skin cancer: Conditions could include basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma or melanoma skin cancer.

##### Skin Cancer Signs to Look Out For

###### Suspicious Moles or Lesions

Most moles are harmless, but if you notice any new moles or skin lesions that appear abnormal or undergo changes, get it checked out immediately. The ABCDE rule can help you identify potential signs of melanoma:

- Asymmetry: One half of a mole or lesion doesn’t match the other half.
- Uneven Border: The edges are uneven and the spot has an irregular outline.
- Color variation: The mole or lesion varies in different colors.
- Diameter: The size of the mole or lesion is larger than 6 millimeters.
- Evolving: The mole or lesion is changing in size, thickness, or texture.

###### New Growths

Be aware of any new growths or lesions that appear on your skin, especially if they differ from your normal skin features. These can include reddish patches, scaly areas, or rough, elevated bumps. At Juniper Dermatology, we are extremely passionate about skin cancer treatment and take it very seriously, so even if you are unsure about this new growth, we highly recommend scheduling an appointment to have us look at it.

###### Itchiness, Pain, or Tenderness

Unexplained itching, pain, or tenderness in a mole or lesion should never be ignored. Take note of any abnormal changes.

###### Changes in Skin Appearance or Texture

If there are any areas of your skin that become rough or scaly patches, red or inflamed, or if it becomes thinner or more translucent, give us a call. These types of changes should be evaluated by a dermatologist to determine the underlying cause, which could potentially be skin cancer.

At Juniper Dermatology, we recommend year-round, daily use of sun protection. This doesn’t just apply to summer, but also during winter! There are many different precautions you can take when it comes to sun protection. Sunscreen, sun-protective clothing, and seeking shade while outside are all key elements to maintaining the health of your skin while also enjoying the outdoors, especially here in Oregon.

###### When choosing a sunscreen look for one with the following characteristics…

1. Broad-spectrum protection (protects against UVA and UVB rays)

2. Sun Protection Factor (SPF) 30 or higher

3. Water resistance

We often get asked what the best sunscreen to use is? Our answer is simple: the one that you like and will use. There are two categories of sunscreens to consider, chemical and physical.

Chemical sunscreens contain organic (carbon-based) compounds, such as avobenzone, oxybenzone, octinoxate, and octisalate. These sunscreens create chemical reactions when they are exposed to UV light. They work by absorbing and changing harmful UV rays into heat, and then release the heat from the skin. When searching for a chemical sunscreen, check to make sure that it contains active ingredients that block out both UVB and UVA rays. This is also referred to as “broadspectrum”.

Physical sunscreens contain mineral ingredients, such as zinc oxide or titanium dioxide. They deflect any damaging UV rays away from the skin. Physical sunscreens block both UVA and UVB rays.

##### Understanding Age Spots and Sun Damage

Age spots, often referred to as liver spots or sunspots, are flat, brown, or black spots that appear on the skin, typically on areas exposed to the sun, such as the face, hands, shoulders, and arms. These spots are primarily caused by prolonged exposure to the sun’s harmful UV rays, which stimulate the production of melanin, the pigment responsible for skin color. Over time, the uneven distribution of melanin leads to the development of pesky, no fun age spots.

Sun damage can also manifest as fine lines, wrinkles, and a loss of skin elasticity. Much like canyons are formed by weather erosion and a river cutting through underlying surfaces, your skin kind of does the same thing. The sun’s UV rays break down collagen and elastin fibers, essential proteins that keep the skin supple and youthful. Without these proteins, the skin loses its firmness and elasticity, resulting in sagging skin, fine lines, and wrinkles as deep as the Grand Canyon. OK, not really, but still deeper than any of us would like.

So, what can you do to tackle these common summer skin concerns and prepare for the upcoming holiday season with a radiant glow?

##### Prevention is Key

The first step in addressing age spots, sun damage, and fine lines is prevention. To prevent further damage and maintain youthful skin, take these proactive steps:

Sun Protection: Even during the cooler months in Central Oregon, it’s important to use a broad-spectrum sunscreen with an SPF of at least 30 every day. We get on average 300 days of sun! Although, even on overcast days, the sun is powerful and you need that extra protection. Apply it generously to all exposed areas, and reapply every two hours when outdoors. Protective clothing, hats, and sunglasses are also essential in shielding your skin from harmful UV rays.

Stay Hydrated: Drink plenty of water to keep your skin hydrated. Moisturized skin is less prone to fine lines and wrinkles. If it helps, enlist some of your friends to join you in a water challenge! Creating healthy habits is always more fun to do with other people. Plus, who are we kidding, we all need accountability.

Limit Sun Exposure: Avoid prolonged sun exposure, especially during peak UV hours between 10 am and 4 pm. Seek shade and take breaks in the shade during outdoor activities. Yes, even during the winter months. The sun is more intense all year long than we give it credit for.

Chemical Peels: Chemical peels use a chemical solution to remove the top layer of skin, revealing smoother, more even-toned skin underneath. At Juniper Dermatology, we offer three different types of peels: VI Peels, TCA, and Acne Peels.

### Oh my Gourd! What’s a Dracula Facial?!

In the ever-evolving world of cosmetic treatments and skincare, there is a seemingly endless array of options available to those seeking to rejuvenate their appearance and maintain youthful skin. But what exactly is a Dracula Facial, how does it work, and who stands to benefit from it? Get ready to vamp up your beauty…

###### Understanding the Dracula Facial

###### The Origins

When you think of the name Dracula, you may think of a vampire ready to suck the life out of humans. However, the procedure itself is more formally known as Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy, and it breathes new life into your skin. The nickname “Dracula Facial” comes from the process of using one’s own blood to enhance facial skin.

PRP therapy, which has been used in various medical fields for decades, was adapted for cosmetic purposes to promote skin rejuvenation and address a range of concerns, including fine lines, wrinkles, acne scars, and uneven skin tone. This innovative treatment has since captured the imagination of many individuals seeking natural and non-surgical methods to enhance their appearance.

###### How it Works

Our own blood contains valuable growth factors and platelets that can stimulate tissue repair and collagen production. The Dracula Facial treatment involves several steps:

- Blood Extraction: To begin, a small amount of the patient’s blood is drawn, similar to a regular blood test. A little bit goes a long way!
- Centrifugation: The extracted blood is then placed in a centrifuge, a machine that spins at high speeds to separate its components. This process isolates the platelet-rich plasma (PRP) from the red blood cells.
- PRP Application: The PRP, which is rich in growth factors and platelets, is then carefully applied to the patient’s face through a series of micro-injections or by applying it topically in combination with microneedling.
- Microneedling: Microneedling is performed in conjunction with the PRP application. Microneedling involves the use of tiny needles to create controlled micro-injuries in the skin’s surface, stimulating collagen production and allowing the PRP to penetrate deeper.
- Skin Rejuvenation: Over the following weeks and months, the PRP works to stimulate collagen production, improve blood flow, and enhance overall skin texture and tone.

###### Pain Management

The Dracula Facial is generally well-tolerated, and pain is typically minimal. Most practitioners apply a topical numbing cream to the treatment area before the procedure to ensure patient comfort. Patients may experience mild discomfort during the microneedling portion of the treatment, but it is usually brief and manageable.

September not only marks the beginning of fall, but also signifies National Skin Care Month! Skin Care from the Inside Out

In the quest for flawless skin, many of us tend to concentrate solely on external remedies such as creams, lotions, and serums. However, true beauty starts from within (not just the cheesy one-liner we often hear), and the health of your skin is directly linked to your overall well-being. Here are some holistic tips to ensure your skin radiates health from the inside out:

1. Stay Hydrated: Because your body is made up of 50-65% water, one of the simplest yet most effective ways to maintain healthy skin is by staying hydrated. Water helps flush out toxins from your body and keeps your skin supple and plump, giving you that more youthful glow. Aim to drink at least 8 glasses of water a day to maintain optimal hydration. Another good rule of thumb is to take your body weight, cut it in half, and that’s how many ounces you should be drinking per day.

2. Eat a Balanced Diet and Antioxidant-Rich Foods: Your diet plays a significant role in your skin’s appearance. Incorporate a variety of fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, whole grains, and healthy fats into your meals. These nutrient-rich foods provide essential vitamins and minerals that promote skin health. Antioxidants combat the damaging effects of free radicals, which can contribute to premature aging and skin damage. Berries (which helps fight free radicals, reduce inflammation, and promote collagen production for youthful skin.), leafy greens, nuts, and seeds are excellent sources of antioxidants that protect your skin from the inside.

3. Omega-3 Fatty Acids: Foods rich in omega-3 fatty acids, such as fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines), flaxseeds, and walnuts, can help maintain the skin’s natural oil barrier, keeping it moisturized and supple.

4. Limit Sugar and Processed Foods: This is a big one! Excessive sugar intake and processed foods can lead to inflammation, which can manifest as acne, redness, and other skin issues. Opt for whole, unprocessed foods to keep your skin looking its best.

5. Manage Stress: Did you know that chronic stress can wreak havoc on your skin? Not to mention your whole body! Practice stress-reduction techniques like meditation, deep breathing, yoga, or spending time in nature to promote healthy, clear skin.

6. Quality Sleep: As with so many things in life, adequate sleep is essential for skin repair and rejuvenation. Strive for 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night to wake up with a refreshed complexion.

7. Regular Exercise: Physical activity improves blood circulation, which delivers essential nutrients to your skin cells and helps eliminate toxins. Aim for at least 30 minutes of exercise most days of the week, even if it is just taking a walk during your lunch hour.

1. Cleansing: Start your skincare routine with a gentle cleanser to remove dirt, makeup, and impurities from your skin. Cleansing twice a day helps prevent clogged pores and breakouts.

### Holy Lipoma! What is That?! Causes, Symptoms, and Treatment Options for Lipomas

- August 16, 2023

Warning, there are some graphic photos below. Woozy readers, beware!

Lipomas, though generally harmless, can raise concerns when discovered beneath the skin. These benign tumors are formed from fat cells and are typically soft, painless, and slow-growing. Don’t fret though, lipomas are usually not a cause for worry. Understanding their characteristics, causes, and possible treatment options can help you make informed decisions if you find yourself dealing with a lipoma.

Uhhh…What are Lipomas?

A lipoma is a common type of benign tumor composed of adipose (fat) tissue. These tumors are usually located just beneath the skin and look kind of like a speed bump under your skin. They often feel soft and rubbery to the touch and can develop anywhere on the body, though they commonly appear on the neck, shoulders, back, arms, and thighs. They may look intimidating, but these growths are typically slow-growing and rarely cause pain or discomfort.

Symptoms and Characteristics of Lipomas

Lipomas are generally easy to identify due to their distinctive characteristics. Some key features of lipomas include:

Texture and Appearance

Lipomas are usually soft to the touch and have a doughy or rubbery consistency (think like a somewhat squishy bouncy ball). They can vary in size from less than one inch to several inches in diameter.

Painlessness

In most cases, lipomas are painless. However, they may cause discomfort if they press against nerves or other tissues.

Mobility

Lipomas are often moveable when touched. Not moveable as in it’s going to travel and go on vacation somewhere in your body, but it moves around the general area it rests. This is because they are located beneath the skin and can be shifted with gentle pressure.

Growth Rate

Lipomas tend to grow slowly over time. While their growth is typically gradual, it’s essential to monitor any changes in size, shape, or appearance.

Location

These fatty bumps called lipomas can develop anywhere on the body, including areas with significant fat deposits (hence the super scientific name we’re adopting here, “fatty bumps”).

Causes and Risk Factors

The exact cause of lipomas is not always clear, but there are several factors that may contribute to their development.

Genetics

There is evidence to suggest that genetics play a role in the development of lipomas. If you have a family history of lipomas, you might be at a slightly higher risk.

Age

Lipomas are more commonly diagnosed in adults between the ages of 40 and 60. However, they are certainly not discriminatory of being pesky to younger people as well.

Gender

While lipomas affect individuals of any gender, some studies suggest that they are more frequently observed in men.

Inherited Conditions

Certain inherited conditions, such as multiple familial lipomatosis, can increase the likelihood of developing multiple lipomas.

Other Factors

While rare, some factors such as trauma or injury to a specific area may trigger the growth of a lipoma.

How Does One Treat Lipomas?

Honestly, most lipomas are asymptomatic and do not require treatment unless they become bothersome due to size, location, or cosmetic concerns. Treatment options vary and depend on factors such as the size, location, and individual preferences. It’s important to consult a medical professional (https://www.juniperdermatology.com/patients/#self-scheduling) to determine the most appropriate course of action.

Observation

In many cases, healthcare providers may recommend simply monitoring the lipoma without intervention. If the lipoma is not causing discomfort or affecting your quality of life, observation may be a reasonable approach.

Surgical Removal

Surgical excision is the most common method of removing lipomas. The procedure involves making a small incision and removing the tumor along with any surrounding tissue. This method ensures complete removal and minimizes the risk of recurrence.

Liposuction

For larger lipomas, liposuction may be used to remove the growth. During this procedure, a thin tube is inserted through a small incision, and the fat is suctioned out. Yummy, right?

Steroid Injections

In some cases, a healthcare provider may recommend injecting steroids into the lipoma to reduce its size. This approach is often considered for lipomas that are difficult to remove due to their location.

Lipoma Removal Techniques

Depending on the characteristics of the lipoma, techniques such as minimal excision extraction or laser removal may be considered.

When to Seek Medical Attention

While lipomas are generally harmless, it’s important to seek medical attention if you notice any of the following:

Rapid Growth

If a lipoma suddenly grows or changes in appearance, consult a healthcare provider (https://www.juniperdermatology.com/patients/#self-scheduling).

Pain or Discomfort

While lipomas are usually painless, if you experience pain or discomfort in the area of a lipoma, it’s advisable to seek medical evaluation.

Uncertainty

If you’re unsure whether a growth is a lipoma or another type of growth, or you’re feeling anxious about it, come see us! We’ll be happy to check it out and help put your mind at ease.

Cosmetic Concerns

If a lipoma is affecting your appearance and causing cosmetic concerns, schedule an appointment and we’ll take a look and help you find a solution you feel confident about (https://www.juniperdermatology.com/patients/#self-scheduling).

Lipomas, although benign, can vary in size and location. Understanding the symptoms, causes, and treatment options associated with lipomas is essential for decision-making, but we’re here to help guide you through the process. If you’re concerned about a growth on your body, schedule an appointment so we can take a look and give you a proper diagnosis. Whether you choose observation, surgical removal, or another treatment method, the goal is to ensure your comfort, peace of mind, and overall health!

### 5 Skin Care Myths You Need to Stop Believing

- May 10, 2023

Taking care of your skin is important, but with so many skin care myths floating around, it can be hard to know what’s true and what’s false.

At Juniper Dermatology, we care about your health and wellbeing, which is why we strive to offer compassionate, and comprehensive dermatological care. When it comes to your skincare, it’s important to know the truth. Here are five common skin care myths that you need to stop believing:

Myth #1: You don’t need to wear sunscreen if it’s cloudy.

This is incredibly false! UV rays are present even on cloudy or cool days. In fact, up to 80% of the sun’s UV rays can pass through clouds. Any exposure to these UV rays can damage your skin and increase your risk of skin cancer, regardless of whether you burn or not. UV rays can also cause premature aging and other skin problems, so it’s important to wear sunscreen every day to protect your skin (and health)!

We recommend that you look for a broad-spectrum sunscreen with an SPF of at least 30 or higher. In addition to applying sunscreen before going out, make sure to reapply it every two hours if you’re spending time outside! The effects of sunscreen can wear out over time, so it’s vital that you reapply to continue protecting your skin throughout the day!

Myth #2: You should exfoliate your skin every day.

This is another big no! Over-exfoliating can damage your skin and cause irritation. When you over-exfoliate, your skin can become irritated and red, and it can disrupt your skin’s protective barrier. Instead of exfoliating every day, aim to exfoliate your skin once or twice a week, and be sure to use a gentle exfoliant or chemical exfoliant, such as alpha or beta hydroxy acids.

Myth #3: You can get rid of wrinkles with a cream.

While some creams may help reduce the appearance of wrinkles temporarily, they cannot get rid of them completely. While some anti-aging creams may contain ingredients that can help improve the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles by plumping and hydrating the skin, they do not actually reverse the aging process or eliminate wrinkles altogether. Wrinkles are caused by a variety of factors, which include genetics, sun damage, and your body’s natural aging process. Wrinkles are a natural part of aging and can only be treated with cosmetic procedures.

It’s also important to note that the best way to prevent wrinkles is to protect your skin from the sun by wearing sunscreen with an SPF of at least 30 and avoiding prolonged exposure to UV rays. A healthy diet and lifestyle, including regular exercise and sleeping for 7-9 hours each night, can also help support healthy skin and slow down the aging process.

Myth #4: You should always wash your face with hot water.

Hot water is damaging to your skin for a few different reasons. Constantly washing your face with hot water can strip the skin of its natural oils, which may lead to dryness, irritation, and even inflammation. This can be especially problematic if you already suffer from dry or sensitive skin, but it also affects people with oily skin.

Instead of hot water, we recommend that you use lukewarm water to wash your face and avoid prolonged exposure to hot water in showers or baths. This can help to keep your skin hydrated and healthy, while avoiding the potential damage caused by hot water.

Myth #5: The more expensive the skincare product, the better it is.

The price of skincare products is not always an indicator of its quality or effectiveness. In fact, some expensive products may contain unnecessary or even harmful ingredients, while some less expensive products may be just as effective or even better for your skin.

Instead of focusing on the price of a product, we recommend that you look at the ingredients and formula of a product to determine if it’s right for your skin. Look for skincare products that contain high-quality, dermatologist-recommended ingredients that have been shown to be effective for your specific skin concerns. A few key ingredients that you should look for are:

- Retinoids: Retinoids are a form of Vitamin A and are commonly used in skincare products due to their ability to increase cell turnover, stimulate collagen production, and improve the appearance of fine lines, wrinkles, and acne. Retinoids may be available in prescription-strength or over-the-counter formulations.
- Vitamin C: Vitamin C is an antioxidant that can help protect the skin from damage caused by UV radiation and environmental pollutants. It can also improve skin tone, brighten the complexion, and reduce the appearance of dark spots and hyperpigmentation.
- Hyaluronic Acid: Hyaluronic acid is a humectant that can help hydrate and plump the skin. It can hold up to 1,000 times its weight in water, making it an effective ingredient in moisturizers and serums.
- Alpha and Beta Hydroxy Acids: Alpha and beta hydroxy acids (AHAs and BHAs) are chemical exfoliants that can help improve the texture of the skin, reduce the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, and unclog pores. AHAs, such as glycolic acid, are water-soluble and work on the surface of the skin, while BHAs, such as salicylic acid, are oil-soluble and work on the surface of the skin and inside pores.
- Niacinamide: Niacinamide is a form of Vitamin B3 and has been shown to improve the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, reduce hyperpigmentation, and increase skin hydration. It may also have anti-inflammatory properties, making it a good option for those with acne-prone or sensitive skin.

It’s also important to avoid products that contain potentially harmful ingredients, such as fragrances, alcohol, or harsh surfactants. Of course, everyone’s skin is unique and the ingredients you prefer may depend on your skin type, sensitivities, and preferences, so it’s always a good idea to consult with a dermatologist or healthcare professional before using any new skincare product. #### Understanding Contact Dermatitis

##### Contact dermatitis occurs when your skin reacts to substances it touches. There are two main types:

Allergic Contact Dermatitis develops when your immune system becomes sensitized to a specific allergen. Common triggers include nickel, fragrances, preservatives, poison ivy, and certain cosmetic ingredients. This type of allergic contact dermatitis rash can appear hours to days after exposure.

Irritant Contact Dermatitis results from direct damage to the skin from harsh chemicals, soaps, or frequent hand washing. This type typically appears more quickly after contact.

Both types can affect any area of the body, but we commonly see allergic contact dermatitis on the face and contact dermatitis on the eyelid and lips due to cosmetic and personal care product exposure.
