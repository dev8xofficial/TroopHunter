### ABOUT

INSPIRED is an impact-driven innovation community designed to bring groundbreaking ideas to life. We connect visionaries, investors, and collaborators in a digital space where disruptive innovation meets the right people—without barriers.
‍
Founded in August 2023 by Maximilian Werner, INSPIRED was created to solve a critical challenge—too many world-changing ideas struggle to reach the right people, limiting their potential for impact. Maximilian envisioned a better way: a global platform where bold ideas get the support they deserve.
‍
More than just a platform, INSPIRED is a catalyst for bold innovation. By leveraging social technology, we make it easier than ever for important ideas to gain visibility, attract support, and drive real impact.
‍
Based in Zürich, Switzerland, we collaborate with creative minds around the world to shape the future of innovation. With big plans ahead, INSPIRED is set to become the go-to platform for the next generation of changemakers—those who dare to think big and build a better world.
