# Community Content Contributors – AI, Circularity, Climate, AgriTech, MedTech — INSPIRED — Job Posting

## Role Summary
INSPIRED is a curated global social network for innovation, connecting over 25,000 members across 150 countries to advance ideas aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals. The platform has launched dedicated community spaces across domains such as AI, Circularity, Health, and AgriTech. This role involves contributing short-form, high-signal content to activate and engage these communities. Contributors are responsible for producing concise, thought-provoking posts that stimulate discussion and add domain-specific insight.

## Responsibilities
- Write 3 short-form posts per week (50–250 words each) within selected vertical(s).
- Create content that sparks conversation and community engagement.
- Share perspectives on relevant news with original commentary.
- Contribute informed opinions or observations grounded in domain expertise.
- Engage with community threads and discussions.

## Requirements

##### Must Have
- Demonstrated involvement or expertise in at least one vertical (AI, Circularity, Climate, AgriTech, MedTech).
- Ability to produce concise, insightful written content quickly.
- Familiarity with trends, developments, and discourse within the chosen field(s).
- Strong opinionated writing style suitable for community engagement.

##### Nice to Have

- Ability to cover multiple verticals.
