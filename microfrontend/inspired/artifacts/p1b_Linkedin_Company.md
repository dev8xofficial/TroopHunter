# INSPIRED — LinkedIn Profile

## About

INSPIRED is the world's first social network built entirely for innovation. We give innovators, investors, event organizers, and ecosystem builders a continuous, purpose-built space to connect, collaborate, and grow. Year-round, across borders, beyond the event.

For innovators, INSPIRED is your global stage. Showcase your work, signal what you need and get discovered by the right people at the right time. For event organizers, INSPIRED becomes your 365-day digital extension. Exhibitors, speakers, and attendees stay connected long after the last keynote, inside a branded space that carries your identity forward. For ecosystem builders, your cohorts, alumni chapters, and accelerator programs get a home that grows with them without the noise of generic platforms or the friction of juggling multiple tools. For investors, we surface vetted innovators, high-signal projects, and deal flow from a curated global community of 25,000+ changemakers, NGOs, foundations, and ventures driving real-world impact.

🌍 Swiss-founded. Globally connected.

🥕 INSPIRED Innovator Spotlight: Zélo
They are scaling zero-waste grocery shopping with a smart reusable-container model that removes disposable packaging from the equation.

Why it's special: Zélo's innovation is a QR-code system that ensures full traceability for every container. Each QR code links to a digital product label, so customers get transparency without single-use packaging. Containers are free for customers, and a smart monitoring script tracks returns within 3 months. QR codes are scanned when orders are prepared and again when containers arrive back at the depot, closing the loop in a model that's circular, transparent, and built to scale.

🚀 On INSPIRED, they're looking for franchise partners and local operators to expand across Swiss cities, plus sustainable brands and producers who want to join a traceable, reusable packaging ecosystem.

🚀 INSPIRED Innovator Spotlight: REA Space
In space, survival isn't just about technology, it's about protecting the human body. As missions get longer, microgravity remains a major challenge, weakening muscles, reducing bone density, and impacting cardiovascular health.

REA Space is tackling this with EMSi: a next-generation suit designed to recreate the benefits of Earth's gravity in space, helping astronauts stay stronger, healthier, and mission-ready for long-duration exploration.

Why it's special: Instead of relying on bulky equipment, REA Space brings gravity-like support into a wearable system, enabling continuous protection of human performance where gravity doesn't exist.
