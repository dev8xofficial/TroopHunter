### Healing in ASL. Therapy that understands Deaf experience.

ASL-centered online therapy for Deaf, DeafBlind, Hard of Hearing, DeafDisabled, and Late-Deafened communities.

We serve clients in: Georgia, Pennsylvania, Florida, New York, Maine, Maryland, Texas, and Utah.

- 12 Deaf therapists
- 8 states licensed

#### Created by and for the Deaf Community
As a Deaf-led practice, we center the lived experiences of our community. No explaining. No educating. No communication barriers. Care that starts from shared ground. We bring lived Deaf experience, ASL fluency, and trauma-informed practice into every session.

#### Insurance & Payment Options
We accept many major insurance plans, including Medicaid, Medicare, and commercial insurance plans. Coverage varies by state and plan. We'll verify your coverage before your first session.

#### How It Works
1. **Schedule a Consultation** — Reach out by form, email, text, or videophone. We usually respond within 1–2 business days.
2. **Meet With Your Therapist** — Discuss fit, communication needs, and goals — no pressure.
3. **Begin Therapy** — Choose a schedule that fits your life. We'll support you along the way.

### Therapy That Respects Language and Culture

We provide therapy in ASL, so Deaf and Hard of Hearing individuals can get support without communication barriers. We aim to respect identity and build trust.

#### Why Deaf-Led Therapy Matters
Many Deaf people have experienced communication barriers and a lack of understanding from hearing clinicians. DMC bridges this gap with lived community experience.
