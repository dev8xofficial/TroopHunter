Tax Planning Notes

📌 Mortgage Interest: Clients can deduct mortgage interest on loans up to $750,000 for tax years 2018 and beyond.

📌 Property Tax Cap: SALT deduction is capped at $10,000 per household.

📌 Capital Gains Exclusion: Primary residence sales may qualify for $250,000 ($500,000 joint) exclusion if lived in 2 of last 5 years.

📌 1099-S Filing: Required for real estate transactions $600 or more. Verify all clients received proper forms.
