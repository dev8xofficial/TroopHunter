# User Personas: Admin Portal

## Persona 1: Sarah Burke (System Administrator)
- **Role Code**: ADMIN
- **Goals**: Monitor the overall financial and operational health of the brokerage. Manage the vendor (partner) network. Onboard new real estate agents.
- **Pain Points**: Needs quick access to aggregate data without clicking through individual transactions. Needs to ensure new agents and partners are onboarded smoothly but strictly vetted.
- **Primary Screens**: Dashboard, Analytics, Users, Partners.

## Persona 2: Mark Davis (Transaction Coordinator)
- **Role Code**: TC
- **Goals**: Keep all real estate transactions moving smoothly, legally, and compliantly. Approve or reject agent document submissions. Verify stage changes reflect actual legal contract status.
- **Pain Points**: Gets flooded with emails about documents. Needs a structured "Inbox" of actionable approvals instead of hunting for what's changed.
- **Primary Screens**: Dashboard, Transactions, Documents.
