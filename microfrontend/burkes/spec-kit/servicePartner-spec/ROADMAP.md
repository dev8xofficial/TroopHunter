# Roadmap — Service Partner Portal Spec-Kit

## Vision

Deliver a production-ready Service Partner Portal that empowers Burkes Group service partners with a unified, spec-driven platform for managing referrals, jobs, quotes, reviews, service areas, earnings, and their company profile.

**Target**: Q2 2026

| 001 — Dashboard | ✅ Approved | P0 |
| 002 — Referrals | ✅ Approved | P0 |

**Goals**:
