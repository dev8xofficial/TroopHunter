# Roadmap — Attorney Portal Spec-Kit

## Vision

Deliver a production-ready Attorney Portal that empowers Burkes Group closing attorneys with a unified, spec-driven platform for verifying transaction amounts, reviewing legal documents, managing client cases, and ensuring closing compliance.

**Target**: Q2 2026

| 001 — Dashboard | ✅ Approved | P0 |
| 002 — Transactions | ✅ Approved | P0 |
