# GLOSSARY

* **Closing Cost**: The total itemized amount required to finalize the property transaction, payable at the signing.
* **Cash to Close**: The net liquid amount a buyer must bring to closing or a seller will receive out of escrow.
* **Closing Disclosure**: Official document detailing loan terms, projected payments, and total closing costs.
* **Asset Split**: A percentage or flat-sum allocation of real estate properties between parties, usually during divorce.
* **Title Commitment**: Evidence of the title company's intent to insure the property if specific requirements are met.
* **Net Equity**: Total available dollar amount to be distributed among parties after subtracting loan liabilities and closing costs.
* **Discrepancy Flag**: A registered business violation in data consistency (e.g. loan amount mismatch) that halts a transaction workflow until explicitly resolved by an authorized actor.
