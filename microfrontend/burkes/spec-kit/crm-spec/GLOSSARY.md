# Glossary - CRM Platform

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Activity | An immutable event attached to a contact or lead, such as a call, SMS, email, note, or stage change. |
| Arive | The mortgage platform that remains required for mortgage workflow and must integrate into the CRM. |
| Burkes Group Marketing LLC | The legal platform owner and data owner for the CRM. |
| Call recording retention | The required period that recorded calls must remain accessible for compliance evidence. |
| Client Portal intake | A web or QR-code driven external form submission that creates or enriches a CRM contact. |
| Compliance bridge | The legal and operational role the marketing entity plays between mortgage and insurance teams. |
| Contact | The canonical customer record shared across departments. |
| Department owner | A senior operator with write access in their department and read access across the CRM. |
| Follow Up Boss | Legacy real-estate CRM to be replaced by the new CRM during Phase 1. |
| Lead transfer | A deliberate reassignment of departmental ownership for a contact or lead. |
| Missing Data | A contact state where minimum intake exists but enrichment fields still need completion. |
| Outlook auto-log | The process of linking inbound or outbound email metadata to the relevant CRM contact. |
| Portal layer | The six external-facing Burkes portals that share the backend with the CRM. |
| Recording reference | A storage pointer and retention metadata record for a call recording. |
| Unified contact record | The principle that customer information is stored once and progressively enriched, not duplicated. |
| VOIP provider | The external telephony service used for calling, SMS, and recording. |
