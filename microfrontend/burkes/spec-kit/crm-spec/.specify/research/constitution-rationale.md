# Constitution Rationale

This note explains why the CRM constitution prioritizes clarity, unified data, compliance, and SaaS readiness.

## Why operator-first clarity matters

Internal users already juggle multiple systems. The CRM only succeeds if it reduces mental overhead and surfaces the next action quickly.

## Why unified contacts matter

The Burkes model depends on coordinated service across insurance, mortgage, and real estate. Duplicate contacts would recreate the current fragmentation.

## Why compliance is a first-class principle

Data ownership, call recording, and retention requirements shape the platform architecture. These are not implementation details to postpone.

## Why graceful incompleteness matters

Real operators start with imperfect data. The system must accept partial intake while still guiding enrichment.

## Why SaaS readiness is included now

The business explicitly wants future commercialization. Early contract discipline is cheaper than retrofitting later.
