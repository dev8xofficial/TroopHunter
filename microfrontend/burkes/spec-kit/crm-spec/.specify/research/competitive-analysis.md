# Competitive Analysis

## Follow Up Boss

### Strengths

- Mature real-estate lead workflow
- Familiar experience for real-estate operators

### Gaps for Burkes Group

- Not built for three coordinated departments
- Weak fit for shared compliance and marketing-entity ownership model
- Limited path to Burkes-controlled SaaS packaging

## Agency Zoom / Vertafore

### Strengths

- Insurance-specific workflow support

### Gaps for Burkes Group

- Insurance-centric rather than cross-department
- Does not solve unified contact ownership
- Adds another silo for activity history

## HubSpot-Class General CRM

### Strengths

- Broad integrations and automation

### Gaps for Burkes Group

- Would still require heavy customization for department scopes, retention rules, and portal integration
- Cost and vendor lock-in are misaligned with the internal-then-SaaS goal

## Conclusion

The CRM needs a custom operational model anchored around a unified contact record and compliance-aware communications, not just generic lead management.
