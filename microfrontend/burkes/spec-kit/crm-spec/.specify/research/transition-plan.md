# Transition Plan

## Goal

Move Burkes Group operations from Follow Up Boss and fragmented department tooling toward the new CRM without losing history or interrupting customer communication.

## Sequence

1. Define and validate Phase 1 data contracts.
2. Import and normalize legacy contacts from Follow Up Boss.
3. Stand up communications workflows for calling, SMS, and email.
4. Run parallel validation for a limited operator group.
5. Cut over real-estate lead handling to the CRM.
6. Expand department adoption and reduce reliance on legacy CRM screens.

## Risks

- Duplicate contacts during migration
- Missing ownership mapping from legacy tools
- Communication mismatch if provider integrations are incomplete

## Mitigations

- Deduplication by email and phone before final import
- Explicit ownership mapping review during migration rehearsal
- Internal-only cutover first, followed by staged production use
