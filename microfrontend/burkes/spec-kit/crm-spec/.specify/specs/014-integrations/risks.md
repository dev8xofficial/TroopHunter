# Risks: CRM Integrations (014)

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Connectors present inconsistent health semantics | Medium | High | Normalize status and freshness models across providers |
| Users overestimate what reconnect actions can fix | Medium | Medium | Label supported actions clearly and provide fallback guidance |
| Sensitive admin detail leaks to non-admin users | Low | High | Enforce role-based visibility and audit access |
