# Success Metrics: CRM Integrations (014)

| KPI | Target | Alert |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mean time to detect degraded connector | < 10 min | > 30 min |
| Mean time to recover supported connector issues | < 2 hr | > 8 hr |
| Connector freshness within SLA | > 95% | < 85% |

## Success Definition

Integrations is successful when provider health, impact, and supported remediation become visible enough that connector issues stop feeling like unexplained product failures.
