# Success Metrics: CRM Calls (006)

| KPI | Target | Alert |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Call connection success | > 97% | < 90% |
| Recording success for required calls | > 99% | < 95% |
| Post-call note completion | > 85% | < 60% |
| Unmatched-call resolution within 1 day | > 90% | < 70% |

## Success Definition

Calls is successful when business calling shifts into the CRM and recorded history becomes reviewable without relying on personal devices or disconnected logs.
