# Success Metrics: CRM SMS (007)

| KPI | Target | Alert |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SMS send success | > 98% | < 92% |
| Inbound reply routing accuracy | > 95% | < 85% |
| Unread reply aging over 1 business day | < 10% | > 25% |
| Opt-out block correctness | 100% | Any confirmed miss |

## Success Definition

SMS is successful when text conversations stay in the CRM and operators trust unread and ownership state enough to stop relying on external messaging inboxes.
