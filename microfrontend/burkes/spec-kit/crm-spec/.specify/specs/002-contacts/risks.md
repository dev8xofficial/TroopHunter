# Risks: CRM Contacts (002)

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duplicate contacts fragment communication history | High | High | Strong duplicate warnings and import review |
| Minimal-data records never get enriched | Medium | Medium | Missing-data filters and task prompts |
| Consent or source information is lost in intake | Medium | High | Require normalized intake payloads |
