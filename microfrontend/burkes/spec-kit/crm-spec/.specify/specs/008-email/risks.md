# Risks: CRM Email (008)

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Outlook connectivity issues create distrust | Medium | High | Visible degraded state and read-only fallback |
| Unknown senders accumulate | Medium | Medium | Resolution queue and operator prompts |
| Users expect full mailbox parity | Medium | Medium | Keep the feature clearly operational and CRM-focused |
