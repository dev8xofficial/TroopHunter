# Success Metrics: CRM Insurance (011)

| KPI | Target | Alert |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quote-readiness completion rate | > 85% | < 65% |
| Renewal follow-up before due date | > 90% | < 75% |
| Legacy sync freshness within SLA | > 95% | < 85% |

## Success Definition

Insurance is successful when the team can manage quoting and renewals from the CRM without relying on scattered spreadsheets or ambiguous legacy-system context.
