# Risks: CRM Admin Settings (016)

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Excessive admin power creates accidental platform changes | Medium | High | Require confirmations, validations, and audit trails |
| Delegated settings drift across departments | Medium | Medium | Limit owner delegation and show effective scope clearly |
| Entitlement language confuses implementation readiness with billing reality | Medium | Medium | Separate active entitlements from future subscription automation |
