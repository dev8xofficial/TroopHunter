# Roadmap - CRM Spec-Kit

## Vision

Deliver a compliance-aware, multi-department CRM that becomes Burkes Group's operational backbone first and a SaaS-ready platform second.

### Phase 1 goals

- Replace Follow Up Boss for operational lead handling
- Establish the unified contact record and transfer workflow
- Launch internal communications through calls, SMS, and email
- Provide role-scoped access with compliance-safe activity logging
- Bridge client portal intake directly into CRM contacts and leads
