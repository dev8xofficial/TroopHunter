# Code of Conduct - CRM Spec-Kit

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

### Positive behaviors

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Giving constructive, project-centered feedback
- Focusing on clarity, rigor, and shared success
- Showing empathy toward collaborators and reviewers

### Unacceptable behaviors

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Publishing private information without permission
- Personal attacks or hostile review behavior
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering environment

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
