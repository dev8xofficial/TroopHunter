# FAQ - CRM Platform

## Why is the CRM separate from the portals?

The portals serve external and semi-external users. The CRM serves internal operators who need communications, lead routing, compliance history, and cross-department visibility. Both layers share a backend and data model, but they have different user needs.

## Who owns the data?

Burkes Group Marketing LLC owns the platform and the data. This is a compliance requirement, not an organizational preference.

## Why is Phase 1 focused on calls, SMS, and email?

Meeting analysis showed these three channels are the non-negotiable communications backbone. Contact management and pipeline workflows are built around them.

## Why build a custom CRM instead of using Follow Up Boss or Agency Zoom?

The business needs unified cross-department visibility, tighter compliance behavior, long-term SaaS potential, and control over customer data and workflow design.

## Does Phase 1 include a native mobile app?

No. Phase 1 is designed for responsive web and PWA compatibility. Native mobile is deferred until a later phase if the PWA proves insufficient.

## Will mortgage and insurance users be able to edit each other's data?

No. The CRM provides cross-department visibility, but writes remain scoped to the relevant department and role.

## Are emails and SMS messages stored inside the CRM?

The CRM stores metadata, linkage, and operational context. External providers remain the system of record for message storage where appropriate.

## What happens to Follow Up Boss?

Phase 1 includes import and migration support so Burkes Group can transition off Follow Up Boss once the core CRM is operationally ready.

## Is Arive being replaced?

No. Arive remains a required mortgage integration in Phase 1.

## What makes this implementation-ready?

An implementation-ready phase has approved feature specs, data contracts, interaction flows, plans, and task breakdowns that are precise enough for engineering to begin without re-discovery.
