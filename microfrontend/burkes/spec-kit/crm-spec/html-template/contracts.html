Schemas

Top-level models represented in the HTML template

CRM Contact

`contact.schema.json`

  • Required: id, first_name, last_name, source, departments, assigned_agents, pipeline_stage
  • Includes consent, portal origin, missing_data, and department sub-records
CRM Lead and Activity

`lead.schema.json` and `activity.schema.json`

  • Lead fields include department, stage, priority, assigned_agent_id, last_activity_at
  • Activity fields include type, actor_id, occurred_at, metadata, notes, recording reference
Communication schemas

Calendar, call recording, campaign, meeting

  • Calendar Event: provider, starts_at, ends_at, contact and lead linkage
  • Call Recording: department, recorded_at, expires_at, access_scope
  • Email Campaign: audience_count, excluded_count, metrics
  • Video Meeting: provider, starts_at, retention_expires_at
Department sub-records

Insurance, Mortgage, Real Estate

  • Policy: policy_type, carrier, premium, effective and expiration dates, VIN
  • Mortgage: status, arive_loan_id, loan_type, pre_approval_amount, lender_name
  • Transaction: transaction_type, status, property_address, dotloop_id, closing_date, commission_amount
Operations

Integration, report, user, admin settings

  • Integration Connector: provider, status, priority, owner, affected_features, actions
  • Report Request: scope, date_range, metrics, group_by
  • User: role, departments, active, license_number
  • Admin Settings: users, retention_policies, entitlements
