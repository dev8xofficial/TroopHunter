# Domain Glossary

## Core Entities
- **Purchase Agreement**: The legally binding contract between the buyer and seller confirming the offer.
- **Buyer Disclosure Form**: Form detailing specific disclosures required from the buyer.
- **Seller Disclosure Statement**: Form detailing known property issues declared by the seller.
- **Pre-Approval Letter**: Document from the lender confirming the buyer is qualified for a specific loan amount.
- **Loan Estimate**: Document detailing the estimated interest rate, monthly payment, and total closing costs for the loan.
- **Income Verification (W-2)**: Financial document provided by the client to prove income history.
- **Closing Disclosure**: Final document detailing all actual costs associated with closing the mortgage.
- **Title Commitment**: Document from the title company committing to issue a title insurance policy.

## Business Concepts
- **Transaction Progress**: The overarching state machine determining what phase the real estate purchase is currently in (11 stages).
- **Mortgage Application**: The structured payload of personal, property, employment, and financial data required to request a loan.
- **Insurance Portfolio**: The aggregated details of Auto, Home, and Warranty policies linked to the new property.
- **Partner Service Provider**: A pre-vetted professional (plumber, roofer, electrician) recommended based on the property zip code.
