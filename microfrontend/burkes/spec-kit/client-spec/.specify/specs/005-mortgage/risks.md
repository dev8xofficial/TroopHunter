# Risk Register: Mortgage Spec

## Critical Risks

| Risk      | Title                                        | Probability  | Impact   | Mitigation                          |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------ | -------- | ----------------------------------- |
| D-MTG-001 | Underwriting stalls (delayed clear-to-close) | High (50%)   | Critical | Escalation alerts; weekly check-ins |
| D-MTG-002 | Conditions lost/forgotten                    | Medium (25%) | High     | Checklist + notifications           |
| D-MTG-003 | Interest rate lock expires                   | Medium (30%) | Medium   | Auto-reminders; lock tracking       |

---

## Success Criteria

✅ Clear-to-close issued 3-5 days pre-closing
✅ Zero condition tracking failures
✅ Rate lock never expires pre-closing
