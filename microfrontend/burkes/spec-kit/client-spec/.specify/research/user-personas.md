# User Personas

## Overview

These personas represent the 6 canonical roles in the Burkes Group portal. Based on interviews, role analysis, and journey mapping (Jan-Apr 2024).

---

## 1. Sarah Chen, Client (CL)

**Age**: 35 | **Occupation**: Software engineer | **First-time buyer**: Yes (**Profile**: Tech-savvy but financially anxious)

### Goals

- Know the status at all times ("Am I on track?" "What could delay closing?")
- Minimize time in meetings (prefers async updates)
- Understand next steps without jargon
- Protect privacy (don't want team seeing financial details more than necessary)

### Pain Points

- Gets 10+ emails per day from team; cannot find key info
- Doesn't know who to contact for what (agent? attorney? lender?)
- Scared of "something going wrong" but no visibility
- Previous home purchase was chaotic; wants better control this time

### How She'd Use Portal

- **Daily** (8 AM): Check portal status during coffee; see "Next: Submit appraisal"
- **Weekly** (Friday): Read activity feed; see what team did
- **Blocker**: Needs to upload financial docs; uses mobile, struggles with file size
- **Closing day**: Needs simple checklist (what to bring, where to be, who's there)

### Ideal Experience

```
Portal homepage: Big status (Underwriting 🟡)
CTA: "Submit Appraisal Form" (button)
Activity: "Amy (your agent) is still working on conditions"
Timeline: 11-stage progress bar; we're at stage 9
Days: 12 days to closing (⚠️ holidays approaching)
```

### Success Metric

"I felt informed throughout. No surprises at closing. Preferred portal + 1 email vs 10 emails + call."

## 2. Michael Garcia, Real Estate Agent (AG)

**Age**: 42 | **Tenure**: 15 years | **Transactions/year**: 45 | **Tech comfort**: Medium

### Goals

- Close transactions smoothly without drama
- Reduce admin time (currently 30% of workday is "chasing down docs")
- Maintain relationships (agent ↔ client, agent ↔ attorney, agent ↔ lender)
- Get paid (ensure closing happens on schedule)

### Pain Points

- Juggling 20+ concurrent transactions via email, calls, SMS → chaos
- Cannot remember "who promised what and by when"
- Clients get angry when things delayed; but delay root cause was lender/attorney, not agent
- Licensure requires strict process; worries about "did we follow CTC guidelines?"

### How He'd Use Portal

- **Daily (9 AM)**: Dashboard; quick scan of all 20 transactions (which need attention?)
- **Action**: Mark docs received, email client "your appraisal is in; attorney reviewing"
- **Escalation**: Notes that client unhappy; escalate to coordinator to call
- **CYA**: Activity log proves "I sent appraisal to attorney 2/15; they didn't act until 3/1"

### Ideal Experience

```
Agent Dashboard: 20 transactions, color-coded status (red = at risk, yellow = pending)
Quick Actions: "Send appraisal to attorney", "Request updated employment letter"
Activity Feed: Shows last action per transaction (when, by whom)
Risk: Flag if > 10 days between milestones without activity
```

### Success Metric

"Closed 45 transactions without major delays. Zero compliance issues. Cut admin time 40%."

## 3. Jennifer Walsh, Mortgage Lender (LN)

**Age**: 38 | **Role**: Loan Officer | **Loans/month**: 15-20 | **Tech comfort**: High

### Goals

- Underwrite loans quickly (30-45 days is industry standard)
- Get all required documents without 3 follow-up emails
- Verify insurance/appraisal before clear-to-close
- Limit liability (prove we followed guidelines; CTC checklist complete)

### Pain Points

- Clients are slow to provide docs
- Cross-communicate with agent + attorney slows timeline
- Manual tracking of doc checklist (spreadsheet = error-prone)
- Compliance officer audits CTC; must prove "we requested X on date Y, client provided date Z"

### How She'd Use Portal

- **Weekly (Monday 10 AM)**: Review 15-20 active loans; note status of each
- **Doc request**: Click "request appraisal", system sends to agent → client sends back
- **Clear-to-close**: Verify appraisal ≥ LTV, insurance covers amount, title clear → click "Ready"
- **Audit**: Export activity log as proof for compliance review (who did what, when)

### Ideal Experience

```
Lender Dashboard: 20 loans; status per stage (underwriting, conditions pending, CTC ready)
Checklist: ☐ Appraisal ☐ Insurance ☐ Title | 2/3 done (appraisal pending 6 days)
Activity: "Agent: Appraisal ordered 3/1, Appraiser: Completed 3/12, You: Reviewed 3/13"
CYA: One-click "export activity log" for auditor
```

### Success Metric

"Average loan 35 days (industry = 40). Zero CTC denials. Auditor passes without questions."

## 4. David Norton, Closing Attorney (AT)

**Age**: 55 | **Practice**: Solo | **Closings/month**: 30-40 | **Tech comfort**: Low

### Goals

- Spot title issues early (before closing; costly to delay)
- Manage closing day efficiently (can't afford to over-run)
- Protect client legally (flag issues, manage escrow, final review)
- Work efficiently (still prefers phone calls to email; respects client privacy)

### Pain Points

- Title search takes 5-7 days; if delays found, client blames attorney
- Closing day often chaos: missing docs, last-minute disclosures, client questions mid-closing
- Cannot see full picture (agent says one thing, client and lender may have different understanding)
- Worried about liability (missed lien, bad title, client sued)

### How She'd Use Portal

- **When case assigned**: Download transaction summary (all players, timeline, property address)
- **Doc review**: Title report submitted → flag any issues in activity
- **Pre-closing**: Checklist of docs needed for closing day; verify all present
- **Closing**: Minimal portal use (but audit log captures who signed what)

### Ideal Experience

```
Attorney Dashboard: 30 active cases; "Closing in 3 days" flags with doc status
Title Check: Any liens/encumbrances auto-flagged
Pre-Closing Checklist: Ensures all required docs present 48h before closing
```

### Success Metric

"All closings completed on time. Zero title issues post-closing. Clients hire me again (repeat biz)."

## 5. Robert Kim, CPA (CP)

**Age**: 48 | **Specialization**: Real estate tax | **Clients/year**: 50-100 | **Tech comfort**: Medium

### Goals

- Advise clients on 1031 exchange implications (deferring capital gains tax)
- Coordinate with closing date deadline (taxes due date implications)
- Have visibility without being overwhelmed
- Keep client data private (confidential tax info)

### Pain Points

- Usually added late in transaction ("Hey CPA, we close in 10 days; do we pay capital gains?")
- Cannot see transaction details (agent doesn't share closing timeline)
- Difficult to coordinate with attorney (scheduling, doc sharing)
- Compliance: Must keep tax advice confidential (other roles shouldn't see)

### How She'd Use Portal

- **When engaged**: Added to transaction; can see real estate details, closing date only
- **Tax planning**: Notes on portfolio implications (1031 vs. taxable sale)
- **Coordination**: Messages with lender + agent (timing for capital gains calc)
- **Privacy**: Attorney notes hidden (CPA focus = tax only)

### Ideal Experience

```
CPA Portal Access: Property address, client info, closing date, transaction value
Tax Notes: Record 1031 opportunity, estimated tax impact
Messages: "Client wants to reinvest proceeds; timeline options?"
Restricted: Cannot see attorney work product; attorney cannot see tax strategy
```

### Success Metric

"Identified 1031 opportunity saving clients $50K in taxes. Coordinated closing date to optimize timing."

## 6. Michelle Rodriguez, Transaction Coordinator (TC)

**Age**: 32 | **Tenure**: 7 years | **Transactions/month**: 20-30 | **Tech comfort**: Very High

### Goals

- Keep transaction flowing smoothly (no dropped balls)
- Communicate with all stakeholders (client, agent, lender, attorney, suppliers)
- Meet deadlines (inspections due by X, appraisal by Y, clear-to-close by Z)
- Reduce repetitive tasks (sending reminders, chasing missing docs)

### Pain Points

- Remembering which doc is missing from whom (20 transactions × 10 docs = 200 pieces to track)
- Clients miss deadlines because they didn't know due date
- Manual tracking spreadsheet = error-prone
- Constantly copying emails, documents into multiple systems

### How She'd Use Portal

- **Daily dashboard**: 20 transactions; due dates color-coded (red = overdue, yellow = due soon)
- **Doc tracking**: Who uploaded what; auto-generate "we're still waiting on: appraisal, insurance"
- **Automation**: Auto-email reminders ("Your appraisal due in 3 days")
- **Reporting**: Can pull "X docs uploaded, Y pending, Z overdue" for Friday meeting

### Ideal Experience

```
Coordinator Dashboard: 20 transactions; doc status by category
Overdue Alert: "Appraisal overdue 4 days (due 2/28, today 3/4); attorney didn't get yet"
Automation: Auto-email client "Insurance quote due in 2 days"
Reporting: "Week summary: 6 appraisals due, 5 received, 1 overdue"
```

### Success Metric

"Avg transaction moves to close 2 days faster. Zero missed deadlines. Admin time reduced 50%."

## Cross-Persona Journey Map

### Transaction Day 10 (Inspection Stage)

**Sarah (CL)** - Emotional state: Hopeful ✅

- Sees: Inspection scheduled (green), inspection date, inspector name
- Does: Confirms she can be home at 2 PM; uploads inspection fee (if required)
- Feels: In control; knows what's happening

**Michael (AG)** - Emotional state: Confident 💼

- Sees: 20 transactions; Sarah's is on track (no red flags)
- Does: Marks "inspection confirmed with Sarah" in activity; notes if house has issues post-inspection
- Feels: Organized; client happy

**Jennifer (LN)** - Emotional state: Neutral 📋

- Sees: Sarah's transaction on timeline; appraisal ordered yesterday
- Does: Nothing yet (lender acts after inspection)
- Feels: Patient; knows what's coming

**David (AT)** - Emotional state: Waiting ⏳

- Sees: Inspection happening; will review for title issues if reported
- Does: Nothing yet (attorney acts when title search needed)
- Feels: Non-urgent; routine stage

**Robert (CP)** - Emotional state: Not engaged yet 🚫

- Not contacted yet (added only if 1031 considered)

**Michelle (TC)** - Emotional state: Tracking ✔️

- Sees: Inspection confirmed; next deadline is appraisal (3 days out)
- Does: Pre-emptively messages appraiser; confirms Sarah has contact info
- Feels: Proactive; staying ahead of timeline

## Accessibility & Inclusion Notes

- **Sarah**: Tech-comfortable but English 2nd language; specs avoid jargon
- **Jennifer**: Relies on accessibility features; portal must be WCAG AA compliant
- **Michelle**: High volume; needs efficient UI (keyboard shortcuts, bulk actions)

All personas designed for ~95th percentile user. Edge cases:

- Elderly client (prefer phone, large text)
- Non-English speaker (multi-language support)
- Visually impaired attorney (screen reader; high contrast)

These are Phase 2+ enhancements; Phase 1 focuses on personas above.
