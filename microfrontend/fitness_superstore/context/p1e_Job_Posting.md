# Shopify Plus + QuickBooks Online + A2X Consultant for Fixed-Price Audit, Stabilization & Workflow — Job Posting

## Role Summary

The client runs a multi-entity ecommerce business and needs an experienced consultant or implementer to take over Phase 1 of a systems and accounting workflow project. The work centers on stabilizing and auditing the current Shopify Plus, QuickBooks Online, and A2X environment, then designing a documented future-state operating model for how orders, accounting flows, reconciliations, and exceptions should be handled. The primary outcome is a clean, accurate, well-documented Phase 1 workflow that improves financial reliability and gives the team a clear implementation and handoff plan.

## Responsibilities

- Audit and stabilize the current Shopify, A2X, and QuickBooks Online environment.
- Review and help resolve A2X posting and sync consistency issues.
- Resolve manual order handling, reconciliation, and financial accuracy issues.
- Review Shopify clearing and bank deposit logic, chart of accounts structure, and account mapping.
- Identify and reduce duplicate revenue risk, bank or entity mismatches, and legacy order bleed across entities.
- Design a documented future-state workflow for when orders should stay in Shopify, use Shopify B2B or Company Accounts, use Shopify Draft Orders, or become QBO-only invoices.
- Define how manual orders should be marked paid, reconciled, and posted into QuickBooks Online.
- Configure or recommend the Phase 1 operating model for branded quote, estimate, and invoice setup.
- Establish customer and product governance between Shopify and QuickBooks Online.
- Define sales tax, shipping, freight, payment, deposit, and partial-payment handling.
- Recommend permissions, SOPs, and a clear separation between native platform configuration, automation, and custom development.
- Deliver a current-state audit summary, recommended architecture, order-routing matrix, accounting path into QBO, implementation plan, team handoff notes, and deferred-items list.

## Requirements

##### Must Have

- Strong hands-on experience with Shopify Plus.
- Strong hands-on experience with Shopify B2B, Draft Orders, and Flow.
- Strong hands-on experience with QuickBooks Online.
- Strong hands-on experience with A2X.
- Strong experience with ecommerce operations and accounting workflows.
- Strong experience with multi-entity setups, reconciliation, and clearing-account logic.
- Ability to explain how Phase 1 should be handled across native configuration, automation, and custom development.
- Ability to provide examples of similar work, a Phase 1 approach, a fixed-fee quote, an expected timeline, a rough implementation estimate after discovery or audit, and confirmation of whether the work will be done personally or delegated.

##### Nice to Have

- Understanding of Amazon accounting flows.
- Understanding of shipping and freight workflow design.
- Understanding of exception handling across finance and operations.
