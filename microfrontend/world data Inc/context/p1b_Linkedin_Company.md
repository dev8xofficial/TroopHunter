# World Data Inc. — LinkedIn Profile

## About

World Data Inc. is a leading Information Technology, Engineering, and Consulting firm that has delivered cutting-edge solutions to small and medium-sized enterprises for over three decades. The team of seasoned engineers boasts expertise across a comprehensive range of IT and network domains, encompassing mobility, cloud computing, blockchain, artificial intelligence, and data analytics. Today, World Data and affiliates lead the creation of Decentralized Value Chains (DVCs) by leveraging its identity and transactional blockchain platforms and wallets. Sample projects include:

- Partnering with academic institutions to deploy student IDs, course certificates, diplomas, and academic IP.
- Partnering with financial institutions to deploy a global financial network that performs tokenized clearing and settlement in real time of P2P decentralized transactions.
- Preparing the issuance of a token to foster community development by leveraging the tokenization of real-world assets.
