# WooCommerce Developer Expert — Job Posting

## Role Summary

The client is building a WooCommerce-based multi-vendor eCommerce platform modeled as an online mall, built on WordPress and WooCommerce. The hired developer is responsible for configuring, customizing, and scaling this marketplace — evaluating and integrating existing plugins where possible, and developing custom solutions when needed — while collaborating in a multi-developer environment to ensure smooth, maintainable delivery.

## Responsibilities

- Configure and manage a WooCommerce-based multi-vendor marketplace.
- Evaluate, install, and customize existing plugins to maximize functionality with minimal custom development.
- Develop custom plugins, extensions, or adjustments when existing plugins do not meet business requirements.
- Integrate and configure marketplace features including vendor dashboards, commissions, payments, shipping, and product management.
- Ensure platform performance, security, scalability, and adherence to best practices.
- Collaborate with developers, designers, and stakeholders to coordinate tasks and ensure smooth delivery.
- Provide technical documentation and basic guidance for ongoing maintenance.

## Requirements

##### Must Have

- Strong hands-on experience with WooCommerce and WordPress development.
- Proven experience with marketplace/multi-vendor plugins.
- Comfortable working in a collaborative, multi-developer environment.

##### Nice to Have

- Experience with multi-vendor platform configuration at scale.
- Familiarity with vendor dashboard and commission management systems.
