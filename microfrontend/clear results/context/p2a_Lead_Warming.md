## Lead Warming Plan — Stuart Trier / Clear Results Corp.

### Analysis

#### Target CEO — Key Themes
- Constraint-first thinking: "identify the constraint" and fix the real bottleneck before chasing more activity.
- Profit over vanity revenue: margin, gross profit, and cash visibility matter more than top-line growth.
- Systems over hustle: most businesses are not broken by complexity, but by ignored operational friction.
- Visibility over gut feel: weekly numbers, scorecards, and usable data are what let owners lead instead of guess.
- Simplicity and decisive focus: owners get overwhelmed when they fail to choose what not to do.
- Owner independence: the end goal is a business that runs with less founder involvement and stronger team accountability.

#### Target CEO — Likely Pain Points
- Scaling a founder-led advisory business without losing the plain-spoken, hands-on credibility that wins clients.
- Turning messy client data across tools like Jobber, QuickBooks, or other field-service systems into clean operational visibility.
- Converting strong thought-leadership content into qualified opportunities without attracting poor-fit "just need more leads" prospects.
- Productizing scorecards, SOPs, and implementation workflows so delivery stays consistent as client volume grows.
- Expanding implementation capacity without adding internal complexity or lowering the quality of client execution.

#### Dev8X — Relevant Overlap
- Dev8X builds custom web applications and SaaS products, which overlaps directly with Clear Results' need for usable systems, dashboards, and operational tooling.
- Dev8X works on workflow-heavy software with APIs and integrations, relevant to Clear Results' world of Jobber, ServiceTitan, JobTread, and fragmented operational data.
- Dev8X's product-minded engineering approach fits Clear Results' belief that better execution comes from better systems, not more effort.
- Dev8X can support internal tools, client-facing portals, or visibility layers that turn scorecards, diagnostics, and SOP-heavy processes into something teams actually use.
- Abdul can speak credibly about scaling through systems, because Dev8X sells technical delivery capacity and process clarity rather than generic marketing promises.

#### Shared Ground
The strongest overlap is systems thinking. Stuart helps owners remove operational guesswork inside home service businesses, while Abdul builds the software, workflows, and visibility layers that make that kind of clarity possible.
