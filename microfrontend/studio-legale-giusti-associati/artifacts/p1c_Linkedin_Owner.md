# Ray Johnson — LinkedIn Profile

## Summary

USA Baseball Southeast NTIS State Sectional Coordinator. Associate Scout for the San Diego Padres. Former program activity leader for the City of High Point — task was to create programs and initiatives for the youth, mostly sports related, whether its after school programs or a 1 day program.

Owner of USAPB. We host pickleball tournaments leagues and more. Started in July of 2023, in just 6 months we have grown our participation rate by 230%. We now avg around 99 participants per tournament we host. Pickleball is the fastest growing sport in the country.

Strong recruitment tendencies in baseball and in business. Built 2 businesses from the ground up with $0 funding via marketing. Great tendencies in negotiations, marketing and sales. Built 3 social media pages from the ground up — one of the pages, Padres scout team, has over 55k followers on Instagram. I showcase great relationships with prospects. My job for USA Baseball is to scout the best talent in the Northeast and Southeast for the NTIS. Great at community relationships. I love going to communities working with youth on showing them the love of baseball.

Owner of media company Xclusive Sports LLC. My job was to sell b2b ad space to companies to our website. I have a non-profit baseball organization Carolina Elite Baseball which helps kids that can't afford to play in the same tournaments as their counterparts. We are one of the few organizations in the Carolina's that compete on both local and national levels.
