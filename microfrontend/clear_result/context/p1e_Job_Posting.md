# Jobber CRM Expert: Audit, Optimization, & Scalability Overhaul — Job Posting

## Role Summary

A home service business is seeking a Jobber Power User / Systems Architect to take their existing Jobber account from an organically built, inefficiency-prone setup to a scalable, high-performance operational engine. The account has accumulated messy data, redundant configurations, and underutilized features over time. The hired contractor will audit the full system, clean it up, and build a streamlined workflow that field techs and office staff can run without friction. The primary outcome is a documented, optimized Jobber environment with a handoff SOP so the system stays clean after engagement ends.

## Responsibilities

- Audit the existing client list, quote-to-invoice workflow, and custom fields for inefficiencies and redundancies.
- Remove duplicate records, fix broken automations, and standardize data entry conventions.
- Build a scalable process covering lead intake, scheduling, dispatching, and automated follow-ups.
- Maximize usage of Jobber's full feature suite including Online Booking, Client Hub, and Two-way Texting.
- Verify that QuickBooks/Xero and marketing integrations are syncing correctly (optional scope).
- Deliver a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) document or video walkthrough so the team can maintain the system independently.

## Requirements

##### Must Have

- Proven track record setting up or managing Jobber for service-based businesses (e.g. Electrical, HVAC, Landscaping, or similar trades).
- Strong data integrity mindset — demonstrated ability to build and maintain clean CRM/field service databases.
- Ability to explain technical configurations in plain, non-technical language for field and office staff.
- Knowledge of Jobber shortcuts and efficiency techniques that benefit both field techs and office staff.
- Proposal must begin with the word "SYSTEMS" confirming the applicant has read the posting in full.
- Proposal must describe a recent Jobber project and identify one common mistake people make when setting up the platform.

##### Nice to Have

- Experience integrating Jobber with QuickBooks or Xero.
- Familiarity with marketing platform integrations connected to Jobber.
