#### About us

For over 15 years, Fitness Superstore has provided professional-grade fitness equipment, installation, and warranty service to gyms, schools, government facilities, corporate wellness programs, hotels, and home customers nationwide. Founded by Tim French, who has been in the industry since 2003, Fitness Superstore operates from 63,500 sq ft of California facilities, combining design, quality control, remanufacturing, logistics, and nationwide distribution under one roof. We offer new commercial equipment (exclusive French Fitness lines) and remanufactured cardio and strength machines from leading brands, delivering sales, trade-ins, installation, and long-term service support through an experienced U.S.-based team. Customers choose Fitness Superstore for value, reliability, and complete lifecycle service, from equipment selection and facility layout to delivery, setup, and ongoing warranty coverage. What We Offer New commercial & home fitness equipment (French Fitness) Remanufactured cardio & strength (Life Fitness, Precor, Technogym & more) Full commercial gym design, layout & project management Trade-in programs and fleet upgrades U.S. warranty & service support Warranties French Fitness (new): Lifetime Frame, 10-Year Parts (select lines), 1-Year Labor Remanufactured: 1-Year Parts & Labor (extensions available) Fitness Superstore is the exclusive manufacturer and distributor of French Fitness commercial equipment, designed & quality-controlled in California for elite performance and long-term value. #CommercialFitnessEquipment #GymDesign #BeniciaCalifornia #FrenchFitness #FitnessSuperstore
