# easyGroup — LinkedIn Profile

## About

easyGroup is the UK company owned by Sir Stelios Haji-Ioannou, the creator and owner of the easy® family of brands. Companies trading under the easy family of brands join one of Europe's most recognised brands — alongside easyJet, easyHotel, easyGym, and many more.

This is the result of the strategic decision made during the easyJet flotation that retained ownership of the 'easy' brand in Sir Stelios's private investment vehicle, easyGroup, which now enjoys a steady and predictable income from licensing the easy brand in return for a royalty based on turnover.

easyGroup has gone on to grow successful businesses in different sectors, including aviation, financial services, health and fitness, and travel & tourism.
