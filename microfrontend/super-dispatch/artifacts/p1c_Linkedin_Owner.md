It’s official…
After 10 months of serving as an Advisor to Super Dispatch, I’m thrilled to announce that I will be the company's next CEO.
When I joined as an Advisor, my intention was to simply be an Advisor and to support the leadership team...but after working alongside our founder Bek Abdullayev and the incredible team at Super Dispatch, meeting our customers at industry events like Used Car Week, and truly understanding the impact we have on the auto transport community, I wanted to get back into company-building mode.
For those that don’t know, Super Dispatch powers the everyday experiences of Shippers, Brokers, and Carriers. We want to make Auto Transport Easy.
Here are couple areas of focus for us…
- For Shippers and Brokers, we’re committed to building products that allow our customers to move cars faster, at the right price, with the trusted carrier (or broker) they want.
- For Carriers, we’re focused on improving the load management experience and evolving into a comprehensive operating system that solves a carrier's day-to-day challenges, from faster payments to pricing insights...and so much more.
Thank you to Bek, the Board, and the amazing team for entrusting me with this opportunity.
Let’s go!
