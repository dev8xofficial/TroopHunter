#### About us

TotaLand.com is an online database application designed specifically for professionals in the landman industry. It streamlines daily tasks by enabling landmen to efficiently manage tracts, contracts, parties, instruments, wells, and units, while also maintaining notes and statuses. The platform facilitates the generation of contracts, maps, and reports, while ensuring the security of uploaded files such as chain of title documents and scanned instruments. Collaboration is made easy as all authorized users can view, download, and comment on project progress. Furthermore, the entire project can be downloaded for further processing by the client, saving a substantial amount of paperwork and allowing landmen to focus on their core competencies: research, negotiation, and deal closing. While not a brokerage itself, TotaLand.com empowers brokerages to deliver exceptional service to their clients by providing cutting-edge technology. Additionally, it assists oil and gas, seismic, and pipeline companies in managing large volumes of data efficiently, thus reducing costs. The platform also supports clients in capturing and reporting on land rights acquired through traditional methods. Moreover, TotaLand.com offers supplementary services such as data entry, mapping assistance, and access to database consultants who specialize in seamlessly importing data from various sources, including spreadsheets.
