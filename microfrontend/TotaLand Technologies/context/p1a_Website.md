## 5 — Features

url: https://www.totaland.com/features
type: product
meta: none

### Core Platform Capabilities

- Simple to use — everything is in one place and organized for easy access.
- Simple to access — work anywhere with desktop, tablet, laptop, or smartphone.
- Reporting — summary and detail data, filtering and sorting options, and numerous export options.
- Executable Documents — such as leases, rights-of-way, and seismic permits.
- Payment Documents — such as drafts, orders for payment, and check requests.
- Purchase Reports — LPRs and reports for a variety of document types.
- Custom Output Libraries — reports, lease and payment forms, additional paragraph language, and provision tracking.
- Simple to deploy — no installation required; rich library of video tutorials accessible anytime.
- World Class Support — support team knows field land work; located in the USA.

### Revolutionary Patent-Pending Architecture

Eliminates the negative aspects of both tract-based and lease-based systems using a unique, patent-pending table structure that removes the need for repeated data entry. Allows users to quickly find and access only the information they need.

### Abstract Assembler

Patent-pending tool that takes instrument records within a project and compiles them into a secure PDF for printing, binding, or forwarding to a title attorney. The completed abstract includes a cover sheet with brokerage logo, caption page, table of contents, and auto-numbered index. Miscellaneous images such as disclaimers and tax certificates are also included.

### Broker and Client Billing

Brokers can complete daily billing items, attach scans of receipts, and submit invoices online. Broker billing data can optionally be processed into client invoices. Reduces slow processing, unnecessary overhead, and missed billing opportunities.

### Contract Generator

Contracts (OGMLs, seismic permits, pipeline rights-of-way, etc.) are linked to tracts, parties, instruments, units, and wells. After data entry and linking, a PDF document is generated in one click for printing, execution, and sharing. LPRs and other contract reports are generated instantly from the same data.

### Data-Driven Online GIS Mapping

Maps are linked to project data; tract colors and hatches update automatically based on tract or object status. Users can access information about anything attached to a tract — parties, contracts, images, statistics — all through the online map. Multiple dynamic layers are each symbolized with layer-relevant information. Polygons, once built and linked, never need to be rebuilt unless their shape changes. TotaLand personnel can build polygons on request, or clients can manage their own via secure FTP.

### First Class Training and Support

- Video tutorials
- Interactive user guide
- Page-related and topic-related help resources
- Toll-free phone support
- Support ticket system

### Additional Features

- **GIS Plats On Demand** — Generate data-specific plat maps linked directly to the GIS server; zoom, center, and highlight tracts on demand. Exhibit plats and contract packet plats can be managed and delivered through the website.
- **No Per-User Costs** — Flat licensing eliminates unpredictable per-seat charges.
- **Secure and Reliable** — Data encryption, automatic backups, physical security, redundant power, and 60+ million hits without a security lapse or data loss incident.
- **Discretion** — Client identities are never revealed without specific authorization. TotaLand does not perform brokerage services, eliminating conflicts of interest.
- **Experience** — Personnel know land industry language and requirements across a wide variety of project types.
