## Summary

In 1992, I founded Clarity Consultants with a simple mission: to help organizations find the specific expertise they need to succeed. Over the last three decades, the Learning and Development landscape has evolved significantly, but my commitment to holding the line on quality has never wavered.

My approach is rooted in partnership. I believe in being a strategic, transparent, and invested ally for the leaders we serve. At Clarity, we've developed a unique adaptive enterprise model that provides the agility of a boutique with the reliability needed to support global, complex organizations.

We focus on offering flexible, easy-to-adopt, L&D services that scale from project-based engagement to turnkey managed learning services. I am incredibly proud of our 95% project success rate and the fact that we have become a trusted partner to 70% of the Fortune 2000.
