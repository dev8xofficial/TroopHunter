# Carini Group — LinkedIn Profile

## About
Carini Group is the leading full-service boutique real estate brokerage in NYC, founded on the principles of excellence in customer service, competence, and relentlessness. The firm has assisted hundreds of customers in buying, selling, and renting their homes. Listening to clients and delivering results based on their needs has enabled the building of long-term relationships — clients become friends and friends become clients. 50% of business is domestic and 50% international; the firm also serves as wealth advisor for investors managing real estate portfolios, maximizing returns. Carini Group project manages and works in team with leading banks, attorneys, title companies, architects, general contractors, photographers, accountants, management companies, insurance companies, and press.

Services: Seller's Representation, Buyer's Representation, Landlord's Representation to Lease Properties, Tenant's Representation, New Developments Advisory, Property Management, Investment Advisory, 1031 Exchanges, Market Research/Comparable Reporting, Staging, Construction/Renovation Management, Media/Photo/Video/Press Content Creation.
