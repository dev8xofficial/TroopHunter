# Enterprise Health Architect: Scaling "Pie Health" Home Health & Hospice Workflow — Job Posting

## Role Summary

The client is launching a tech-enabled medical credentialing and payer enrollment agency modeled after piehealthusa.com, serving the entire state of Texas. The agency services both established home health and hospice agencies seeking to expand their commercial/Medicaid footprint and organizations navigating corporate ownership transitions (CHOWs). Due to the May 2026 CMS enrollment moratorium, the immediate 6-month product focus is on optimizing established operational entities — managing staff rosters, processing state re-validations, and enrolling them into commercial/Medicaid networks across all 13 Texas Medicaid Service Areas. The Systems Architect will configure the Healthie Enterprise platform and integrate backend automation tooling to replicate Pie Health's transparency and real-time milestone-tracking model.

## Responsibilities

- Configure Healthie to segment agency branches across Texas territories: Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston-Harris, San Antonio-Bexar, and El Paso.
- Integrate specialized compliance workflows mapping to the June 12, 2026 TMHP IAMOnline + Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) cutover, including tracking of $750 institutional application fee configurations.
- Connect backend data to an automation engine (Assured, MedTrainer, or Andros) to route profiles to TMHP PEMS and major private insurers (BCBSTX, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, and regional MCOs including El Paso Health and Superior).
- Build automated portal notifications alerting clients 60/30/15 days before expiration of any Texas HHSC TULIP license, clinical nursing registry check, or commercial malpractice policy.

## Requirements

##### Must Have

- Proven track record building enterprise-level, multi-location workflows inside Healthie.
- Hands-on experience integrating third-party credentialing APIs or roster management tools.
- Clear working knowledge of Texas-specific portals: TMHP PEMS, TULIP, and Availity.
- Proposal must open with the phrase **"Automated Texas Lone Star Engine Ready"** and include a prior multi-location health platform example.
