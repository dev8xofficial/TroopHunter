### Our Treatments

Experience unparalleled skincare services that combine innovation and expertise. Our team of professionals is dedicated to crafting personalised treatments that enhance your natural beauty.

#### LHALA Peel

price: $250 / session

A luxury 90-minute glow treatment that gently resurfaces your skin for a smoother, clearer, and more radiant look. Think of it as a deep reset for your skin — refining pores, softening texture, and boosting glow without harsh downtime. Perfect for targeting dullness, dehydration, and uneven texture, or anyone wanting that fresh, glass-skin finish.

#### Skin Needling

price: Starting from $350 / session

Skin needling is a rejuvenating treatment that encourages your skin to repair and renew itself in the most natural way. By creating tiny, controlled micro-channels on the surface, the treatment triggers your skin's own collagen and elastin production.

Targets concerns such as acne scars, fine lines, pigmentation, uneven texture, and dullness. With minimal downtime and long-lasting results, skin needling gradually strengthens the skin from within, leaving it looking clearer, smoother, and more radiant with each session.

#### ASCE+ Exosomes

price: Starting from $900 / session

ASCE+ Exosomes is a cutting-edge mesotherapy treatment that delivers potent growth factors and stem-cell-derived exosomes directly into the skin, stimulating natural regeneration from within. This advanced therapy targets fine lines, pigmentation, dullness, and loss of elasticity, helping to restore a youthful, luminous complexion. The result is smoother, firmer, and radiant skin with a revitalised glow that looks naturally luminous and refreshed.
