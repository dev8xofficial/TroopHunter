# Operating Discipline — Process Rules (not prompt logic)

These are operational rules for running the paid pipeline. They are NOT encoded in
the prompts because the prompts generate *deliverables*; these govern *how a human
runs the funnel*. Treat them as standing policy.

---

## 1. Channel & Automation Discipline (LinkedIn)

The first video is sent as "the first DM after a connection is accepted." Acceptance
is NOT consent to be pitched. An immediate video pitch on connect is a recognisable
spam pattern and risks account restriction.

- **Warm before the video.** Run the Phase 2 lead-warming (p2a) comment sequence
  first. The video follows genuine engagement, not the instant a connection lands.
- **Never automate the outreach.** Auto-sent connection notes + auto-DMs are what
  trigger LinkedIn restrictions. The wrapper message and the video send are manual,
  per-prospect, every time.
- **One silent-watcher follow-up maximum** (per p3b). Beyond that, stop — repeated
  unsolicited DMs read as pressure and burn the relationship and the account.
- **Pace the volume.** This is a low-volume, high-personalisation channel. Treat
  daily connection/DM limits conservatively; bursts look automated.

## 2. Funnel Instrumentation (a profit-per-stage model needs metrics)

"Revenue from every stage" is unmanageable without per-step numbers. Track, per
prospect and in aggregate:

| Step | Metric | Why it matters |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Wrapper sent → video opened | **Watch-start rate** | Tests the wrapper (Part 0.1). Low = fix the hook, not the script |
| Video opened → watched ≥50% | **Watch-through rate** | Tests the script + captions + mobile legibility |
| Watched → replied | **Reply rate** | Tests Beat 5 + the silent-watcher touch |
| Replied → answered Message 1 | **Dialogue rate** | Tests Message 1 |
| Offer made → consultation paid | **Paid-conversion rate** | The money step — tests Message 2, fee, guarantee, legitimacy |
| Diagnosis corrected / rejected | **Correction rate** | High = upstream research (Phase 0/1) is too thin; tighten the QA gate |

Diagnose where the funnel leaks before "improving" anything — the biggest assumed
leaks are watch-start (wrapper) and reply (Beat 5). Fix the measured leak, not the
guessed one.

---

## 3. The Abdul Bottleneck (the scaling constraint to watch)

The system's front half — automated research → Decision Card → Problem Register —
**scales**. The back half — a bespoke video personally recorded by Abdul, then a
live consultation Abdul runs — **does not**. As volume grows, Abdul is the ceiling,
and the release valve is usually video quality.

Decide deliberately, before volume forces it:
- **Cap personalised first videos** at a sustainable number per week; reserve them
  for prospects who clear the QA gate strongly (best research = best conversion).
- **Templatise the wrapper + structure, never the substance.** The format can be
  reusable; the specific problems/numbers must stay bespoke or the whole edge dies.
- **Watch the quality release valve.** If videos start feeling generic to protect
  Abdul's time, conversion will fall faster than volume rises — the personalisation
  *is* the product.
- **Plan the hand-off question early:** which parts can ever be delegated (research,
  editing, scheduling) vs. which must stay Abdul (the on-camera read, the live
  consultation) because the peer-to-peer, founder-to-founder trust depends on it.

---

## 4. Guarantee Operating Rules (referenced by p3b Message 2)

The Message 2 guarantee ("if it's not worth more than the fee, you don't pay") only
works if its terms are unambiguous in practice:
- **The client judges "worth it."** Do not argue it. Honouring a rare refund cheaply
  protects the reputation the whole system runs on.
- **The documented diagnosis is delivered and kept regardless of payment.** It was
  real work; withholding it on a refund contradicts the "R&D, not a sales call" frame.
- **Track refund rate.** A spike means the diagnosis isn't landing — a research/QA
  problem upstream, not a guarantee problem.
