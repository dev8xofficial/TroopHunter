## System Overview

Dev8X operates two parallel sales pipelines that share a common research
foundation but diverge in approach, pacing, and psychology.

```
                    ┌──────────────────────────────┐
                    │   SHARED RESEARCH FOUNDATION  │
                    │   Phase 0 + Phase 1 prompts   │
                    └────────────┬─────────────────┘
                                 │
                 ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
                 ▼                               ▼
    ┌────────────────────────┐     ┌────────────────────────┐
    │  CONSULTANCY PIPELINE  │     │  WEB SERVICES PIPELINE │
    │   (Network Prospects)  │     │   (Upwork Prospects)   │
    │                        │     │                        │
    │  You CREATE awareness  │     │  You WIN competition   │
    │  Slow, trust-first     │     │  Fast, proof-first     │
    └────────────────────────┘     └────────────────────────┘
```

### The Operating Model

```
AI System handles:                Abdul handles:
─────────────────                 ────────────────
• Research extraction             • Recording personalized videos
• Decision Cards                  • Live consultations
• Problem Registers               • Live demos
• Lead qualification              • Relationship conversations
• Deliverable generation          • Closing deals
• Follow-up drafting              • On-camera presence
• Objection response drafting     • Anything requiring human trust
```

**Rule:** If a CEO would feel sold to by a machine, Abdul does it.
If a task is research, analysis, or drafting, the AI system does it.

## Pipeline A: Consultancy Pipeline (Network Prospects)

**Who enters:** People from your network, LinkedIn connections, referrals —
anyone who has NOT posted a job but who you've identified as having operational
problems.

**Core psychology:** They don't know they need you. You CREATE the awareness.

**Emotional journey:**

```
Unaware → Curious → Concerned → Trusting → Committed → Paying
```

### Stage Map

| # | Stage | Type | What Happens | Deliverables | Status |
|---|-------|------|-------------|--------------|--------|
| 0 | Intelligence | Internal | Research the prospect. Decision Card + Problem Register + Lead Qualification. | Intelligence Package (internal) | ✅ Exists |
| 1 | Warming | Free | 2-3 weeks of LinkedIn engagement. Genuine comments. Build familiarity. | Comment sequence (p2a) | ✅ Exists |
| 2 | 🔥 First Video | Free | Personalized video proving you understand their problem. DM + video. | Brief (p3a) + Video (p3b) + DM wrapper | ✅ Exists |
| 3 | 🔥 Messaging | Free | Reply handling, objection responses, every scenario covered. | Follow-up sequence + War-game responses | 🔥 BUILD NOW |
| 4 | 🔥 Consultation | **PAID** | Live 45-60 min diagnosis. Capture real numbers. Leave-behind document. | Playbook (p3c) + Operational Diagnosis | ✅ Exists |
| 5 | Demo | **PAID** | Working prototype built around their operations. | Demo portals (p4d) + Pitch (p4e) | Future |
| 6 | Proposal | **PAID** | Full proposal + pitch video + blueprint. | Proposal suite (p5a-c) | Future |
| 7 | Build + Expand | **PAID** | Development, delivery, training, expansion discovery. | Project deliverables | Future |

### The Hold-Back Chain

Each stage proves depth and withholds the next stage's payload:

| Stage | SHOWS | WITHHOLDS |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| First Video | The problem, the cost, the research transparency | Solutions, ROI, build plan, price |
| Messaging | Guarantee, stakes number, proof of competence | Full diagnosis, working solution |
| Consultation | Full diagnosis, real numbers, solution DIRECTION | Working software, build cost |
| Demo | Working prototype, how it solves their problems | Build price, timeline, team |
| Proposal | Scope, timeline, pricing, deliverables, safety case | The build itself |

### The Two-Step Money Rule

Money is NEVER asked for inside a deliverable. The video ends on a reply ask.
The consultation offer comes in the follow-up AFTER dialogue exists. The demo
is offered AFTER the consultation delivers value. Each paid gate is earned, not
demanded.

## Pipeline B: Web Services Pipeline (Upwork Prospects)

**Who enters:** People who posted a job on Upwork or similar platforms.

**Core psychology:** They already know they need help. You must WIN against
competitors.

**Emotional journey:**

```
Comparing → Impressed → Convinced → Trusting → Committed → Paying
```

### Stage Map

| # | Stage | Type | What Happens | Deliverables | Status |
|---|-------|------|-------------|--------------|--------|
| 0 | Intelligence | Internal | Research posting + company + poster. Compressed — speed matters. | Targeted Intelligence Package | Future |
| 1 | Proposal Response | Free | Personalized Upwork proposal + mini research video. Stand out from 50. | Upwork proposal (w1a) + mini video | Future |
| 2 | Discovery | Free | Use the interview to demonstrate depth. Present unexpected findings. | Interview prep (w1b) | Future |
| 3 | Scoping | **PAID** | Paid scoping session. Documented scope + architecture. | Scoping deliverable (w2a) | Future |
| 4 | Build + Expand | **PAID** | Development + delivery + move them to Consultancy Pipeline. | Project + pipeline transfer (w2b) | Future |

### Key Differences from Consultancy Pipeline

| Dimension | Consultancy | Web Services |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Warming period | 2-3 weeks | None — speed wins |
| First touch | Problem video (create awareness) | Solution preview (outperform) |
| Research depth | Very deep | Targeted to their ask |
| Tone | Diagnostic, curiosity-driven | Competence, execution-focused |
| Urgency source | You create it (Regret Gap) | It exists (they posted the job) |
| Competition | None | 20-50 other proposals |

## The War-Game Matrix — Every Scenario, Every Response

> 🔥 **IMMEDIATE BUILD SCOPE** — these scenarios are the messaging system.

### After the First Video

| Scenario | Response | Timing |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Watches + replies positively | Message 1: qualitative question, no pitch | Immediate |
| Watches + corrects diagnosis | Thank, absorb, recompute. Bridge to consultation. | Immediate |
| Watches + no reply | Silent-Watcher: one new insight + low reply bar | 4-6 days |
| Doesn't watch | Second wrapper: different angle, shorter | 5-7 days |
| "Not interested" | Graceful exit + door open | Immediate |
| "Too busy" | Acknowledge + offer to revisit | Immediate |
| "What's the cost?" | Reframe: video is free. If they mean consultation, bridge to Message 2. | Immediate |
| "Send more info" | Send brief + one-paragraph summary | Immediate |
| "Already have a developer" | "Not about replacing — sharing research" | Immediate |

### After Consultation Offer (Message 2)

| Scenario | Response | Timing |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Agrees | Schedule + payment details | Immediate |
| "Too expensive" | Anchor against stakes + guarantee | Immediate |
| Wants free call | Hold line + offer 15-min fit check if insistent | Immediate |
| "Let me think" | Restate stakes per month + guarantee | Immediate |
| Goes silent | Message 3: nudge + guarantee + easy out | 3-4 days |
| Asks for references | Provide one real result | Immediate |
| "What do I get?" | Describe the leave-behind | Immediate |

### After Consultation

| Scenario | Response | Timing |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Wants the demo | Schedule + send Operational Diagnosis | Same day |
| "Need to discuss with partner" | Send Internal Briefing Kit | Same day |
| "Not right now" | "Diagnosis is yours. We pick up where we left off." | End of call |
| Ghosts after | "Making sure the diagnosis arrived." | 5-7 days |
| Questions findings | "Tell me which part doesn't match." Revise if warranted. | Immediate |

### After Demo (Future)

| Scenario | Response | Timing |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Ready to proceed | Send proposal within 48 hours | 24-48 hrs |
| Impressed but hesitant | "What would need to be true for this to be a yes?" | End of demo |
| "Can we start smaller?" | Phase 1-only proposal | Immediate |
| Price shock | Anchor against stakes. Break into phases. | Immediate |
| Chooses competitor | "If it doesn't work out, research is still valid." | Immediate |

### After Proposal (Future)

| Scenario | Response | Timing |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Accepts | Welcome email + onboarding | Same day |
| Negotiates price | Remove scope, never discount without trade-off | Immediate |
| Negotiates timeline | Honest compression with stated risks | Immediate |
| Cash flow constraint | Milestone-based payments | Immediate |
| Goes dark | "Did the proposal raise any questions?" | 5-7 days |

## Operating Rules (from OPERATING_DISCIPLINE.md)

1. **Warm before the video.** Never send the video as the first DM.
2. **Never automate outreach.** Manual, per-prospect, every time.
3. **One silent-watcher follow-up maximum.** Beyond that, stop.
4. **Pace the volume.** Low-volume, high-personalization.
5. **Track funnel metrics.** Watch-start → watch-through → reply → dialogue → paid.
6. **Guard Abdul's time.** The AI scales; Abdul does not.
7. **Honour the guarantee.** The client judges "worth it." Never argue.
8. **Track refund rate.** A spike = upstream research problem.

## Lead Qualification Rubric (Directional — p0c Will Formalize)

Before Abdul invests time in a prospect, score them on these dimensions:

| Dimension | Strong Signal (3) | Medium Signal (2) | Weak Signal (1) |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Problem severity** | Clear, costly, documented bottleneck | Probable inefficiency, not yet measured | Vague, generic, or assumed problem |
| **Company size / revenue** | Established business, multiple employees, revenue visible | Growing business, some traction | Very early stage or hobby business |
| **Decision-maker access** | You're talking to the CEO/owner | CEO is reachable via one hop | No path to decision-maker |
| **Timing signals** | Hiring, expanding, just raised money, posted a job | Active on LinkedIn, talking about growth | Dormant, no recent activity |
| **Budget indicators** | Has spent on similar services, runs paid tools | Uses some paid infrastructure | DIY everything, no tech spend visible |
| **Tech fit** | Problem is clearly solvable with web/automation | Probably solvable, needs validation | Problem is non-tech (hiring, management) |

**Scoring:**
- **15-18:** GO — high-priority prospect, invest fully
- **10-14:** QUALIFIED — proceed with video, watch for signals
- **6-9:** DEFER — not worth Abdul's video time yet; nurture passively
- **Below 6:** NO-GO — don't invest

## Cross-Pipeline Movement Rules

Prospects can move between pipelines as their situation changes:

| Movement | Trigger | What Happens |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Consultancy → Web Services** | Network prospect posts a job on Upwork | Fast-track the Web Services approach; you already have deep research |
| **Web Services → Consultancy** | Upwork client completes a project | Shift to long-term relationship mode; identify next automation opportunity |
| **Any → Nurture** | Prospect exits at any stage | 90-day re-engagement queue |
| **Nurture → Active** | Prospect re-engages (replies to nurture, posts about a problem, or contacts Dev8X) | Restart at the appropriate stage with fresh research layered on the original |

**Key rule:** When a prospect moves between pipelines, CARRY the intelligence
forward. Never re-research from scratch — the original Decision Card and
Problem Register are the baseline.

## Additional Entry Points

### Referral Entry

When an existing client refers someone:

| Element | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Psychology** | Trust is pre-built. The referrer's credibility transfers. |
| **Warming** | Skip — they already know about you through the referrer |
| **First touch** | Acknowledge the referral. Do abbreviated research. Send a shorter, warmer video. |
| **Tone** | "I worked with [referrer], and they mentioned you might be dealing with [problem]. I took a look..." |
| **Speed** | Faster pipeline — trust baseline is higher, warming is unnecessary |
| **Pipeline** | Enter Consultancy Pipeline at Stage 2 (First Video) |

### Inbound Entry

When someone contacts Dev8X proactively (through website, LinkedIn, email):

| Element | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Psychology** | They came to you. Intent is high. Trust is self-generated. |
| **Warming** | Skip entirely |
| **First touch** | Respond within 24 hours. Ask what prompted their outreach. |
| **Research** | Run abbreviated Phase 0 + Phase 1 AFTER the first conversation |
| **Next step** | If the problem is clear: offer the consultation directly (skip the video — they don't need awareness building) |
| **Pipeline** | Enter Consultancy Pipeline at Stage 3 (Dialogue) or Stage 4 (Consultation) depending on readiness |

## Re-Engagement Return Protocol

When a prospect comes back after the 90-day (or longer) re-engagement:

| Situation | How to Handle |
|-----------|--------------|
| **They reply to a nurture touchpoint** | Treat as a warm restart. Reference the original research: "We looked at [bottleneck] back in [month]. Has anything changed?" |
| **They contact you proactively** | Fast-track. They remember you. Skip warming. Go directly to updated video or consultation offer. |
| **Their situation changed** (new problems, growth, new hire) | Run a LIGHT refresh on the research. Update the Decision Card. The original is the baseline — don't redo from scratch. |
| **They're ready to pay** | Skip everything between their current position and payment. If they want the consultation, schedule it. Don't force them through stages they've already mentally passed. |

**Key rule:** Never make a returning prospect re-prove their interest.
Meet them where they are, not where the pipeline says they should be.

## Pipeline Health Metrics

What a healthy pipeline looks like:

| Metric | Healthy Range | Warning Sign |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| **Active prospects** | 3-5 at any time | >7 = quality drops; <2 = pipeline drying up |
| **Video watch-start rate** | >40% | <25% = fix the DM wrapper |
| **Video watch-through rate** | >60% of watchers | <40% = fix the script or recording quality |
| **Reply rate** | >15% of watchers | <10% = Beat 5 or wrapper needs work |
| **Message 2 conversion** | >30% of replies | <15% = the offer/guarantee/fee needs adjustment |
| **Consultation satisfaction** | >90% pay willingly | >1 refund in 10 = research quality problem |
| **Diagnosis → demo conversion** | >40% | <25% = consultation isn't landing |
| **Time from first comment to payment** | 3-5 weeks | >8 weeks = pipeline friction somewhere |
| **Abdul hours per prospect** | ~2.5 hours to consultation | >4 hours = process inefficiency |

### Seasonal / Timing Considerations

| Period | Impact | Strategy |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **January** | High intent — new year, new budgets, fresh goals | Good outreach window |
| **Q1 (Jan-Mar)** | Budget cycle start for many businesses | Best conversion period |
| **Summer (Jun-Aug)** | Decision-makers on vacation, slower responses | Continue research; reduce outreach volume |
| **Q4 (Oct-Dec)** | Budget exhaustion or "spend it before we lose it" | Mixed — some rush to spend, others freeze |
| **Holiday weeks** | Low response rates | Pause outreach; resume after |
| **Monday mornings** | Inbox buried | Send videos Tuesday-Thursday |
| **Friday afternoons** | Low attention | Avoid sending anything important |
