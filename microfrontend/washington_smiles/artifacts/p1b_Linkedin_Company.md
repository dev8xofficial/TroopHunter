# Washington Smiles — LinkedIn Profile

## About

We are your local dentist in Washington, MO. We have proudly been caring for the Washington community. We understand that going to the dentist can be a daunting task for lots of people, so we want to make your visit with us as comfortable and enjoyable as possible. When you visit our office for the first time, you will notice that we are very focused on your needs. Your dental health is very important, and we will make sure to listen to all your concerns and questions. We will do our best to take care of your needs and those of your family and friends as well. We strive to offer you and your family the most advanced dental care available. Depending on your needs, we will give you all the options for treatment so you can decide what is best for you.

Last month, I received an online review claiming "insider knowledge" that I buy my team purses.
