#### About us

We are your local dentist in Washington, MO. We have proudly been caring for the Washington community. We understand that going to the dentist can be a daunting task for lots of people, so we want to make your visit with us as comfortable and enjoyable as possible. When you visit our office for the first time, you will notice that we are very focused on your needs. Your dental health is very important, and we will make sure to listen to all your concerns and questions. We will do our best to take care of your needs and those of your family and friends as well. We strive to offer you and your family the most advanced dental care available. Depending on your needs, we will give you all the options for treatment so you can decide what is best for you.

Last month, I received an online review claiming “insider knowledge” that I buy my team purses.

Let me be clear: I absolutely do.

I’ve also bought them sunglasses, jewelry, and treated them to experiences like olive oil and balsamic vinegar tastings. Why? Because they work hard. Really hard.

Every single day, they show up for our patients, create meaningful experiences, support their doctors, and keep everything running smoothly behind the scenes. That kind of dedication doesn’t go unnoticed and it shouldn’t go unrewarded.

I believe in celebrating people who go above and beyond. I believe in showing appreciation in ways that feel personal, fun, and memorable. And I believe that when you take care of your team, they take even better care of the people you serve.

So yes, to anyone raising eyebrows about it, I’m proud to say I spoil my team a little. They’ve earned it.

And here’s us, caught in the act, pretending to steal each other’s purses.

Turns out treating your team well isn’t a scandal. It’s the standard.
