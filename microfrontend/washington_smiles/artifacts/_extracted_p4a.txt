3. Product / Service Breakdown

3.1 Core Services
Dental Cleaning & Exams: Hygienist removes plaque and tartar. Dentist reviews x-rays, checks teeth and gums, and makes treatment recommendations. Recommended every six months.

Dental Fillings (Composite): Tooth-colored resin fillings for cavities. Replaces decayed tooth structure. Nearly invisible compared to metal fillings.

Dental Crowns: Covers and rebuilds a damaged or decayed tooth. Available in gold, all-porcelain, or porcelain-over-metal. Process takes two appointments.

Root Canals: Removes infected pulp from inside a tooth, seals the canal. Saves the natural tooth. May be done in-office or referred to an endodontist.

Tooth Extractions: Removes teeth that cannot be saved or need to come out for orthodontic or health reasons. Includes wisdom teeth.
3.2 Optional / Specialty Services
Dental Implants: Titanium posts placed in the jawbone to replace missing teeth. Require osseointegration (bone bonding) over weeks to months. Performed by Dr. Eric Egan.

Invisalign: Clear plastic aligners that gradually straighten teeth. Starting at $108/month. Includes before-and-after consultations.

Teeth Whitening (The Whitening Bar): In-office (1-3 hours) or take-home bleaching trays. ADA-approved products.

Veneers & Bonding: Porcelain or plastic shells bonded to front teeth. Fix stains, chips, gaps. Bonding is a single visit; veneers require at least two.

Botox: Treats TMJ, bruxism, chronic migraines, and cosmetic facial concerns. 15-30 minute procedure. Performed by trained dentists.

NTI Migraine Device: Custom mouthguard that reduces jaw clenching by 70%, lowering migraine frequency. Custom-fitted via dental impression.

Custom Mouthguards: Athletic and night guards. Superior fit and protection compared to store-bought versions. Group fittings available for sports teams.

Oral Cancer Screenings: Painless visual exam included in routine visits. Checks for red/white patches, sores, and abnormal tissue.

Dentures & Partials: Full or partial replacement teeth. Removable. Includes InPlace overdentures. Conventional and immediate options available.

Sedation & Oral Surgery: IV sedation, nitrous oxide, or oral sedation available. In-office bone grafting, implant placement, and periodontal surgeries.

5.4 Key Operational Workflows
Scheduling
The office manager and scheduling coordinator manage a productive schedule — balancing provider time, procedure mix, and patient flow. The goal is to minimize gaps and last-minute cancellations while ensuring a sustainable pace for the clinical team.
Collections
Washington Smiles is a zero-balance office. Payment is due at the time of service. Payment options are explained before or at the appointment. The front office handles financial conversations clearly and confidently. Daily collections are tracked and reported.
Insurance Processing
Insurance benefits are verified before appointments. The front team submits claims after procedures and follows up on outstanding claims. Patients are informed of their out-of-pocket costs.
Treatment Planning
After each exam, the dentist creates a personalized treatment plan. The front team presents the plan to the patient with cost options, financing alternatives, and scheduling for future phases.
Emergency Protocol
Patients in acute pain during office hours call immediately for a same-day consultation. After hours, patients who have been seen within 7 days page the Doctor on Call. All others are directed to the ER.

14. Summary of Business Operations

Washington Smiles is a full-service dental practice in Washington, Missouri, operating for over 40 years. It is owned by Dr. Melissa A. Smith and is one of five locations within Integrity Dental Group.

The practice earns revenue by treating individual patients across a complete range of dental services — from routine cleanings to complex oral surgery and cosmetic dentistry. All major services are delivered in-house, reducing the need for patient referrals.

Patients find the practice through Google search, social media, community involvement, and word-of-mouth. The website offers new patient discounts and financing options to reduce the barrier to booking. Most patient conversion happens through an online booking tool or by phone.

Once a patient books, the practice delivers a full exam and treatment plan on the first visit. Ongoing care is maintained through twice-yearly recall scheduling, a mobile patient app, a loyalty rewards program, and a monthly membership plan for uninsured patients.

Day-to-day operations are managed by an office manager following a structured playbook covering scheduling, collections, and team management. The clinical team is led by five dentists with specializations in implants, oral surgery, Botox, systemic health, and general dentistry. The practice invests heavily in team training and continuing education.

Washington Smiles is part of a growing dental group that is building shared systems, a leadership playbook, and a culture of professional development. This creates consistent processes across locations and a scalable model for future growth.

For external service providers, the highest-value opportunities are in patient acquisition marketing, insurance billing, technology integrations, HR and staffing, and financing solutions. The practice has the infrastructure and leadership to adopt and benefit from the right partnerships.
