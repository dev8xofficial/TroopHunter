## 13 — Dental Crowns

url: https://www.washmosmiles.com/services/cosmetic-dentistry/crowns/
type: product
meta: none

> CTA: $200 OFF First Visit

A dental crown restores flawed, damaged, or decayed teeth when damage is too extensive for a filling, or when the tooth is badly broken, cracked, or worn down. Crowns rebuild a tooth to its original shape and size rather than extracting it.

### Types of Crowns

- **Gold crowns** — Require the least tooth preparation, preserving the most natural structure. Will not chip and rarely cause wear on adjacent teeth. Highly workable for a secure fit; some patients dislike the appearance.
- **All porcelain / all ceramic crowns** — Most natural-looking; often used on front teeth. May require significant tooth preparation, leaving less existing structure.
- **Porcelain-over-metal crowns** — Balance aesthetics and durability. Natural-looking with a metal base for strength. A thin black line may appear at the gumline due to the underlying metal.

### Crown Procedure

If the tooth is severely damaged, a root canal may be performed first. The tooth is filed down and impressions are taken to model the permanent crown. A temporary crown is worn for approximately two weeks while the lab prepares the permanent one. At the second appointment, the temporary is removed and the permanent crown is cemented. With proper care, crowns last ten to fifteen years or longer.

## 15 — Tooth Removal & Extractions

url: https://www.washmosmiles.com/services/dental-problem-treatments/tooth-removal-extractions/
type: product
meta: none

> CTA: $200 OFF First Visit

Tooth extraction is the safe removal of a tooth by a trained dental professional, sometimes called "pulling teeth." For wisdom teeth, the dentist discusses options and may refer to an oral surgeon if needed.

### Reasons for Extraction

- Severe gum disease (periodontitis)
- Preparation for braces or orthodontic treatment
- Crowding or damage to surrounding teeth
- Impacted tooth (cannot properly emerge through gums)
- Broken tooth that cannot be repaired
- Heavily decayed tooth that cannot be saved

### Wisdom Teeth

Wisdom teeth are the third set of molars at the back of the mouth, typically emerging in the late teens or early twenties. Common reasons for removal include: risk to other teeth, partial eruption leading to plaque and decay, and misaligned eruption causing crowding.

## 27 — Oral Cancer Screenings

url: https://www.washmosmiles.com/services/general-dentistry/oral-cancer-screenings/
type: product
meta: none

> CTA: $200 OFF First Visit

Oral cancer screenings are painless exams. When detected early, oral cancer can be treated effectively.

### What Is Oral Cancer?

Oral cancer involves the rapid and uncontrollable growth of cells that damage the mouth, throat, tonsils, lips, and tongue. It has a high mortality rate if not caught early.

### Signs of Oral Cancer

- Red or white spots or patches anywhere in the mouth
- Trouble swallowing or sore throat
- Pain or difficulty chewing, speaking, or moving the jaw or tongue
- Sores persisting beyond two weeks
- Oral bleeding
- Loose teeth
- Changes in denture fitting or bite
- Pain, tenderness, or numbness in the mouth, lips, or tongue

### Risk Factors and Prevention

Risk factors include family history of cancer, tobacco use, exposure to HPV-16, and excessive alcohol consumption. Risk can be reduced by quitting tobacco, minimizing alcohol, and practicing good oral hygiene. Regular screening is recommended for everyone — not only high-risk individuals.

## 28 — Root Canals

url: https://www.washmosmiles.com/services/dental-problem-treatments/root-canals/
type: product
meta: none

> CTA: $200 OFF First Visit

A root canal saves a damaged or infected tooth by removing infected pulp, cleaning and disinfecting the root canals, and sealing them to prevent further infection. The procedure relieves pain, restores tooth health, and preserves the natural smile. Sedation and local anesthetics are available; most patients experience minimal discomfort.

### Root Canal Procedure

The root canal may be completed in-office or referred to an endodontist. Steps include:

1. An opening is created in the hard surface; a specialized tool removes soft tissue, bacteria, infection, and nerves.
2. The empty spaces and root canals are filled with a special material to prevent future infection. If infection severity warrants, a temporary filling is placed for one week before permanent filling.
3. After treatment, a dental crown may be placed on the tooth for extra support, as treated teeth can be slightly more brittle.

## 31 — Dental Implants

url: https://www.washmosmiles.com/services/cosmetic-dentistry/dental-implants/
type: product
meta: none

> CTA: $200 OFF First Visit | Free Complementary Consultation

### Benefits of Dental Implants

- Prevent bone loss and gum recession by integrating into the bone structure
- Do not require support from surrounding teeth, leaving adjacent teeth unaltered
- Restore clear speech and comfortable eating
- Cared for like natural teeth (brush and floss as usual)
- Preserve natural facial structure, unlike dentures

### Dental Implant Process

Tiny titanium posts are placed in the jawbone where teeth are missing. During osseointegration, bone bonds with the titanium to create a firm foundation. After several weeks of gum healing, the implant crown is fitted to the post. Bone may take up to six months to fully grow around the anchor.

Some implants require a second surgery; in many cases, the anchor and post are placed simultaneously in a single procedure.
