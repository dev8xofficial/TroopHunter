### CROWNS

A dental crown could be the answer if you want to restore flawed, damaged, or decayed teeth!

#### $200 OFF FIRST VISIT

#### WHY USE A DENTAL CROWN?

A crown may be necessary if a tooth is too damaged for a filling, badly broken or cracked, or badly worn down. In the case of a filling, sometimes there is too much damage to the tooth structure to which a filling will not securely adhere. Also, instead of pulling out a damaged tooth, we can use a crown to rebuild it to its original shape and size.

#### TYPES OF CROWNS

Crowns are available in various variations of materials. There are three types commonly used:

- Gold crowns: Compared to other types of crowns, gold requires the least amount of preparation for the existing tooth, allowing us to maintain the most natural tooth structure. Gold crowns will not chip and most likely will not cause wear on other teeth. The gold material is highly workable, allowing a secure fit between the crown and the existing tooth. Some people, however, may not like its distinctive appearance.
- All porcelain or all ceramic crowns: All porcelain or all ceramic crowns look very natural and are often used on the front teeth. Sometimes, the dentist may need to prepare the tooth significantly (which leaves less of the existing tooth), too. However, this is a popular choice for dental crowns.
- Porcelain-over-metal crowns: Porcelain-over-metal crowns balance excellent esthetics and durability. They are natural-looking and are fused to a metal base, allowing an exceptionally durable and strong bond. With this type of crown, however, a thin, black line might appear where the crown meets the gum (because of the metal underneath).

After discussing your options with one of our dentists, you’ll decide which type of crown is best for you!

#### CROWN PROCEDURE

The procedure and steps depend on each patient’s case. A root canal may be performed first if a tooth is highly damaged. Otherwise, we will prepare the tooth by filing it down to ensure a proper crown fit on top of it and between the adjacent teeth.

Impressions are taken and used as a model to create the new crown. The lab typically takes two weeks to prepare the new, permanent crown. In the meantime, you will wear a temporary crown. During your second visit, the temporary crown will be removed, and the new crown will be permanently cemented. With proper care, crowns can last from ten to fifteen years or longer.

### TOOTH REMOVAL & EXTRACTIONS

Tooth extraction is a fairly standard procedure only performed by a trained dental professional.

#### $200 OFF FIRST VISIT

#### EXTRACTION

It is the safe removal of a tooth. This procedure is often performed at our office, so you’re in excellent, caring hands. It is sometimes called “pulling teeth” or “getting a tooth out.”

If you need a wisdom tooth extracted, our dentists will discuss your options to determine the best action. In certain circumstances, we may refer you to an oral surgeon.

#### REASONS FOR EXTRACTION

There are a few reasons someone may need to get a tooth extracted:

- If a patient has a severe gum disease called periodontitis
- If a patient needs a tooth extracted to prepare for braces or orthodontic treatment
- If a tooth is causing crowding issues or damage to surrounding teeth
- If a tooth is impacted (cannot properly emerge through the gums)
- If a tooth has been broken and cannot be repaired
- If a tooth is heavily decayed and cannot be saved

#### WISDOM TEETH

Wisdom teeth are the third set of molars located in the back of the mouth, on the top and bottom. They usually erupt through the gums during the late teenage years or the early twenties.

They are mainly removed so they do not cause problems with the other teeth. Also, if the wisdom teeth only partially erupt through the gums, they may become difficult to clean, leading to plaque accumulation problems and tooth decay. In this circumstance, you may want to remove the wisdom teeth.

Another reason to remove wisdom teeth is if they are not positioned straight and do not erupt straight. This can cause crowding issues with your other teeth.

#### IV VS. TRADITIONAL SEDATION

Understanding the difference between IV and traditional sedation is crucial for patients, as this knowledge empowers them to make well-informed decisions regarding their health and safety.

IV Sedation: Delivered directly into the bloodstream, IV sedation offers rapid onset and precise control over sedation levels, which makes it highly effective for more intensive or lengthy procedures.

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas): Inhaled through a mask, nitrous oxide provides light sedation and wears off quickly. It’s a convenient option for patients with mild anxiety but doesn’t offer the deeper relaxation provided by IV sedation.

Oral Sedation: Often administered as a pill, oral sedation is generally lighter and takes longer to take effect. It’s suitable for less invasive procedures and shorter appointments.

At Washington Smiles, we’ll discuss your sedation options and help you choose the method that best suits your comfort level and procedure requirements.

#### WHAT’S IN THE IV?

The medications used vary based on your distinct health profile and procedure. Still, our experienced team will discuss them with you beforehand to ensure you feel informed and at ease. This sedation involves a carefully controlled blend of medications tailored to meet individual needs, such as:

Sedatives: Medications like midazolam, meperidine, or propofol help relax the body and mind, reducing stress and worry.

Analgesics: These pain relievers guarantee you will experience no discomfort during your procedure.

Anti-nausea Medications: To prevent nausea after surgery and improve your comfort during recovery.

The combination of these medications helps to alleviate anxiety, induce relaxation, and ensure a pain-free experience during dental procedures. We prioritize your comfort and safety, monitoring the sedation process to ensure optimal results.

#### BENEFITS OF IV SEDATION FOR PATIENTS

Utilized for complex or lengthy procedures, IV sedation provides essential benefits for individuals preparing for oral surgery, enhancing the overall experience and ensuring greater comfort.

Flexible Dosing: Because it's administered intravenously, your dentist regulates the level of sedation, adjusting it as needed to ensure comfort without prolonging recovery.

Faster Acting: Unlike oral sedatives, which take time to activate, IV sedation works almost instantly.

Instantaneous Peace: IV sedation induces a deep state of relaxation, reducing anxiety and allowing patients to feel more at ease.

Shorter Rally: IV sedation often allows faster recovery than general anesthesia.

Limited Memory of Procedure: Many patients report little to no recollection of their procedure, helping to reduce anxiety in future appointments.

Overall, IV sedation not only aids in creating a tranquil environment but also contributes to a more efficient and less traumatic procedure for patients.

#### WHY DO TEETH BECOME STAINED OR DISCOLORED?

There are many contributing factors to stained teeth. As we age, minerals penetrate the tooth’s enamel, which causes discoloration. Factors that cause tooth discoloration and staining include:

- Age
- Natural staining and discoloration from foods and beverages
- Tobacco use (cigars, pipes, smokeless tobacco, cigarettes)
- Certain foods and drinks include red wine, dark sodas, tea, coffee, etc.
- Certain medications (tetracycline and some antibiotics)

Depending on your teeth and situation, you may choose in-house whitening, take-home whitening, or both. Whichever option you select, Washington Smiles will use teeth whitening products approved by the American Dental Association to guarantee your safety.

#### HOW DOES TEETH WHITENING WORK?

A bleaching gel (hydrogen and carbamide peroxide) is typically used to whiten teeth professionally. It is applied directly to the tooth’s surface. This gel helps remove stains and makes teeth appear whiter and brighter without harming them.

The professional teeth whitening procedure takes one to three hours, depending on the type of whitening chosen and the severity of the stains. At-home whitening procedures can also be used. Patients who choose this route usually see results within three to four days after the first use, with the final result taking approximately two weeks.

Washington Smiles offers both in-office and take-home whitening options. Depending on certain factors, you may need either one or the other method or both, which our dentists can discuss with you.

#### WHAT ARE DENTURES? WHY DO I NEED THEM?

Custom dentures are removable replacement teeth. They can be either a full or partial set (commonly called “partials”). At Washington Smiles, we offer InPlace™ overdentures. If you don’t have healthy teeth, a complete denture may be the solution, but if you need to replace only a few missing teeth, a partial denture may be proper.

##### REASONS FOR DENTURES

- Your face may sag or develop a sunken look, making you look older than you are.
- Replacing missing teeth may help you chew better.
- Getting dentures helps you speak better.
- Dentures help strengthen your facial muscles.
- Dentures are made from acrylic resin material and are strong and durable.

#### TYPES OF DENTURES

Complete dentures: Complete dentures, also known as full dentures, replace all the teeth in your mouth. They can also be used when a person’s only remaining teeth are unhealthy and must be removed.

Partial dentures: Partials are used when some teeth need to be replaced. The healthy teeth act as strong anchors to which the partial denture firmly attaches with metal attachments.

Conventional dentures: This type of complete denture is made and placed after the remaining teeth have been extracted and the tissue has healed. This may take approximately six weeks to several months. The patient may wear a “temporary” or an immediate denture during this time.

Immediate dentures: This type of denture is placed immediately after the dentist has removed the remaining teeth. They may require more office visits and raise costs for the denture procedure. However, after they are placed, immediate dentures can help reduce the initial swelling after teeth removal. Once healing is finished, the dentures will be relined to ensure proper fit, or they can be replaced entirely with a permanent conventional denture.

#### WHAT IS ORAL CANCER?

Oral cancer is a common type of cancer. The rapid and uncontrollable growth of cells damages the mouth, throat, tonsils, lips, and tongue tissues. Oral cancer is on the rise and has a high mortality rate if not caught and treated early on.

#### WHAT ARE THE SIGNS?

- Red or white spots/patches anywhere in the mouth
- Trouble swallowing or sore throat
- Pain or difficulty when chewing, speaking, or moving the jaw or tongue
- Sores that are still present after two weeks
- Oral bleeding
- Loose teeth
- Changes in denture fitting
- Changes in bite
- Pain, tenderness, or numbness in the mouth, on the lips, or the tongue

#### RISK AND PREVENTION

Several factors can increase an individual’s chances of developing oral cancer. These include a family history of cancer, the use of tobacco products, exposure to the HPV-16 virus, and excessive alcohol consumption. Fortunately, many of the causes of oral cancer are preventable. You can reduce your risk by quitting tobacco use, minimizing or eliminating alcohol consumption, and practicing good oral hygiene.

Good oral health involves not just what you do at home but also regular dentist visits for check-ups and screenings. Anyone can develop oral cancer, so it is vital to stay vigilant and continue regular screenings, even if you do not fall into a high-risk category.

#### BENEFITS OF DENTAL IMPLANTS

Dental Implants provide a multitude of benefits, including:

- Dental implants help to prevent bone loss and gum recession since they integrate into the bone structure.
- Dental implants do not need support from surrounding teeth. Therefore, the surrounding teeth remain unaltered and untouched.
- Dental implants will allow you to speak more clearly and eat more comfortably again.
- Clean and care for your implants like your natural teeth–brush and floss like usual.
- Dental implants are an excellent option for tooth replacement.
- Dental implants preserve the natural facial structure, unlike dentures.

#### DENTAL IMPLANT PROCESS

The implants are tiny titanium posts placed in the jawbone where teeth are missing. During osseointegration, the bone bonds with the titanium, creating a firm foundation for the implant.

After the gums have several weeks to heal, the next step begins– the implants are made and fitted to the post (a portion of the anchor). Surgery must be performed to place the anchor. The bone may take up to six months to fully grow around the anchor.

Some dental implants may require a second surgery, but only one surgery is needed, mainly since the anchor and post are attached and placed simultaneously.

#### INVISALIGN® TREATMENT:
WHAT IS IT? HOW DOES IT WORK?

Crooked or misaligned teeth can be a real hassle, causing discomfort or insecurity. The thought of traditional braces, especially at an older age, is usually not a preferred solution. But with Invisalign clear braces, your teeth could be fixed quicker than you’d think.

Rather than using metal and wire, these modern braces use removable plastic aligners that gently straighten your teeth. The patients receive various-sized custom trays that apply slight resistance to the teeth. This compels them to move over time to give you the smile you’ve always wanted without feeling self-conscious about their appearance.
