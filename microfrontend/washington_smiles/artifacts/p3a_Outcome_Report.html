What's Coming

What this costs in 12–24 months if nothing changes

As offices multiply
Management burden scales with each new office
Opening location #6, #7, #8 demands proportionally more of Dr. Smith's personal time to train, monitor, and support every manager.
→ A central system distributes oversight, so growth doesn't stall at the limit of CEO bandwidth.
On first manager exit
Knowledge walks out when a key manager leaves
A paper playbook is personal — a single departure forces a reactive rebuild from memory, under pressure.
→ Digitized, transferable workflows keep institutional knowledge in the system, not in one person's head.
12–24 months out
Inconsistent experience erodes the brand
Without shared standards, each office drifts; one bad-experience location dents a 21-year reputation built across all of them.
→ Systemized standards keep every location performing to one consistent bar.
