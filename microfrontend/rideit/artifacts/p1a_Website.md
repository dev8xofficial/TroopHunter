## 2 — On Logos

url: https://www.ahumansanctuary.com/post/on-logos
type: article
date: September 20, 2020
author: Julie Anne Steiger
meta: none

Our vision for a human sanctuary has been the creation of a space that acts as a safe haven and stomping grounds for returning back to our true natures in a society that leaves little room for this exploration. We wanted a symbol to represent this. We call it simply — The Logo. What does a logo for this space look like? Is it physical? Emotional? Spiritual? We have had trouble allowing it to naturally express itself.

Originally Julie came up with the name "Heart in Hand Sanctuary," based off a personal childhood experience that lived up to its name. She was in a car accident and as a result of her extensive injuries, held her heart in her hand until help arrived. She came up with the idea of a heart of love held in joining hands as the container for all of the hurt that a human sanctuary accepts, receives, and helps transform. She said she wanted trees so the hands were turned into trees. I wanted something true to development so I threw in the stages of a fetus. There is a flower developing alongside the stages of the fetus as it travels from rhizome to antithesis ending in the last stage of the fetus' hands. Similarly the tree hands hold the container of the heart as a representation of the vessel of life, or the womb. This image was not reaching the right representation of the sanctuary so I moved into the shape of a circle. Everything is contained by the circle and it stands as a symbol of the contents inside it. Working within this container felt more neutral than the one previous with its pointed and shaped edges.

Following this, Julie had a dream of the flickering of a distant star with a seed in its central fore. She mentioned this in connection with the logo. Knowledge is always at a distance before it becomes obtainable. I saw a seed of life embedded in the heart of the star in her description.

During the development of this image came a third image, and for a change of pace I moved into a digital rendition. It being digital loses the timeless appeal I find in freehand work. The digital space is at the mercy of the technological advancements of the times, so this image will age as quickly as the advancements in technology can replace it. This image feels dull to the touch as it does not express the imperfections and nuances of physical creation true in hand drawn work. The software's textures felt organic and fluid which made the process of creation on such a medium easier than expected.

From the first two drawings, we felt better relationship and collaboration was needed, deciding to express this in the manner of masculine and feminine coming together. Holding hands meet in the middle with a sun and rock face for masculine energy, and a moon and flower for the feminine. A rigidness in the rock on the right represents a masculine rigidness to counteract the movement and changing force of the moon. The feminine side is always changing in the cycle of the moon, and offers the gift of life that the masculine does not. The rock was born from the volcanic eruption as a woman in childbirth who brings life into the world. A human being is both masculine and feminine within as in the circle of life and this symbol. One requires the other, is opposed to the other, and also supports the other.

And there it was, the Logo ❤️

As the logo continues to develop we will stick with what feels most comfortable until something new comes along to replace it. The logo or overarching symbol of ourselves and our offerings may always be changing and developing with us as we continue to develop ourselves.

## 4 — Psychotherapy (Service Page)

url: https://www.ahumansanctuary.com/service-page/psychotherapy

A safe therapeutic space to receive support in your healing journey. All Psychotherapy services are currently provided by Julie Anne Steiger, LCSW, SEP.

### About Therapy with Julie Anne

I provide a safe, nonjudgmental atmosphere where clients can connect to their inner compass and work toward a more fulfilling and purposeful life. Embracing a collaborative, strengths-based perspective, I draw from various therapeutic approaches tailored to the values, needs, and goals of the individual. In session, clients can expect to be met with utmost compassion and understanding. I do not see clients as a diagnosis, a set of symptoms, or a case to be "solved" but a growing, evolving, and intricately developing human who will only blossom when met in a warm and trusting atmosphere. Although we will explore and process areas that are causing pain and discomfort, sessions will be paced with concrete practices that immediately increase feelings of wellness, leaving clients not only with more capacity for daily life but also possessing new coping skills to carry with them for life. This emphasis on creating more stability and empowerment for the client provides the foundation needed for deep and lasting change.

The relationship that we create in our sessions is vital. Much of the wounding we experience in life is relational and it can be particularly intimidating to be opening up to someone new. I appreciate the courage it takes to be seen and the willingness to share in vulnerability. I want to assure prospective clients that a safe container is created and prioritized in every session. Being with people as they share their most vulnerable moments, times of profound beauty and devastating loss is an honor that I hold so very delicately.

As a multi-modal trauma specialist, I have dedicated my career to training in the most effective practices for healing and growth after trauma. These healing methods also apply to the pain, wounding, and upheaval that occurs naturally in life which can cause long lasting dysregulation. Along with psychoeducation, I regularly utilize Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing, Somatic Experiencing, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, Mindfulness Based Practices, Expressive Arts, Nature Based Therapy, and Equine Therapy.

This option is for CA and TX residents only and for people who prefer to stay under the umbrella and boundaries indicated by licensed practice.

### Notice of Privacy Practices

PO Box 56, Idyllwild, CA 92549 | 760-285-3125

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW HEALTH INFORMATION MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.

#### I. My Pledge Regarding Health Information

Health information about you and your health care is personal. A record of care and services is created to provide quality care and comply with certain legal requirements. This notice applies to all records generated by this mental health care practice.

Required by law to:

- Keep protected health information ("PHI") that identifies you private.
- Provide this notice of legal duties and privacy practices.
- Follow the terms of the notice currently in effect.
- Terms of this Notice may be changed; the new Notice will be available upon request, in the office, and on the website.

#### II. How Health Information May Be Used and Disclosed

**For Treatment, Payment, or Health Care Operations:** Federal privacy rules allow health care providers with a direct treatment relationship to use or disclose PHI without written authorization to carry out treatment, payment, or health care operations — including consultations with other licensed providers and referrals.

**Lawsuits and Disputes:** PHI may be disclosed in response to a court or administrative order, subpoena, or other lawful process, provided efforts have been made to notify you or obtain a protective order.

#### III. Certain Uses and Disclosures Require Your Authorization

- **Psychotherapy Notes** — Any use or disclosure requires authorization unless used for: treatment; training or supervising mental health practitioners; legal defense; HHS HIPAA compliance investigation; requirements of law; health oversight; coroner duties; or averting a serious threat to health and safety.
- **Marketing Purposes** — PHI will not be used or disclosed for marketing.
- **Sale of PHI** — PHI will not be sold in the regular course of business.

#### IV. Certain Uses and Disclosures Do Not Require Authorization

PHI may be used or disclosed without authorization for:

- State or federal legal requirements.
- Public health activities, including reporting suspected abuse or serious threats.
- Health oversight activities (audits, investigations).
- Judicial and administrative proceedings.
- Law enforcement purposes.
- Coroners or medical examiners performing authorized duties.
- Research purposes.
- Specialized government functions (military, intelligence, correctional institutions).
- Workers' compensation compliance.
- Appointment reminders and health-related benefits or services.

#### V. Uses and Disclosures Requiring Opportunity to Object

PHI may be provided to a family member, friend, or other person involved in your care or payment unless you object.

#### VI. Your Rights Regarding PHI

- **Request Limits** — You may request restrictions on use/disclosure for treatment, payment, or operations; these requests may be declined if they would affect your health care.
- **Out-of-Pocket Restrictions** — You may request restrictions on disclosure to health plans for services paid in full out-of-pocket.
- **Preferred Contact Method** — You may request a specific contact method or mailing address.
- **Access and Copies** — You have the right to an electronic or paper copy of your medical record (excluding psychotherapy notes) within 30 days of written request; a reasonable fee may apply.
- **Accounting of Disclosures** — You may request a list of disclosures made in the last six years for purposes other than treatment, payment, or operations; response within 60 days; first request free, additional requests in the same year subject to a reasonable fee.
- **Correct or Update PHI** — You may request corrections or additions; requests may be denied with written explanation within 60 days.
- **Copy of This Notice** — Available in paper or electronic form upon request.

### Practice Policies

#### Appointments and Cancellations

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. Full session fee applies to cancellations with less than 24 hours notice. A $10.00 service charge applies to returned checks. Late arrivals may result in a shortened session.

#### Telephone Accessibility

Leave a voicemail between sessions; calls returned within 24 hours. In-person sessions are preferred; phone sessions are available when out of town, ill, or needing additional support. For true emergencies, call 911 or a local emergency room.

#### Social Media and Telecommunication

Friend or contact requests from current or former clients are not accepted on any social networking site (Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.) to preserve confidentiality and maintain appropriate therapeutic boundaries.

#### Electronic Communication

Confidentiality of electronic communications (email, text) cannot be guaranteed. Email or text may be used for scheduling and cancellations only — not for therapeutic content or emergency requests.

Services by electronic means are considered telemedicine under the California Telemedicine Act of 1996. Key provisions include:

- Consent may be withheld or withdrawn at any time without affecting future care.
- All existing confidentiality protections apply.
- Access to all information transmitted during telemedicine is guaranteed; copies available for a reasonable fee.
- Identifiable images or information will not be disseminated without consent.
- Potential benefits include improved access, reduced costs, and better continuity of care.
- Potential risks include the therapist's inability to make direct visual or olfactory clinical observations (physical condition, grooming, body language, facial expression, congruence of verbal and non-verbal communication).

#### Minors

Parents may be legally entitled to some information about a minor's therapy. What is appropriate to share will be discussed with the minor and their parents.

#### Termination

Termination includes an appropriate process for closure. Treatment may be terminated after discussion if psychotherapy is not being effectively used or if payment is in default. A list of qualified psychotherapists will be provided upon request. If no appointment is scheduled for three consecutive weeks without prior arrangement, the professional relationship is considered discontinued for legal and ethical reasons.

## 22 — Workshops & Retreats

url: https://www.ahumansanctuary.com/communityofferings
type: landing
meta: Join nature-based groups and workshops at A Human Sanctuary. Heal relational trauma through connection to Earth's cycles, movement, and community in Idyllwild, CA.

### Partnering with Gaia, The Great Mother

Exposure to relational trauma frequently severs the trust and safety with other humans. For many children, being in the natural world creates a bridge back to feeling connected and held in a safe way. When processing childhood trauma, many clients naturally recall their safe places being in Mother Earth's embrace. At A Human Sanctuary, a space is held for connecting to the natural world as a stepping stone to healthy attachment with people.

### Connecting to Nature's Cycles

Nature is used as a lens toward healing and growth. Partnering with the elements, the rhythms, the dark and light aspects, we help people to see, process, and renegotiate their life story through this lens — not only metaphorically, but physically moving our collective bodies to reconnect and see our own experiences with this sight. As we plant, grow, harvest, renourish, and rest, the psyche and soul is allowed to follow suit.

### Animals Teaching Safe Connection

Our rescue animals hold a history, triggers, and fears, as well as unique beauties and presence that they bring. We pair children with the rescues, encouraging them to witness and see the possibility of overcoming or integrating past trauma. The limitations toward healing are seen as opportunities to learn to love others in a new way, unique to their individual needs, without judgment or intention.
