### Drive with Rideit

If requirements are met, complete the form; an email with onboarding instructions will follow.

### Driver Requirements

Minimum Requirements:
- Minimum driving age: 25
- At least 3 years of licensed driving experience in the United States
- Must use an eligible 4-door vehicle

Required Documents:
- Valid US driver's license
- Proof of residency (city/state/province)
- Proof of vehicle insurance
- Driver profile photo

### Driver Pre-Screening

Applicants complete a pre-screening form. Those who pass are contacted to schedule an interview with a RideIt regional manager, during which background check requirements, MVR reviews, and vehicle inspection standards are explained.

Submitting the form and completing an interview does not guarantee approval. Drivers operate as independent contractors; final approval is contingent on meeting all eligibility and safety requirements.

## 21 — Community Guidelines

url: https://www.rideitrr.com/community-guidelines
type: utility
meta: none

### RideIt Community Guidelines

Creating a safe, respectful, and reliable transportation community across rural North Carolina.

#### 1. Safety Comes First

- All riders and drivers must obey federal, state, and local laws including traffic laws, seatbelt requirements, and anti-discrimination laws.
- Drivers undergo background checks, MVR screening, annual re-runs, and insurance verification.
- Riders must not request trips beyond the 70-mile drop-off policy; drivers may end a trip if they feel unsafe.
- Weapons of any kind are prohibited.

#### 2. Respect for Everyone

- No discrimination based on race, age, gender or gender identity, ethnicity, disability, religion, sexual orientation, or national origin.
- No harassment, verbal or physical threats, unwanted comments, or inappropriate behavior.
- Riders should be clean and odor-free; drivers must maintain a clean, smoke-free vehicle. No open containers of alcohol.

#### 3. Appropriate Conduct

- Violence or physical confrontation results in immediate removal.
- Zero tolerance for sexual comments, unwanted contact, or inappropriate conduct. Drivers should never ask for a rider's personal contact information.
- No use or transport of illegal drugs; no riding while intoxicated to the point of creating a safety risk.

#### 4. Integrity & Fair Use

- Prohibited: fake accounts, chargeback fraud, fare manipulation, GPS tampering, and requesting rides without intent to take them.
- All payments must go through the RideIt platform — no cash fares, no off-platform rides.
- Use real name and accurate contact information; drivers must keep all documents current.

#### 5. Protecting Our Community

- No bodily fluids, blood, or hazardous materials; riders who damage a vehicle may be charged a cleaning or repair fee.
- Report concerns in the app; all reports are investigated.
- Riders are responsible for their belongings; drivers must return lost items through the official process.

#### 6. Additional Guidelines for Drivers

- Treat all riders with courtesy; keep conversations appropriate; avoid political or sensitive topics.
- Vehicles must be clean, smoke-free, in good working condition, registered, and insured.
- Avoid unnecessary cancellations; never cancel for discriminatory reasons.

#### 7. Additional Guidelines for Riders

- Be ready within 2 minutes of driver arrival.
- No slamming doors, feet on seats, or spills.
- Provide a proper car seat for young children following NC law.

#### 8. Enforcement

Actions range from temporary suspension to permanent removal and possible law enforcement reporting, depending on severity and frequency.

### RideIt Non-Discrimination Policy

Applies to all Riders, Drivers, and RideIt personnel.

#### Zero-Tolerance for Discrimination

No Rider or Driver may be refused service, treated unfairly, harassed, or subjected to biased behavior based on race, color, national origin, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, age, marital or family status, pregnancy, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic. Violations may result in account suspension or removal.

#### Service Must Be Provided to All Eligible Riders

Drivers may not refuse, cancel, or avoid rides based on protected characteristics, including neighborhood-based discrimination. Riders may not request drivers of a specific protected characteristic or treat drivers in a discriminatory manner.

#### Service Animals

Drivers must accept service animals in accordance with the ADA and state law, regardless of personal preferences, allergies, or religious objections. Refusal may result in permanent platform removal.

#### Accessibility

Drivers must provide reasonable assistance to riders with disabilities — including extra boarding/exit time and assistance with mobility aids. Drivers may only refuse a rider with a disability if their vehicle physically cannot accommodate a specific device and no safe alternative exists.

#### Reporting

Report discrimination via the RideIt app safety center, email support@rideit.com, or the emergency hotline for active safety events. All reports are reviewed promptly; severe violations may result in permanent account removal.

#### Enforcement

Actions include required training, temporary suspension, permanent account removal, and cooperation with law enforcement if laws were violated.

### RideIt Zero Tolerance Policy (Driver Code of Conduct)

#### 1. Professionalism and Courtesy
Drivers must treat all passengers, staff, and road users with respect at all times. Rude, aggressive, or discriminatory behavior is not tolerated.

#### 2. Safety and Compliance
Drivers must obey all traffic laws, wear seatbelts at all times, and not engage in reckless, speeding, or distracted driving (including phone use while moving). All vehicles must pass required inspections and remain in safe operating condition.

#### 3. Substance Use — Zero Tolerance
Drivers must not operate a vehicle under the influence of alcohol, illegal drugs, or any impairing substance. Violations result in immediate suspension or permanent removal. RideIt may cooperate with law enforcement for incidents involving suspected impairment or endangerment.

#### 4. Passenger Relations
Drivers must ensure all passengers feel safe and comfortable. Harassment, physical contact, and inappropriate comments are strictly prohibited. Drivers should assist passengers with boarding and respect personal boundaries.

#### 5. Cleanliness and Vehicle Condition
Vehicles must be kept clean, odor-free, and well-maintained. Drivers are responsible for meeting RideIt's vehicle eligibility standards at all times.

#### 6. Trip Integrity
Drivers must complete all accepted trips as assigned. Unapproved pickups, cash transactions, and off-platform arrangements are prohibited. Cancellations should only occur for legitimate safety or operational reasons.

#### 7. Confidentiality and Data Privacy
Drivers must not disclose, record, or misuse passenger personal information. All RideIt app and platform data are confidential.

#### 8. Reporting and Accountability
Drivers must report accidents, incidents, or policy violations immediately through the RideIt app or company support. Failure to report or cooperate with investigations may result in suspension or termination.

### RideIt Customer Cancellation Policy

**Free Cancellations:** Customers may cancel for free within 5 minutes after a driver has been assigned, provided the driver has not yet arrived.

**Cancellation Fee:** A $3.50 fee may be charged if the customer cancels more than 5 minutes after a driver has been assigned.

**No Fee If:**
- No driver has been assigned
- The driver does not arrive
- Cancellation occurs within the free window

**Payment:** All trips require a valid debit or credit card. Cancellation fees are charged automatically to the card on file.

**Additional Notes:** RideIt may waive fees for system errors, emergencies, or service issues. Repeated cancellations may result in account review or restrictions.

type: utility
date: December 22, 2025

### RideIt Driver Cancellation Policy

**Driver Cancellations (No Penalty):**
- Driver has waited at least 5 minutes at the pickup location and the rider has not arrived.
- Driver is unable to complete the trip due to safety concerns, rider behavior, or circumstances beyond their control.

**Distance-Based Responsibility:**
- If a driver cancels before arriving at or making reasonable progress toward the pickup location, the rider is not charged a cancellation fee.
- If a driver has arrived and waited at least 5 minutes and the trip is then canceled due to rider unavailability, a cancellation fee may apply to the rider.

**Driver Fees:** RideIt does not charge drivers a cancellation fee at this time. Cancellation fees collected from riders are retained by RideIt unless otherwise specified.

**Additional Notes:** Repeated driver cancellations are subject to review to maintain service reliability.
