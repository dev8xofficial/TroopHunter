#### RideIt Community Guidelines

Creating a safe, respectful, and reliable transportation community across rural North Carolina.

RideIt is committed to connecting riders and drivers through safe, respectful, and trustworthy experiences. These Community Guidelines outline what we expect from every member of our community — riders, drivers, and RideIt staff — to keep our platform safe and reliable.

By using RideIt, you agree to follow these guidelines at all times.

1. Safety Comes First
1.1 Follow the Law
All riders and drivers must obey all federal, state, and local laws, including:

- Traffic laws and speed limits
- Seatbelt requirements
- Anti-discrimination laws
- No tampering with safety equipment or SOS features

1.2 Driver Screening

RideIt drivers undergo:

- Background checks
- MVR (driving record) screening
- Annual re-runs
- Insurance verification

1.3 Secure Trips

For everyone’s safety:

- Riders must not ask drivers to exceed the 70-mile drop-off policy.
- Drivers may end a trip if they feel unsafe.
- Riders must not request dangerous, illegal, or off-road trips.

1.4 No Weapons

Weapons of any kind are prohibited during a RideIt trip.

2. Respect for Everyone
2.1 No Discrimination

RideIt prohibits discrimination based on:

- Race
- Age
- Gender or gender identity
- Ethnicity
- Disability
- Religion
- Sexual orientation
- National origin

Drivers must provide service to all riders unless safety is at risk.

2.2 Respectful Communication
Treat others the way you want to be treated:

- No harassment
- No verbal or physical threats
- No unwanted comments
- No inappropriate behavior

2.3 Cleanliness & Personal Standards

- Riders should keep themselves and their belongings clean and odor-free
- Drivers must maintain a clean, smoke-free vehicle at all times.
- No open containers of alcohol.

3. Appropriate Conduct
3.1 No Violence or Threats
Violence or physical confrontation of any kind will result in immediate removal from RideIt.
3.2 No Sexual Misconduct
Zero tolerance for:

- Sexual comments
- Unwanted contact
- Inappropriate questions or conduct

Drivers should never ask for a rider’s personal contact information.

3.3 No Illegal Substances

- No use or transport of illegal drugs.
- No riding while intoxicated to the point of creating a safety risk.

4. Integrity & Fair Use
4.1 Fraud Prevention
RideIt prohibits:

- Fake accounts
- Chargeback fraud
- Manipulating fares
- GPS tampering
- Requesting trips you don’t intend to take

4.2 Honest Payments

All payments must go through the RideIt platform. No cash fares. No soliciting off-platform rides.
4.3 Accurate Account Information

- Use your real name.
- Use accurate contact information.
- Drivers must keep all documents (license, registration, insurance) up to date

Drivers may not refuse riders because of disability, unless the driver’s vehicle cannot physically accommodate a specific device and no safe alternative exists.

5. Protecting Our Community

5.1 Health & Cleanliness

For safety and courtesy:

- No bodily fluids, blood, or hazardous materials during trips.
- Riders who damage a vehicle may be charged a cleaning or repair fee.

5.2 Reporting Concerns
If something feels off, report it in the app or at RideIt support. We investigate all reports.

5.3 Lost Items

Riders are responsible for their belongings. Drivers must return lost items through the official process.

6. Additional Guidelines for Drivers
6.1 Professional Conduct
Drivers must:

- Treat all riders with courtesy
- Keep conversations appropriate
- Avoid political or sensitive topics

6.2 Vehicle Standards
Vehicles must be:

- Clean
- Smoke-free
- In good working condition
- Registered and insured

6.3 Cancellation Standards

Drivers should avoid unnecessary cancellations and must not cancel for discriminatory reasons.

7. Additional Guidelines for Riders
7.1 Timeliness

Riders should be ready within 2 minutes of the driver’s arrival.
7.2 Respect the Vehicle

- No slamming doors
- No feet on seats
- No spills or damage

7.3 Children & Car Seats

Riders must provide a proper car seat when riding with young children, following NC law.

8. Enforcement of These Guidelines
RideIt may take action, including:

- Temporary account suspension
- Permanent removal from RideIt
- Reporting to law enforcement (when required)

Actions depend on the severity and frequency of violations.

9. Updates to the Guidelines

RideIt may update these guidelines as we grow into new counties and expand our service offerings. Notice of updates will be posted in the app and on the website.

- Riders with cognitive or communication disabilities

Drivers must provide reasonable assistance, including:

- Racial slurs

All reports are reviewed promptly. Severe violations may result in permanent account removal.

RideIt may take any of the following actions depending on the severity and pattern of behavior:

- Temporary suspension
- Permanent removal of Driver or Rider accounts

- Improve built-in app features that support safe and inclusive rides
- Monitor patterns of behavior to identify concerns early

RideIt Driver Cancellation Policy

(Effective on 12/22/2025 for all RideIt rides)

Driver Cancellation Policy

RideIt values driver time and reliability while also recognizing that unexpected situations may occur.

Driver Cancellations

Drivers may cancel a ride without penalty if they have waited at least 5 minutes at the pickup location and the rider has not arrived.

Drivers may also cancel for free if they are unable to complete the trip due to safety concerns, rider behavior, or circumstances beyond their control.

Distance-Based Responsibility

If a driver cancels before arriving at the pickup location or before making reasonable progress toward the pickup, the rider is not charged a cancellation fee.

If a driver has arrived at the pickup location or has been en route and has waited at least 5 minutes, and the trip is then canceled due to rider unavailability or related circumstances, a cancellation fee may apply.

RideIt may use distance traveled, time elapsed, and driver arrival status to determine cancellation responsibility.

Driver Fees

RideIt does not charge drivers a cancellation fee at this time.

Cancellation fees collected from riders are retained by RideIt unless otherwise specified under a separate no-show or wait-time compensation policy.

Additional Notes

RideIt reserves the right to review repeated driver cancellations and may take action to maintain service reliability.
