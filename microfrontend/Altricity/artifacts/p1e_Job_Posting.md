**Context for Screening Questions Below**

Altricity sells through multiple channels including dealers, installers, fleets, and direct-to-consumer campaigns. Our primary website currently emphasizes dealer acquisition and partner support, while direct online purchases are being tested through targeted Meta campaign landing pages.

We are looking for someone who can evaluate conversion performance within this multi-channel strategy rather than assuming a pure ecommerce business model. For example, “Find a Dealer” is an intentional CTA in our site design, while direct purchase CTAs may be used only on specific campaign-based landing pages.

We are specifically looking for candidates who have solved this type of challenge before: a novel product, limited category awareness, warm/high-intent paid traffic, little or no add-to-cart activity, and behavioral data available through tools like Microsoft Clarity.

We use and value AI tools as a company. However, canned or generic AI-generated answers will not be adequate here. Please answer thoughtfully and specifically based on your own experience and how you would approach this situation.
