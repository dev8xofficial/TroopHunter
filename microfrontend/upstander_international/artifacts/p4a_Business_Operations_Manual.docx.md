## 1. Company Overview

### 1.1 Business Name

UPstander International

### 1.2 Industry

Speaking, Training, and Education. The company also publishes books and online courses. It sits at the intersection of professional speaking, diversity consulting, and e-learning.

### 1.3 Core Products or Services

- Live keynote speeches and workshops for organizations
- Online courses on diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI)
- Books and music/motivational audio projects

### 1.4 Target Customers

- Corporate organizations (companies seeking DEI training)

- Colleges and universities
- Government agencies
- Nonprofits and community organizations

### 1.5 Value Proposition

UPstander International helps organizations and individuals stop being bystanders and start being upstanders — people who take a stand when they see injustice. The company delivers this through a mix of keynote speaking, performance (spoken word and hip-hop), and practical training. The founder, Dr. Omekongo Dibinga, is the product. His personal story, credentials, and multi-format delivery (speech, poetry, hip-hop, dialogue) make the experience different from a standard consultant.

- Course enrollment fees (e.g., R.E.A.Ch! online course at $199 per participant)

**Pricing model:**

- Speaking engagements: custom pricing per event (not publicly listed)

**Revenue streams:**

1. Live speaking events (primary)
2. Online course sales
3. Book sales

5. International/government training contracts

## 14. Summary of Business Operations

UPstander International is a one-person speaking and training business founded and led by Dr. Omekongo Dibinga, based in Washington, DC. The business has operated since 2004.

**What it does:** Dr. Dibinga travels to companies, schools, universities, and government organizations to speak, train, and consult on diversity, equity, inclusion, and leadership. He also sells online courses and books through his website.

**How it makes money:** The primary revenue comes from keynote speaking fees. Secondary revenue comes from online course enrollments ($199 per student), book sales, and consulting contracts with organizations that want ongoing DEI support.

**How it finds clients:** Almost entirely through inbound channels. Dr. Dibinga's visibility on CNN, NPR, FOX, and other media drives awareness. His LinkedIn activity, TEDx talks, and speaking at major conferences generate inquiries. Books on Amazon lead people to reach out for speaking engagements.

**How it delivers:** Dr. Dibinga personally delivers every keynote, workshop, and training session. Online courses are self-paced and hosted on WordPress using the Masteriyo LMS. Consulting clients receive regular facilitation sessions, resource guides, and strategic planning support.

**How big it is:** Very small. Two employees are listed on LinkedIn. The founder handles nearly all client-facing work. The online course platform currently has 16 enrolled students.

**Where the opportunities are:** The business has a strong brand, deep credibility, and a clear mission. The main gap is in sales infrastructure, marketing systems, and operational support. There is no outbound sales process, no CRM, no email list strategy, and no one to manage logistics except the founder. Any B2B service provider who can take operational or marketing work off Dr. Dibinga's plate — or who can open new organizational sales pipelines — would add immediate value to this business.
