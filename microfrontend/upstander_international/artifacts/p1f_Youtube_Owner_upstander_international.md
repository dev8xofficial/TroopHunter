video almost every day, and individual videos typically draw 2,000–3,500 views. This

## Why This Matters

- **Compounding, not annual.** The speaking-reach figure (6,000+) resets once a
  year; this channel produces a fresh burst of exposure daily. Every day it runs
  without a confirmed working link into the R.E.A.Ch! or L.E.A.D. course pages is a
  day of audience that arrives with nowhere reliable to go — reinforcing, not
  replacing, the broken-checkout finding already documented in p1a_Website.md.
- **Independently verifiable.** Subscriber count and posting cadence are both
  directly observable on the channel itself, giving this a similar L1 evidentiary
  weight to the site-scrape findings, even though the collection method (manual
  review vs. automated scrape) differs.
- **Reinforces urgency, not a new bottleneck.** This is not a new root problem —
  it's a second, larger, more frequent audience source hitting the *same* broken
  purchase and email-capture infrastructure already identified as the primary
  bottleneck.

## Caveats (Read Before Using in Any Deliverable)

- **Collection method differs from p1a/p1b/p1c.** Those files were built from a
  page-by-page scrape with cited page numbers. This file is a manually confirmed
  observation, not a scrape output. Treat subscriber count and view range as L1
  Sourced (directly observed, verifiable by revisiting the channel), and the
  ~912,500 annual-views figure as L2 Estimated — it must always carry an
  "[Estimated]" label and state its assumption when used.
- **Views ≠ unique viewers.** 2,000–3,500 views per video does not mean that many
  distinct new people; a meaningful share is likely repeat/returning subscribers
  rewatching or catching multiple uploads. Do not present the annual estimate as
  "912,500 new people reached" in any deliverable — that overstates the claim.
- **Whether video descriptions/pinned comments link to a course page is unverified.**
  This file confirms the channel is active and disconnected in principle from the
  broken checkout; it does not confirm whether Dr. Dibinga has ever attempted a
  direct video-to-course link. That remains an open discovery question (see
  p0b_Problem_Register.md).
