About: 

I am an UPstander. My life’s mission is to inspire all across the globe to take a stand when they witness an injustice, no matter how small or large. I'm an international speaker, trilingual poet, TV talk show host, rapper, and professor of Inter-cultural Communication at American University, where I also serve as a faculty affiliate to the Antiracist Research & Policy Center. My Urban Music Award winning work has best been described by Nikki Giovanni as “outstanding, exciting, and new while being very old.” My book, "From the Limbs of My Poetree" was described by Essence Magazine as “a remarkable and insightful collection of exquisite poetry that touches sacred places within your spirit.” I was one of 5 international recipients out of 750,000 to win the first ever “CNN iReport Spirit Award.” I have received over 1,000,000 views on CNN.com and I am a 4x TEDx speaker.

My writings and performances have appeared in O Magazine, as well as on TV and radio from CNN, BET, and the BBC to NPR, Music Choice, and Voice of America in millions of homes in over 150 countries. I have also written songs for major motion pictures as well as organizations such as NASA and the Enough! Project. I have spoken before the United Nations, and I partner with the State Department to conduct leadership trainings overseas. I speak to corporate, government, and educational organizations across the country on issues centered on leadership, antiracism, diversity, and inclusion. 

My music and writings have appeared alongside artists such as Sheryl Crow, Angelina Jolie, Norah Jones, Damien Rice, Angelique Kidjo, Don Cheadle, and Yassine Bey. He has shared the stage with Jane Fonda, Joaquin Phoenix, Martin Sheen, OutKast, Sonia Sanchez, The Last Poets, and NFL great Aaron Rodgers. Internationally, I have directly shared my work in nearly 30 countries.

At UMD, I worked with the Southern Poverty Law Center’s “Teaching Diverse Students Initiative.”  
I have published and produced 7 books, 8-fusion music and motivational CDs, and one independent DVD. My motivational book "G.R.O.W. Towards Your Greatness! 10 Steps to Living Your Best Life" has received praise from great motivational speakers such as Les Brown and Willie Jolley. My most recent book “The UPstander’s Guide to an Outstanding Life” is a life balance book for students.

As we honor what would have been Ahmaud Arbery’s 32nd birthday on May 8, 2026, we remember a life filled with promise that was tragically stolen by hatred and violence. Born in 1994, Ahmaud was a beloved son, brother, and friend known for his love of running and his vibrant spirit.On this day, his family continues to carry the immense pain of his absence, while advocates and supporters nationwide recommit to the fight for racial justice and safety for Black people.

Supporters and loved ones are remembering Ahmaud, who would have turned 32, by celebrating his life and sharing his story to ensure he is never forgotten.

While the three men responsible for his murder in Feb. 2020 are serving life sentences, the fight against systemic racism continues.

The Ahmaud Arbery Foundation and community members continue to hold events, including memorial runs, to honor his memory and demand a world free from racial violence.We remember Ahmaud not only for how his life was taken but for the vibrant life he deserved to keep living. His memory serves as a reminder to keep pushing for justice, accountability, and a world where Black lives are fully valued and protected.
Rest in power, Ahmaud Arbery.
