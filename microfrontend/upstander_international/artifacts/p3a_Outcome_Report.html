Section 4

What breaks in the next 12–24 months without a fix

Three compounding risks — each preventable with the right build now.

Risk 01 · 2026 Book Launches

Three book launches with no capture infrastructure — repeat of the 16-student pattern at larger scale

Each launch drives a short, high-intent traffic spike from media appearances and social posts. Without a landing page, pre-order email capture, and post-purchase course cross-sell, the spike produces no durable list and no downstream revenue. The paperback re-release of "Lies About Black People" has already launched without this in place.

✓ Prevented by: Pre-launch landing pages with email capture and automated course cross-sell built before each launch window opens.
Risk 02 · Corporate Buyer Scrutiny

DEI budget tightening pushes institutional buyers toward vendors with credible digital presence

Corporate procurement increasingly involves a digital due-diligence step where a buyer's team reviews the vendor's site before approving an engagement. A site with 404 errors and no case studies signals organisational instability — disqualifying Dr. Dibinga before a conversation begins, regardless of his credentials.

✓ Prevented by: A working corporate landing page with L.E.A.D. course structure, case studies, and a B2B inquiry form that matches institutional buyer expectations.
Risk 03 · Compounding Technical Debt

Continued Upwork patching builds cost and fragility — the next major update requires a rebuild, not a fix

The current WordPress/Masteriyo stack has already failed structurally — 17+ broken pages, duplicate error routes, MailPoet misconfiguration. Each incremental Upwork patch adds complexity to an unstable foundation. The $50 current job is not a solution; it is a deferral of the rebuild that becomes more expensive with every month of delay.

✓ Prevented by: A single architecture decision to move to a stable platform (Webflow or Kajabi) before the 2026 book launch cycle accelerates content update volume.

Industry reference points: Benchmarks for solo educator course conversion rates are used only as directional context in body copy — not as headline figures. They are flagged explicitly when referenced and described as "based on comparable platforms; not specific to UPstander International."
