## Discovery Questions

The following questions must be answered to strengthen L2/L3 metrics to L1 and to resolve the two "no proof screen" problems before deliverable build.

1. **[Resolves Problem 8 — dual domain architecture]** "Which domain is intended to become your primary professional home — upstanderonline.com or omekongo.com? Are both domains currently under your control? Have you considered redirecting one to the other, or do you want to maintain them as separate properties for different audiences?"

2. **[Resolves Problem 6 — book/Amazon cross-sell]** "For the three books coming in 2026, do you want to capture the sale on-site, or is Amazon fulfillment intentional and your goal is simply to add email capture before the handoff? Do you want each new book to have its own dedicated landing page with a pre-order / launch email capture mechanism?"

3. **[Upgrades Problem 5 from Partial to Yes]** "When a corporate buyer arrives at the site looking to book the L.E.A.D. corporate course, what is the ideal next step — a direct purchase, a discovery call, or a proposal request? What is the typical deal size for a corporate L.E.A.D. engagement?"

4. **[Quantifies the Upwork dependency — upgrades Problem 3 to L1 metric]** "In the last 12 months, how many times have you had to hire someone on Upwork specifically to fix or update the current site — and roughly how long does it take from the moment you post a job to the moment the fix is live?"

5. **[Validates speaking audience size and email list baseline]** "You have spoken to 6,000+ students in a single year. Of those, how many ended up on an email list of any kind — Mailchimp, MailPoet, any platform? If you had a functioning email opt-in on the site today, what lead magnet would feel most natural to offer a first-time visitor?"

6. **[Validates platform preference and rules out hidden constraints]** "The Upwork posting listed WordPress, Webflow, Systeme.io, and Kajabi as preferred platforms. Is there a specific reason for the WordPress preference — existing content, plugin dependencies, or familiarity? Would you be open to Webflow for the marketing site with Kajabi for course delivery, if that combination produced the cleanest self-management experience?"

7. **[Surfaces timeline pressure from 2026 book launches]** "You mentioned three books coming out in 2026. What is the publish date for the first one? Is there a hard deadline for when the new site needs to be live relative to any launch event or media appearance?"

8. **[Identifies decision-blocking concern not visible in research]** "You have 18 five-star reviews on Upwork and $19,900 invested in digital work. What has made those investments feel less-than-satisfying — is it the technical outcome, the time it takes, the need to re-explain yourself with each new hire, or something else?"

9. **[Resolves Problem 9 — YouTube-to-course conversion path]** "Your YouTube channel posts almost daily to 25,000 subscribers. Do your video descriptions or pinned comments currently link to either course? If someone finishes a video and wants to learn more, where does that click actually go today?"
