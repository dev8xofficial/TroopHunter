Vs. Ball Screw Modules :
Unlike ball screw systems that are limited by a single drive shaft, our system allows for independent control of multiple movers on a single track. Each mover can operate with its own specific speed, stroke, and acceleration. Furthermore, our solution offers a more compact footprint, and for travel lengths exceeding 1 meter, our cost-efficiency becomes significantly more competitive as the limitations of high-precision screws increase.

Vs. Traditional Belt Drives:
Our linear motor technology offers a massive leap in stability, repeatability, and service life. While belts suffer from elasticity and wear over time, our system maintains rock-solid precision. The longer the travel distance, the more pronounced the advantages in performance and maintenance savings become.

XiaoLan power guide rail, new standard of linear motor stages. 

Traditional linear motors are cumbersome to assemble, have long ordering cycles, and are rarely available in desired lengths, making it difficult for distributors to plan, resulting in numerous models, high inventory costs, and hindering secondary distribution. This impacts end-user delivery times and significantly increases costs.

Xiao Lan was developed to address this pain point, precisely designed for rapid and long-stroke object handling in automated production lines or equipment.

It features only three track widths, matching 11 different carrier for various load capacities, allowing for modular linear motor designs to meet different length requirements. It can be cut, assembled, and used immediately, with same-day delivery, making it ideal for standardization and secondary distribution. It also reduces inventory models and lowers capital pressure.

This solution will:

Phase Loss Compensation: Automatically adapts to single- or two-phase losses, ensuring continuous power to critical systems like pumps.

Remote Control Enablement: Allows you to monitor and manage your irrigation system anytime, anywhere, even from home in Singapore, through a user-friendly remote interface.

Wide Voltage Adaptive Dedicated Inverter: Adjusts the inverter to accommodate a wider range of voltage conditions, ensuring optimal operation of pumps and irrigation equipment.

With this setup, your farm's irrigation system will operate reliably 24/7, eliminating disruptions caused by power instability. This solution is designed to help more farmers facing similar power challenges ensure their irrigation systems operate efficiently year-round.
