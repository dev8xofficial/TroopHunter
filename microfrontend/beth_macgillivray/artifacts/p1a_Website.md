#### What's Your Property Worth?

Home valuations give valuable knowledge that can help plan for the future and support smart decisions — it's good practice to stay informed about home equity and borrowing or selling potential. This tool provides a more robust, accurate assessment than the major real estate portals offer; for the most precise valuation, clients can request a customized Comparative Market Analysis or an appraisal.

#### Home Valuation FAQ

**What Is a Home Valuation?**
A home valuation determines the current market value of a residential property. It is crucial for real estate transactions, preventing excessive borrowing and financial losses, since the home acts as loan collateral and a thorough valuation safeguards the lender's ability to recover costs if the mortgage isn't repaid.

**How Is the Valuation of My Home Calculated?**
Calculated from location, age, size, condition, improvements/renovations, and recent comparable sale prices in the neighborhood, factoring in current market trends, inventory, interest rates, and buyer sentiment.

**How Accurate Is the Online Home Valuation?**
Online valuations offer a good general starting estimate but may not capture recent renovations, unique features, historical value, architectural significance, or subjective market perception. An in-person appraisal is recommended for the most accurate assessment.

#### How Is a Valuation Performed?

**Comparative Market Analysis** — A CMA evaluates similar homes recently sold nearby. Agents typically identify three comparable sales, price out the differences from the subject home, and adjust each comp to estimate current market value.

**Appraisals** — An unbiased, professional valuation typically ordered by a lender for purchases and refinances (cost up to $500, paid by the homeowner). The appraiser inspects the home's interior and exterior, reviews comparable sales and market trends, and compiles a report with a building sketch, comp map, photos, and square footage calculation.

#### Why Is a Valuation Important?

- **Refinancing** — Lenders typically allow borrowing up to 75%–96.5% of property value; more equity means better refinance terms.
- **Home Improvements** — Helps avoid over-improving a home already priced at the high end for its neighborhood.
- **Qualifying for Credit** — A HELOC typically requires at least 20% equity; a valuation helps determine eligibility.
- **Planning** — Knowing home value and available equity supports planning for unforeseen financial needs or a quick relocation.
