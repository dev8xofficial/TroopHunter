2. **[L4 → L1] Inquiry volume:** "How many inquiries per week do you receive from international clients — and how do you track and respond to them today? Does it all come through your phone and email personally, or does anyone else field first contact?"

3. **[L4 → L1] Media conversion:** "After a media appearance — RAI, La7, Newsmax — how many leads typically reach out in the following days? And of those, what percentage convert to a first meeting or appointment?"

4. **[L4 → L1] Time cost:** "How many hours per week do you personally spend on lead intake — responding to inquiries, qualifying new contacts, scheduling first calls — versus time spent on active deals, negotiations, and existing client relationships?"

5. **[Bottleneck validation] Agent onboarding:** "When you bring on a new agent, what does the first 30 days look like for them? Could they take a deal from first inquiry to close without checking in with you? If not — where do they typically get stuck?"

6. **[Commercial vs. residential] Pipeline differentiation:** "How do you manage commercial inquiries differently from residential today? Is there a separate intake path, a different follow-up process, or does everything come through the same channel and you sort it manually?"

7. **[Team roles] Eddie and Vivek:** "What do Eddie and Vivek currently handle day-to-day — are they client-facing, operational, or administrative? I want to make sure whatever we build works for the whole team, not just you personally."

8. **[Investor advisory] Reporting workflow:** "When you produce a comparable analysis or an investment report for a client, what does that process look like end-to-end — how long does it take, and how do you deliver it to them?"

9. **[Decision risk] Prior tech experience:** "Have you looked at any CRM or real estate tech platforms before? If yes — what happened, and what didn't work? I'd rather know what failed before than sell you something you've already tried."

10. **[Hiring timeline] Agent recruitment urgency:** "Your LinkedIn says you're actively hiring. Do you have a timeline — are you looking to bring someone on in the next 30 to 60 days? That changes what we'd prioritize in the initial setup and which features we'd configure first."
