## GOVERNING RULES (apply to every message and branch below)

### Hold-Back Chain — This Stage's Slice

| Stage | SHOWS | WITHHOLDS |
|---|---|---|
| Video (already sent) | The bottleneck, 4 problems, honest-limit boundary, ONE stakes number, ONE proof-of-competence line | Any solution, feature, ROI table, build timeline, team, cost, or price |
| **Messaging (this stage)** | The guarantee, stakes tie-back, proof of competence, legitimacy | The full diagnosis, any working solution, any build detail |
| Consultation (future — `p3d_Consultation_Playbook.md`) | Full confirmed diagnosis, real numbers, cost of bottleneck computed live, solution direction (named capabilities only) | Working software, screen-by-screen features, build timeline, team, build price |

### Two-Step Money Rule
Money is never asked for inside a deliverable. The video ended on a reply ask. The consultation fee is offered in Message 2 only — **after** Message 1 dialogue exists. Each paid gate is earned, never demanded. Message 2 must never be sent without a prior reply from Alex.

### ROI Integrity (Decision Card §8)
The stakes figure is L2 (Estimated). Every reference in every message must match the exact phrasing from Beat 4. Do not introduce new numbers, commission rates (L3 — excluded), or precise dollar figures. "Six-figure territory per appearance" is the ceiling of specificity permitted.

### Forbidden Language
Never use: "automate," "replace," "scale your team," "transform your operations," "streamline."
Always use: "capture," "amplify," "preserve," "extend."

### CONFIDENCE SIGNAL ADJUSTMENT — READ BEFORE DRAFTING MESSAGE 2

**Current signal: Medium.** Apply these rules when writing or adapting Message 2:

| Signal | Message 2 Fee Treatment |
|---|---|
| High | Fee stated confidently, no softening language. |
| **Medium (current)** | Stakes figure stated as a conservative estimate. Session framed as where the real number gets confirmed. "Pin your actual number down" is the required hedge. ✅ Applied below. |
| Low | Do NOT push the full paid ask — Message 1 does more qualification. Message 2 leads with method rigour and the guarantee rather than a confident diagnosis. |

At Low confidence, when in genuine doubt, gather more before charging — pushing a paid offer on thin research does the most damage.

## B.2 — The War-Game Matrix

---

### BRANCH A — Correction: "You Got It Wrong"

Beat 5 explicitly invited this. A correction is the strongest argument for the paid diagnosis, not against it. Never defend. Never cling to a figure he just refuted.

---

> That's genuinely useful — thank you for telling me. [Acknowledge the specific correction Alex gave, directly and without deflection.] From outside, [the thing flagged] looked like the main gap — if I had that wrong, that changes the picture.
>
> That's exactly why doing this properly matters. From outside I'll always have blind spots. The session is where my guesses get replaced with your real numbers.

---

**If the correction guts the entire diagnosis ("we don't even do that"):** Do NOT push the consultation offer. Acknowledge, ask one genuine question, leave the door open.

> Appreciate you telling me — that changes things significantly. Can I ask: where does the real friction actually live for you right now? I'd rather understand it properly than push something that doesn't fit.

Leave it there. Do not follow up unless Alex re-engages.

---

### BRANCH B — Silent Watcher: Watched, No Reply

**Timing:** One touch only, 4–6 days after the video was sent.

**Option 1 — Sharp question (use this if no new public observation exists):**

> No need to reply to the video — one quick question: is the intake gap behind your media appearances actually what's slowing your international growth, or am I off?

**Option 2 — New public observation (use only if a genuine one exists — do NOT imply insight was withheld from the video):**

*Candidate observation for Carini Group:* If Alex has posted a new media appearance, listing close, or hiring announcement since the video was sent, open with a brief acknowledgement of that — then ask the same question. Example: "Saw the [new post/appearance] — congratulations. One quick question: is the intake gap the main thing holding that momentum back, or is there something else in the way?"

**Maximum one silent-watcher touch.** Further messages read as pressure. If no reply: stop. Re-engage in 90 days with a fresh industry insight — not about Carini Group.

### BRANCH J — "I Want a Free Call Instead"

---

> I get it — paying a stranger feels like a risk, and I respect that. The guarantee exists for exactly that reason: if the session doesn't earn its fee, you keep the diagnosis and pay nothing. You decide, not me.
>
> That said, if you'd like a quick 15-minute call just to see if this is worth your time, I'm happy to do that first. No diagnosis in that call — just a fit check.

---

The 15-minute fit-check is a concession, not the default. Offer it only if Alex is clearly interested but stuck on trust.

**Minimum-viable fit-check structure (use this if the call happens before `p3d_Consultation_Playbook.md` is generated — discard once §F.8 exists):**

1. Open by asking which of the four flagged problems matters most to him and why now — keeps the call anchored to what he said in Message 1, not a fresh pitch.
2. Answer **orienting questions only**: how the paid session works, what the guarantee covers, what he keeps either way. Never answer **diagnostic questions** (his actual numbers, a specific recommendation, "what would you build") — redirect those with the Branch S line.
3. Close with a direct yes/no: "Does this feel worth the paid session, or should I leave it here?"

**Mid-call Branch S rule:** If the call drifts into free diagnosis at any point, apply the Branch S response verbally and stop answering substantive questions.

### BRANCH S — Free Consulting Extraction

**Detection signals:** Multiple detailed questions after Message 1 with no movement toward Message 2; questions that ask for the consultation's deliverables ("so what would you recommend?", "what tool should we use for X?"); a "just one more question" pattern; or mid-call drift into diagnostic territory.

**First response:**

---

> Great question — that's exactly the kind of thing the session is built to answer properly. I could give you a quick take here, but the honest answer is it depends on your numbers, and I'd rather give you the right answer than a fast one. That's what the session is for.

---

**If it continues:**

> I want to give you a proper answer, not a corridor one. The session is where I can do that.

Then stop answering substantive questions.

**Mid-call application:** If this pattern emerges during the Branch J fit-check call, apply the same line verbally and redirect to the close: "Does this feel worth the paid session, or should I leave it here?"

**The boundary:** factual observations about their problem = free (already given in the video and the brief). Specific recommendations, solutions, tool choices, or architecture = paid.
