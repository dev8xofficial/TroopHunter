### About

CARINI GROUP is a full-service boutique real estate brokerage in NYC, founded on excellence in customer service, competence, and relentlessness. The firm has assisted hundreds of clients with buying, selling, and renting. 50% of business is domestic; 50% international. Services include wealth advisory for real estate investors and project management in partnership with banks, attorneys, title companies, architects, general contractors, photographers, accountants, management companies, insurance companies, and press.

- Investment Advisory
- 1031 Exchanges
- Market Research / Comparable Reporting
- Staging
- Construction / Renovation Management
- Media / Photo / Video / Press Content Creation

### Alex Carini, Founder & CEO

Alex Carini is a top NYC real estate broker with transatlantic business contacts. He has represented Fortune 500 executives, politicians, athletes, celebrities, developers, and business professionals for their NYC real estate needs.

Prior to founding CARINI GROUP, he managed a European-based NYC brokerage specializing in international clients, and held a Business Development Manager role at a top NYC law firm helping foreign businesses establish U.S. ventures. He began his career with family real estate businesses: Domus S.r.l. (Italian developer) and Boseba S.l. (Spain development/property management), and remains an advisor to both.

Born in Spain, raised in Italy. Education: B.S. International Business, Northeastern University (Boston); B.S. Economics and Trade and M.S. International Management, Cattolica Del Sacro Cuore University. Fluent in English, Italian, and Spanish; conversational in Portuguese.

## 30 — ADA / Reasonable Accommodations

url: <https://carinigroup.com/?page=policy&sub=ada>
type: utility
meta: none

### Reasonable Accommodations (New York State Human Rights Law)

Housing providers must make reasonable accommodations for persons with disabilities. Tenants may request:

- Permission to modify the interior of their unit (at tenant's expense; restoration may be required on move-out)
- Changes to housing provider rules, policies, practices, or services
- Modifications to common areas (housing provider pays for common-area modifications)

### Examples

- Ramps or accessible entry/exit for mobility impairments
- Permission for assistance animals despite no-pet rules (with healthcare provider documentation)
- Grab bar installation at tenant expense (reinforcement of walls in post-March 1991 buildings paid by housing provider)
- Priority parking near unit

### Required Standards (buildings constructed after March 13, 1991)

- Public/common areas accessible to persons with disabilities
- Doors wide enough for wheelchair passage
- Accessible passageways, fixtures, outlets, thermostats, bathrooms, and kitchens

### Filing a Complaint

Complaints must be filed with the NYS Division of Human Rights within one year, or in court within three years. Website: <www.dhr.ny.gov> | Phone: 1-888-392-3644
