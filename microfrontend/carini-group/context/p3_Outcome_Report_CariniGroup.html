Section 01

Current State Overview

Carini Group is a high-performance boutique brokerage with a genuinely differentiated position — international reach, multilingual service, and a founder brand that drives consistent inbound demand. These are the operational constraints that are currently limiting your growth capacity.

Current Challenge Business Impact
✕
No CRM or lead management system in use — leads arrive by email, phone, and a basic contact form with no scoring or routing.
 As international media exposure grows, untracked leads are falling through the gaps. A single inbox across multiple time zones is a structural bottleneck on revenue.
✕
50% of clients are international, but there are no dedicated digital tools for the remote buyer journey — no virtual tours, multilingual portal, or async consultation booking.
 International clients face unnecessary friction: unfamiliarity with NYC neighbourhoods, inability to view properties remotely, currency complexity, and dependency on scheduled calls.
✕
Transaction coordination across attorneys, title companies, banks, and inspectors is managed entirely by phone and email — no deal room or shared workflow hub.
 With 5–7 external parties per deal, the agent spends significant time on project management rather than deal origination — creating delays, missed follow-ups, and risk of miscommunication.
✕
No systematic lead nurture or post-closing re-engagement — all client communication is manual and personalised, with no automated sequences for past clients or warm leads.
 The firm has no systematic way to stay top-of-mind with the international referral network, re-engage past clients, or convert warm leads during quiet periods — leaving repeat business to chance.
✕
carinigroup.com is English-only, with no structured Italian or Spanish content — despite 50% of business coming from international buyers who search in their native language.
 The firm's strongest competitive advantage — multilingual, international service — is invisible in organic search. Italian and Spanish-speaking buyers cannot find Carini Group through Google in their own language.
✕
Monthly investor market reports are produced manually — no self-service portal, no live portfolio data, no ROI modelling tool for advisory clients.
 Manual report production consumes hours per investor client per month. As the advisory book grows, this becomes a hard ceiling on how many investors the firm can serve profitably.
