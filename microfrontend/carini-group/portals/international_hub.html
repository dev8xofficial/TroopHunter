1031 Exchange Guide
Defer taxes. Grow faster.

The 1031 Exchange allows you to defer capital gains taxes when reinvesting proceeds into a new property. Step-by-step, for international investors.

1
Sell Your Relinquished Property
Close on your existing investment property. The sale proceeds are held by a Qualified Intermediary (QI) — never touching your hands to maintain tax-deferred status.
⏱ Day 0 — Begin identification clock
2
Identify Replacement Property
You have 45 days to formally identify up to 3 replacement properties in writing. Carini Group's network gives you access to pre-market NYC opportunities within this window.
⏱ Day 1–45 — Identification period
3
Close on the Replacement Property
Complete the purchase of your replacement property within 180 days of the original sale. The QI transfers funds directly to the closing. You defer 100% of capital gains.
⏱ Day 46–180 — Exchange window
4
Report & Grow Tax-Free
File IRS Form 8824 with your tax return. Your capital gains are deferred — not eliminated, but rolled into the new asset, compounding your wealth without a tax drag.
⏱ Tax filing season — Complete exchange
