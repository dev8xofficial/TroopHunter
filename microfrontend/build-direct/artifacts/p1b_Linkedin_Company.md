Overview
BuildDirect is a leading flooring and building materials supplier serving both professionals and homeowners across North America. We focus on delivering high-quality, job-ready flooring solutions with the speed, reliability, and pricing today’s contractors, builders, developers, and investors need to win more projects.
Our curated assortment includes waterproof vinyl, laminate, engineered hardwood, tile, carpet and specialty surfaces designed for real-world performance in residential and commercial spaces. We simplify sourcing with transparent pricing, bulk purchasing, and a streamlined digital experience that makes it easy to quote, order, and manage projects from anywhere.
For Pros, BuildDirect provides dedicated account support, jobsite delivery, fast lead times, and access to investor and contractor-focused programs that improve margins, reduce risk, and keep projects on schedule. From rental turnover and multi-family renovations to new construction and retail tenant improvements, we partner with customers at every stage of the build.
For homeowners, we offer expert guidance, curated collections, and sample programs that make choosing the right floor simple and confident. Our goal is to remove friction from the flooring journey while delivering lasting value.
With physical Pro Centers, a growing digital platform, and a relentless focus on customer experience, BuildDirect is redefining how flooring is sourced and delivered, combining technology, service, and supply chain strength to support the next generation of building professionals.
