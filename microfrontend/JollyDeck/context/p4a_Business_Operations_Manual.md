## 1. Company Overview

### 1.1 Business Name

JollyDeck (legally registered as JollyDeck Limited)

### 1.2 Industry

Learning Technology (Learning & Development / EdTech). JollyDeck makes software for workplace training. It sits inside the broader e-learning tools market.

### 1.3 Core Products or Services

JollyDeck sells two connected software products:

- **JollyDeck Create** — A tool for building e-learning content. Users build courses, quizzes, checklists, presentations, and video lessons inside the browser. No coding or design skills needed.
- **JollyDeck LMS** — A system for publishing and running those courses. It handles user enrolment, progress tracking, and reporting.

It also offers:

- **PowerPoint to Course Converter** — A tool that turns existing PowerPoint slides into an interactive e-learning course automatically using AI.
- **AI Copilot** — An AI assistant built into JollyDeck Create. It helps authors write copy, find images, convert slides, generate videos, and translate content.
- **Ready-made Compliance Content** — Off-the-shelf course content covering GDPR, anti-bribery, information security, conflicts of interest, and respect in the workplace. These are pre-built courses that organisations can buy and customise.

### 1.4 Target Customers

JollyDeck sells to organisations that need to train their employees. Its main buyers are Learning & Development (L&D) teams, HR managers, compliance officers, and instructional designers inside companies. It also serves independent instructional design consultants and freelancers.

Known enterprise clients include Deutsche Bahn, Vodafone, and Kingfisher plc. JollyDeck also works with over 200 smaller organisations across all continents.

### 1.5 Value Proposition

JollyDeck's core promise is speed without sacrifice. It lets L&D professionals build professional-quality e-learning much faster than traditional methods. The CEO frames it clearly: lean tools that do exactly what you need, with no bloat, no long contracts, and no hidden fees.

Key promises:
- Build courses faster (claimed up to 50% time saving vs traditional methods).
- No coding or design skills needed.
- One platform covers authoring, publishing, managing, and tracking.
- Costs less than 1% of a typical training project budget.
- Works in 14 languages. More coming.
- Runs on dedicated infrastructure — not a third-party cloud. Claims 100% uptime.

### 1.6 Business Model

JollyDeck makes money through software subscriptions (SaaS). Users pay a recurring fee to access JollyDeck Create and JollyDeck LMS.

**Pricing model:**

| Plan | Who It Is For | Key Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Testing and small pilots | 3,000 AI tokens/month, Live SCORM only |
| Solo | Individual L&D professionals or consultants | 20,000 AI tokens/month, Full SCORM export |
| Teams | Multi-author organisations needing collaboration and SSO | 30,000 AI tokens/month, minimum 2 seats |
| Enterprise | Large regulated organisations needing SLAs, DPAs, and APIs | Custom token pool, negotiated terms |

- Payments are monthly or annual, processed through **Stripe**.
- No refunds on cancellation.
- Free trials available at JollyDeck's discretion.
- Enterprise plans are sold through direct contact with the sales team. All other plans are self-serve online.
- Additional AI tokens can be purchased separately by any active account holder.
- Reviewers (people who only comment on content) are free on all paid plans.
- Learners who access courses through a customer's own LMS do not require a JollyDeck licence.

**Additional revenue streams:**
- Off-the-shelf compliance content packs (sold separately or bundled).
- AI token top-ups (purchased on demand).

## 2. Customer Segments

### 2.1 Ideal Customer Profile

The ideal customer is a company that:
- Has an internal L&D or HR function responsible for employee training.
- Needs to produce or update training content on a regular basis.
- Is frustrated by the cost or complexity of existing tools.
- Has compliance training obligations (GDPR, information security, anti-bribery, etc.).
- Operates across multiple locations or countries, often needing multilingual content.

### 2.2 Customer Industries

From evidence in the files, JollyDeck serves customers in:
- Rail and transport (Deutsche Bahn)
- Telecommunications (Vodafone)
- Retail and home improvement (Kingfisher plc)
- Any regulated industry with compliance training requirements

The platform is industry-agnostic. Any organisation that trains employees at scale is a potential customer.

### 2.3 Customer Size

JollyDeck works with both small and large organisations.

- **Small** — Solo instructional designers or small L&D teams. The Solo plan is designed for this.
- **Mid-size** — Teams of 2 or more authors who collaborate on content. The Teams plan covers this.
- **Enterprise** — Large organisations with procurement processes, legal requirements, and the need for data protection agreements. Kingfisher trained 75,000 employees across 12 countries using JollyDeck.

### 2.4 Common Customer Problems

- Building e-learning is slow and expensive using traditional tools.
- Existing platforms are too complex or too bloated for daily use.
- Compliance training needs updating constantly as regulations change.
- Content review is chaotic — feedback arrives via email, Slack, and old file versions simultaneously.
- Translating and localising content for multiple countries is painful and inconsistent.
- Video content is expensive to produce in a studio. Keeping it updated is even harder.
- Teams use too many separate tools for authoring, storing, reviewing, and publishing.

### 2.5 Typical Customer Goals

- Produce high-quality training content faster and at lower cost.
- Ensure employees complete required compliance training on time.
- Onboard new employees consistently across all locations.
- Keep content up to date as products, processes, or regulations change.
- Roll out training in multiple languages with consistent terminology.
- Track who has completed training and flag who has not.

- **Free Trial** — Available beyond the free plan at JollyDeck's discretion.

### 5.4 Key Operational Workflows

**Content localisation workflow:**
1. Product update or new feature creates new UI strings and system emails.
2. JollyDeck packages strings for translation (approx. 200 terms, 60 system emails, plus additional phrases).
3. Freelance translators are hired directly via Upwork (no agencies).
4. Translated strings are reviewed and integrated back into the platform.
5. This cycle repeats every few months as the product evolves.

**Compliance content update workflow:**
1. Regulatory changes (e.g. GDPR updates) trigger a content review.
2. Waypoint GRC (external compliance partner) reviews and updates course content.
3. Updated content is made available to existing customers.

**Customer review and feedback workflow (within the platform):**
1. Author creates content inside JollyDeck Create.
2. Author invites reviewers (unlimited, no licence needed).
3. Reviewers leave feedback directly inside the content.
4. Author iterates based on inline feedback.
5. Final content is published through one of the available channels.

**Platform infrastructure:**
- JollyDeck runs on its own dedicated Tier IV EU infrastructure (not AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud).
- This is managed internally and is positioned as a reliability and data security advantage.

## 14. Summary of Business Operations

JollyDeck is a small UK-based software company with about 5 employees. It makes tools for building and running workplace training programmes. Its two main products are JollyDeck Create (a course-building tool) and JollyDeck LMS (a system for distributing and tracking training).

The company sells software subscriptions. Plans range from free to enterprise. Most customers sign up and pay online without ever speaking to anyone at JollyDeck. Large organisations (enterprise) contact the team directly to negotiate terms.

JollyDeck attracts customers mainly through its free plan and through LinkedIn content. The CEO and the company page post regularly. The content is practical and aimed at L&D professionals. There is no evidence of paid advertising.

The product is delivered entirely through the cloud. It runs on its own dedicated servers in the EU. Customers log in, build their content, and publish it. JollyDeck maintains the platform and releases new features continuously — multiple times per month.

The team is very small. The CEO handles strategy, partnerships, and enterprise sales. The Chief of Platform handles the technical side. Three other team members cover the remaining functions. Some work — particularly translation for new languages — is outsourced to freelancers via Upwork.

The company is actively growing its product. In the past year it has added AI video with avatars, a translation workflow system, a modular video tutorial builder, branding tools, and PDF export. Each of these was launched and announced on LinkedIn.

For an external B2B partner, the clearest gaps are:

- **Outbound sales** — There is no outbound function. The pipeline is almost entirely inbound.
- **Marketing scale** — Good content but a small audience. 551 LinkedIn followers for a company with 200+ clients and 10+ years of operation.
- **CRM and marketing automation** — No system documented for managing leads or nurturing free users toward paid plans.
- **Translation** — An active, recurring need that grows with every product update.
- **Customer support** — Likely under-resourced relative to platform growth.

The business is operationally lean by design. The CEO explicitly positions leanness as a strength — both for JollyDeck as a company and as a product philosophy. Any B2B partner proposing services should frame their value in terms of speed, simplicity, and measurable output — not complexity or overhead.
