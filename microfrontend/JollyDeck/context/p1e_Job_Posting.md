# English to German: Localisation of an e-learning platform — JollyDeck — Job Posting

## Role Summary

JollyDeck is a workplace e-learning platform used by organisations in more than 20 languages across every continent. The company is seeking a long-term freelance collaborator to localise its platform into German — covering the software interface, system-generated emails, and ongoing updates as the product evolves. This is not a one-off project; ongoing work is expected every few months due to continuous product development. JollyDeck works directly with translators, not through agencies.

## Responsibilities

- Translate and localise the software interface and system emails from English to German.
- Translate approximately 200 key terms and phrases, with time allocated to become familiar with the platform and its tone.
- Translate 60 system-generated emails.
- Translate a few hundred complex lines and phrases.
- Complete ongoing translation work every few months as product updates require.

## Requirements

##### Must Have

- Native German speaker.
- Proficient in UK English.
- Proven experience translating software interfaces or digital products (references or samples required).
- Reliability and adherence to deadlines.
- Strong communication skills and a collaborative mindset.

##### Nice to Have

- Portfolio or selection of work demonstrating quality writing — software translation not required; marketing, advertising, or internal communications samples also accepted.
- Familiarity with JollyDeck (candidates are asked to explore www.jollydeck.com before applying).
