💡 AI video generation is changing the game for instructional designers. It enables you to create professional-looking video content in a fraction of the time it once took. 

But like any tool, success depends on how you use it. 

Here are some practical tips to make AI-generated videos truly add value to your courses:

1️⃣ Don’t just convert course content into video
 AI videos are not a standalone learning format. They work best as an add-on to existing content, for example to:

►Deliver an important message for learners.
► Present a specific case or example.
► Reinforce knowledge from another perspective.

2️⃣ Keep videos short and focused
 Just because you can generate hours of video doesn’t mean you should. Learners switch off if an avatar speaks too long without change. Aim for 1–3 minute clips that tackle a single point. This keeps the pace lively and aligns with microlearning.

3️⃣ Match avatar use to the learning goal
Use avatars where a face and voice add clarity or presence:

►Explaining processes in plain language.
►Presenting real-life scenarios to make content relatable.
►Delivering course intros or wrap-ups.

Avoid avatars when text, diagrams, or interactivity work better.

4️⃣ Blend with other media

 AI avatar videos are most powerful when combined with:
►Screen recordings for software training.
►Infographics for complex information.
►Interactive activities for applied practice.

This prevents fatigue and ensures avatars guide the learner, not dominate the course.

5️⃣ Use dynamic framing and scene changes
Static, single-shot avatars can feel flat. Add variety with:
► Different camera angles.
► Supporting visuals like slides or graphics.
► Background or layout changes.

6️⃣ Pay attention to tone and style
Match the avatar’s look and voice to the audience. A formal, suited avatar may suit compliance training, while a relaxed style works better for creative courses. With tools like JollyDeck Interactive Video, you can adjust narrators, voice speed, and more to fit your organisation’s culture.

---

✅ AI video generation isn’t about replacing your content - it’s about enhancing it. Used wisely, avatars can transform courses into engaging, modern learning experiences.
