AI video generation is changing the game for instructional designers — enabling professional-looking video content in a fraction of the time. But success depends on how you use it.

Practical tips for making AI-generated videos add value to courses:

1. Don't just convert course content into video — AI videos work best as an add-on to deliver a message, present a case, or reinforce knowledge from another perspective.
2. Keep videos short and focused — aim for 1–3 minute clips tackling a single point.
3. Match avatar use to the learning goal — use avatars to explain processes, present real-life scenarios, or deliver course intros and wrap-ups. Avoid where text, diagrams, or interactivity work better.
4. Blend with other media — combine with screen recordings, infographics, and interactive activities to prevent fatigue.
5. Use dynamic framing and scene changes — add different camera angles, supporting visuals, and background or layout changes to prevent flat, static delivery.
6. Pay attention to tone and style — match the avatar's look and voice to the audience. A formal avatar suits compliance training; a relaxed style works better for creative courses.

AI video generation isn't about replacing content — it's about enhancing it.
