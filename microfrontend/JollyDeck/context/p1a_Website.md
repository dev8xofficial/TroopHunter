## Page 8 — Clear plans. Transparent pricing. JollyDeck

**🔗 URL:** https://www.jollydeck.com/pricing

> The Free plan remains permanently free, and our paid plans provide great value for individuals, freelancers, agencies, and large businesses.

- Free: Testing, experimentation, small internal pilots.
- Solo Professional instructional designers, consultants, or solo L&D managers creating commercial-ready SCORM content.
- Teams: Organisations where multiple authors collaborate, require SSO, or need centralised user management.
- Enterprise: Regulated environments, procurement-heavy organisations, or those requiring SLAs, DPA, APIs, and audit workflows.

- Reviewers: No licence is required for colleagues who only need to review content or leave comments. You can invite unlimited reviewers on all paid plans.
- Learners: If you are exporting content to your own LMS, you do not need to pay for the learners who view the course.

- Solo: Designed for a single author. It includes unlimited projects and full export capabilities but does not allow for co-authoring (multiple people editing content on the same accout).
- Teams: Unlocks co-authoring, allowing your team to collaborate on projects. It also includes Single Sign-On (SSO), branded templates, and centralized user management. Note that the Teams plan requires a minimum of 2 seats.

- Free Plan: 3,000 tokens/month (sufficient for testing).
- Solo Plan: 20,000 tokens/month.
- Teams Plan: 30,000 tokens/month.
- Enterprise: Custom token pools per agreement.

- Free: Includes the Lite version.
- Solo: Includes the Plus version (faster processing, better formatting).
- Teams:Includes the Max version for the most advanced document conversion capabilities.

- Live SCORM (Free plan): A lightweight file that links back to JollyDeck. It allows for instant updates (publish once, update everywhere) but requires an internet connection for learners.
- Full SCORM (Solo & Teams): A standalone package containing all media and assets. It is fully offline-compatible and ensures you have a permanent hard copy of your course that you truly own..
