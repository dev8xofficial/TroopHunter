# Website in WordPress — NexBiome — Job Posting

## Role Summary

NexBiome is a probiotics brand seeking a WordPress specialist to rescue and rebuild its existing website (nexbiome.life). The current site was poorly executed by a prior contractor and requires either a full remediation or a ground-up rebuild. The specialist will align the site's visual style across all sections, incorporate the existing logo and brand colour palette, and implement design direction informed by reference templates and visual mockups supplied by the client. The primary outcome is a fully functional, brand-consistent WordPress site ready for public use.

## Responsibilities

- Audit the existing nexbiome.life WordPress site and assess remediation vs. rebuild feasibility.
- Fix or rebuild the site so all sections match in style, layout, and brand identity.
- Implement the client's approved logo, colour palette, and design language throughout.
- Apply design direction from seven client-supplied reference attachments (webp mockups, SVG logo, and a homepage video).
- Ensure all site sections — home/video, blog, "Who We Are", "Buy Online", and "Contact Us" — are functional and visually consistent.

## Requirements

##### Must Have

- Proven WordPress development and design experience.
- Proficiency with Elementor page builder.
- Ability to match and apply an existing brand identity (logo, colours, style) across a full site.
- Experience with website redesign and remediation projects.

##### Nice to Have

- Copywriting or sales copy experience (WordPress Landing Page, Sales Funnel Copywriting, Ad Copy).
- Email copywriting capability.
