## 7 — Odour Control Units (OCU's)

url: https://cobrahydrouk.co.uk/odour-control-units-ocus/
type: product
meta: Odour Control Units (OCU's) — large carbon filter units for industrial odour extraction and neutralisation.

### Odour Control Units (OCU's)

Also known as "Big Bobby" carbon filters, OCU's operate on two principles: extraction of odour or gas, and neutralisation via adsorption using activated charcoal media. Manufactured from HDPE material, resistant to chemicals and gases.

### Components

**Control Panel** — Regulates ATEX fan functions from 25 to 100% efficiency. Signals OCU functions via Modbus, RS or GSM.

**Extraction Fans** — ATEX rated; air flow capability from 150 m³/hour to 30,000 m³/hour. A second fan performs dry scrubbing through activated carbon media.

**CE Certificated Gas Sensors** — Monitors Hydrogen Sulphide, Methane, and Ammonia up to 300ppm.

**Mass Tensometers** — Up to four per OCU; accurately measure carbon media mass, providing efficiency data on the activated charcoal.

## 24 — Odour Management

url: https://cobrahydrouk.co.uk/odour-managment/
type: utility
meta: Odour management is a set of measures and technologies aimed at controlling, reducing, and eliminating unpleasant odours in the environment.

### Odour Management Overview

Odour management encompasses measures and technologies to control, reduce, and eliminate unpleasant odours. Key components include:

#### Odour Control Technologies

Biofilters, charcoal filters, and chemical scrubbers are among the main technologies used to neutralise and reduce odour intensity.

#### Measurement

Olfactometry (using trained human assessors) and chemical analysis are used to assess odour levels and concentration of odorous substances.

#### Dispersion Modelling

Computer models predict odour dispersion based on meteorological conditions and landscape features to support management strategy development.

#### Management Plans

Effective plans combine technological and organisational measures, including regular monitoring, environmental impact assessment, and mitigation activities.

#### Environmental Regulations

Odour management is governed by environmental laws that establish permissible odour levels and prescribe control measures to protect human health and the environment.

#### Impact Assessment

Analysis of odour effects on the environment and human health; identifies and eliminates key odour sources.

#### Mitigation Strategies

Changing production processes, improving ventilation, and installing filters to reduce odour sources and environmental impact.

#### Complaints

Analysing and responding to odour complaints enables quick identification and elimination of odour sources.

### Product Description

Cobra ANL is a liquid blend of pre-selected autotrophic microbes that convert sulphides into elemental sulphur, removing sulphide content from sewage water. The microbial formulation contains bacteria capable of degrading volatile fatty acids and converting sulphides into elemental sulphur. The volatile fatty acid bacteria use acids as a food source, converting them to new biomass and CO2, reducing septic odours. The sulphide bacteria neutralise H2S (rotten egg odour), using inorganic carbon and sulphides to grow and reproduce.
