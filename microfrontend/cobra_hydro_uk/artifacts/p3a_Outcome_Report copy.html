Know Your Team

We're More Than a
Real Estate Agency

Founded by Jahcorrian Burkes — a licensed real estate agent and mortgage lender — The Burkes Group was built on a powerful premise: your transaction shouldn't require two separate teams pulling in different directions. We unified both disciplines under one roof so clients experience a seamless, stress-free journey from consultation to close.

Based in The Woodlands, Texas, we serve first-time buyers, seasoned investors, sellers seeking maximum return, divorce attorneys, CPAs, and self-employed professionals throughout Greater Houston. Our dual expertise means faster approvals, better-coordinated closings, and a long-term partner for your real estate wealth strategy.
