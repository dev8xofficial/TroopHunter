# WordPress Specialist - Site Refresh & Landing Page Creation — Cobra Hydro UK — Job Posting

## Role Summary

Cobra Hydro UK (www.cobrahydro.co.uk) is an industrial/service-sector business seeking a WordPress specialist to refresh their existing site and build targeted landing pages. The site currently runs on the Astra theme with Elementor and is perceived as a basic template that no longer reflects the brand's growth. The specialist is responsible for delivering a modernised, professional web presence — including high-converting landing pages — and handing off a site the client team can self-manage.

## Responsibilities

- Refresh the visual design of the existing Astra/Elementor site to achieve a modern, professional look consistent with an industrial brand identity.
- Build a high-converting landing page for Google Ads campaigns.
- Build a landing page for recruitment and onboarding of "Emergency Water Drivers."
- Optimise the site for speed and full mobile responsiveness.
- Set up and conduct all work within a staging/development environment; migrate to live only after client approval.
- Deliver a clean hand-off so the client can independently manage and edit pages post-project.

## Requirements

##### Must Have

- Expert-level WordPress experience.
- Proven experience setting up and working within staging environments.
- Strong UI/UX judgement.
- Fluent English.
- Proposal must begin with the word "WATER" to confirm the brief has been read.

##### Nice to Have

- Industrial or service-sector design experience.
- Portfolio includes service-based landing pages or recruitment pages (2–3 examples required with application).
