## Discovery Questions

*Prioritised by impact on Confidence Signal upgrade (L4 → L1).*

**Tier 1 — L4 → L1 metric upgrades (ask in the first 10 minutes):**

1. "How much are you currently spending per month on Google Ads, and do you have a sense of your current enquiry-to-lead conversion rate from that traffic?" *(Unlocks: cost of inaction for Problem 1; validates urgency of LP build; upgrades ROI section from proxy to sourced)*

2. "How many inbound enquiries do you currently receive per month through the website, and roughly what proportion comes from the website vs. phone, LinkedIn, or referral?" *(Unlocks: baseline for measuring LP impact; validates or refutes whether the site is currently contributing to pipeline at all)*

3. "How many hours per month does your team currently spend on website edits or coordinating with a developer for changes?" *(Unlocks: handoff efficiency claim — if the answer is >4 hrs/month, this becomes a sourced ROI argument for the self-management deliverable)*

**Tier 2 — bottleneck validation:**

4. "When a prospect contacts you after hearing about the BBC segment or one of the award wins — what page do you point them to, and do you feel the site supports that conversation or works against it?" *(Validates Problem 2 — invisible credibility — directly in the buyer's own words; generates quotable language for the proposal)*

5. "Is the Google Ads campaign currently running, and if so, where is it currently pointing traffic — homepage, a specific product page, or somewhere else?" *(Validates or refutes partial confirmation in Decision Card §4 — if the campaign is live and pointing to homepage, the cost-of-inaction argument can be made immediately)*

6. "On the Bio Block product — are you currently running any paid or outbound activity off the back of the BBC feature and GRA win, or is the inbound largely organic at the moment?" *(Validates Problem 5 commercial urgency; helps size the Bio Block LP as a paid-traffic destination vs. SEO asset vs. both)*

**Tier 3 — stakeholder and process:**

7. "Who on your team will be the day-to-day owner of the site after handoff — and what's their current comfort level with Elementor?" *(Confirms whether Holly Templeton or another team member is the primary Elementor user; sizes the training/handoff deliverable correctly)*

8. "Is Cara the sign-off on the visual direction, or does that go through the broader team?" *(Identifies the approval chain for the staging review; prevents the Decision Risk from materialising — see Decision Card §5)*

9. "On the compliance and regulatory side — are you seeing more enquiries now from environment managers or compliance officers compared to two or three years ago, since the H4 guidance changes?" *(Validates Problem 6; if yes, upgrades compliance messaging from a supporting claim to a primary product page requirement)*

10. "What's the current process for the Emergency Water Driver recruitment — are you fielding enquiries manually, or is there already some form or process in place?" *(Validates Problem 3 and sizes the operational relief argument for the recruitment LP; determines whether Georgia or Lisa owns this)*
