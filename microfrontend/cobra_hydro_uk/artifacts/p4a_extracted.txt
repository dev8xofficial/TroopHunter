1.2 Industry
Cobra Hydro UK operates in the environmental utilities sector. The company specialises in managing odour, dust, fat/oil/grease (FOG), and noise at industrial and wastewater sites.
1.3 Core Products or Services
The company does four main things:
Odour control — stops bad smells escaping from industrial sites
Dust suppression — prevents dust clouds on construction, quarry, and waste sites
FOG management — breaks down fat, oil, and grease in sewers and wet wells
Noise control — reduces sound levels on construction or engineering sites
Products and systems are sold or hired. The company also designs and installs bespoke systems, provides service contracts, and sells chemical treatment products such as Atom Neutraliser.
1.4 Target Customers
Cobra Hydro UK serves B2B clients only. Typical buyers include:
Water utilities (Yorkshire Water, Southern Water, South West Water)
Waste management and recycling companies (Biffa, Veolia, Yellowstone Environmental)
Construction and demolition firms
Quarry operators

Theme parks, leisure facilities, and food industry sites
1.5 Value Proposition

The company provides a full service from initial survey through installation and ongoing maintenance. Clients get one supplier for equipment, chemicals, and servicing.

3.3 Product Features
CHEMICAL PRODUCTS:
Atom Neutraliser — organic, non-toxic odour neutraliser. Available in 12 scents and 4 strengths. Breaks down odour molecules rather than masking them. Used in misting systems, Odourmasters, and dry vapour units.
Anaerobic Neutralising Liquid (ANL) — microbial liquid that converts sulphides (H2S, rotten egg smell) into harmless elemental sulphur. Dosed directly into sewage.
Bio Block — a solid block that hangs in a wet well. Water turbulence erodes it slowly and doses the whole system 24/7. No power, no storage tank, no dosing equipment needed. Full effect after 3–4 weeks.
Oxi Max — food-grade, non-toxic neutralising liquid for use with dry vapour systems.
Oxi Foam — foam layer for short-term odour and dust control. Ideal for waste stockpiles and remediated ground. Available in biodegradable scents.
SYSTEMS AND EQUIPMENT:
High Pressure Misting System (fixed) — perimeter nozzle ring around a site boundary. Dosing system injects Atom Neutraliser into water supply. Fine mist neutralises airborne odours up to 20m. Self-cleaning nozzles. Optional wind sensor.
Mobile High Pressure Misting System — same as above but on a self-contained 2000L bowser. 50m or 100m of nozzle pipework included.
Odourmaster / Mobile Odourmaster — rotary atomiser on a steel mast. Oscillates 100 degrees. 30–45m throw. Available wall-mounted, ceiling-mounted, or mobile (1000L bowser with generator).
Cobra HydroJet — 3-in-1 dust and odour unit. 2000L self-contained bowser. 30–40m throw mast, road spray bar, and high-pressure jet wash. Towable.
Dust Suppression Fans (DS50 / DS100) — large oscillating fans with water misting. DS50: 50m throw, 4500m² coverage. DS100: up to 100m throw. Remote-controlled. Wind speed/direction sensor option.
Dry Vapour System — waterless odour control. Fluid evaporates through perforated pipework. No water supply needed. Emergency response capable. Can be used in buildings, open yards, exhaust stacks, and ventilation systems.
Foam Master — industrial 2000L diesel-powered bowser with 20m fire hose. Sprays foam 20–25m. Road-towable.
Odour Control Units (OCU) / 'Big Bobby' — large HDPE carbon filter units. ATEX-rated extraction fans (150–30,000 m³/hr). Modbus/RS/GSM control panel. CE-certified gas sensors for H2S, methane, ammonia.
Anti Odour Carbon Filter — passive or fan-drawn manhole/vent stack filter. Up to 5-year life. Quick installation.
Acoustic Sound Barrier — sound-absorbing composite panels. Clips to temporary fencing. Up to 30dB noise reduction.

14. Summary of Business Operations
Cobra Hydro UK is a small, specialist environmental services company based in Romsey, Hampshire. It has about 14 staff. The company has been operating for over 20 years.
The company solves four problems for industrial and infrastructure sites:
Bad smells (odour control)
Dust clouds (dust suppression)
Fat and grease blocking sewers (FOG management)
Noise on construction sites (acoustic barriers)
It designs its own equipment, blends its own chemicals, and does its own installations. This is unusual. Most competitors either make equipment or sell chemicals. Cobra does both — and adds installation and servicing too.
Its main customers are water utilities, waste companies, and construction firms. These are B2B clients with compliance obligations. They need solutions that work reliably and hold up to regulatory scrutiny.
Sales start with a free site survey. The technical team visits the site, assesses the problem, and writes a recommended solution. This is the main way the company wins business. The pipeline is: enquiry → survey → proposal → contract → installation → ongoing servicing.
After installation, clients stay with Cobra through service contracts (regular maintenance visits) and ongoing chemical purchases (Atom Neutraliser, Bio Block, etc.). These recurring revenues keep the business stable.
The company is growing. It won a Global Recognition Award for Innovation in 2026. It has been featured on BBC Morning Live. It won two awards at the Test Valley Business Awards in 2025.
The team is small but experienced. The Managing Director (Cara King) and two Business Development Directors (Mandy Barnes-Jackson and Clint Jackson) lead strategy and sales. A field team of two handles all installation and servicing. The office team manages operations, admin, and customer communication.
External service providers can add most value in: CRM and sales technology, outbound prospecting support, digital marketing, field service software, e-commerce for consumables, and remote monitoring systems.
