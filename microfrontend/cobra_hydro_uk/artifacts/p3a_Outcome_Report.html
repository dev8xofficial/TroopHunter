1 What This Project Achieves

Desired Outcome

What Gets Delivered

A modernised WordPress site and two high-converting landing pages — Google Ads and Emergency Water Driver recruitment — built on Astra/Elementor, reflecting 20+ years of industrial authority, and handed over so the internal team can self-manage content post-launch.

Why This Decision Is Happening Now

4 What Breaks in 12–24 Months If Nothing Changes

Forward Risk Register

Risk Horizon: 6–18 months

Widening credibility gap as awards and media stack up

Every new credibility event — award, BBC segment, product launch — published on LinkedIn but not the website compounds the gap between how the brand presents online and how it presents in person. Sophisticated B2B buyers who research before calling find a template site that contradicts the reputation they heard about.

Prevented by: Homepage credibility architecture and modular design system that makes adding new awards and media appearances a 10-minute edit.
Risk Horizon: 12–24 months

Post-H4 compliance buyers land on a site that speaks the wrong language

UK odour regulation is shifting post-H4 guidance withdrawal. A new buyer segment — environment managers, compliance officers — evaluates on regulatory outcomes, not product specs. The current site has zero compliance-framing across all 27 pages, while Cobra's LinkedIn already positions the brand as a compliance partner.

Prevented by: Sector-specific landing pages with compliance-outcome framing — the content already exists on LinkedIn, it needs a web destination.
Risk Horizon: 90 days

Bio Block's BBC awareness window closes without becoming a web asset

Media awareness spikes are short-lived unless converted into discoverable evergreen content. BBC Morning Live, Yorkshire Water validation, and GRA 2026 — three stacking credibility events — have no web anchor. Search and referral traffic from the BBC segment will decay without a conversion mechanism in place.

Prevented by: Bio Block authority page built now — turns a 90-day awareness spike into three years of inbound search value.
