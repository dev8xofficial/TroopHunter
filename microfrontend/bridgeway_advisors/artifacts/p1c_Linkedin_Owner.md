A huge thanks to all of the clients and finance professionals who supported Bridgeway Advisors' Spring Finance Bootcamp. We're especially grateful to Stefan Geldmeyer, Matt Allen, Patrick Cassidy, Jon Haygood, Michael Jensen, Katie McGahan, Ayana Collins, and Rich Levin for sharing their time, feedback, and industry insight with our students.

I believe in the power of Learning by Doing. Throughout the program, 50 college students took a deep dive into financial statement analysis, valuation, and professional presentation development. Each student analyzed either AbbVie, Eli Lilly and Company, or Walmart, built a five year earnings forecast, and presented their conclusions to senior finance professionals.

This program reflects Bridgeway's commitment to expand access to high-quality training, practical finance experience, and mentorship opportunities for aspiring students from all backgrounds.

A Bootcamp Finalist from the University of Georgia shared that the experience meaningfully strengthened their understanding of financial analysis and boosted their confidence in forecasting and evaluating firm performance.

We appreciate everyone who helped make this experience possible.
