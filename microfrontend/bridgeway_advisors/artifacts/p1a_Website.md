### The Bridgeway Internship

Bridgeway Advisors is a Black-owned investment bank based in Austin, TX. The company specializes in capital markets transactions and corporate finance advisory for Fortune 500 clients. The summer internship program gives freshmen and sophomores (2027 & 2028 college graduates) an opportunity to gain work experience in investment banking. This is a paid internship lasting 6-8 weeks.

#### Internship Description
Participants will work through a series of corporate finance and capital markets projects, gaining hands-on training in the core activities of an analyst working in investment banking and capital markets. Interns will work closely with Bridgeway's debt capital markets team, supporting the origination and execution of financing transactions for Fortune 500 clients.

The internship also offers opportunities to network with clients and colleagues at top global investment banks. Upon completion, students will gain the knowledge and skills needed to secure and excel in full-time investment banking positions.

#### Details of Intern's Role
Candidates should expect to develop key skills and experience in the following:

- Develop knowledge of the financial markets and utilize corporate finance and accounting skills to advise corporate debt issuers on optimal financing and refinancing opportunities
- Assist the debt capital markets team in day-to-day functions, including:
  - Preparing financial analysis and models using Excel
  - Preparing client pitches, presentations, and market updates using PowerPoint
  - Conducting economic and financial research using (Factset, Bloomberg?)
  - Participating in meetings with investment bankers and clients
- Receive significant training and mentoring from senior bankers to maximize learning and experience

#### Who Should Apply
- Freshman and sophomores (2027 & 2028 college graduates)
- Highly motivated candidates with a strong interest in finance and/or business
- Resourceful candidates with a natural curiosity for how things work, a tenacious attitude toward research, and the ability to think independently
- Clear and concise written and verbal communication skills
- Strong interpersonal skills and ability to work in a collaborative environment
- Ability to work through multiple tasks in a rapidly changing environment to meet deadlines
- Able to commit a minimum of 10-15 hours a week for 6-8 weeks
- Available to start on or before June XX, 2025
- Option to work remotely or out of Austin, TX or New York, NY

#### How to Apply
Interested candidates should reply with a copy of their resume and a brief description of why they would be a great candidate for this internship (250-word limit). Bridgeway will respond to top candidates within 1-2 weeks.
