#### About us

Aligned Hospice is comprised of empathetic and knowledgeable healthcare workers with a passion for helping patients during their end of life journey. We provide expert interdisciplinary care, consultation, support, and education for those affected by serious illness, death, and grief. Our team is committed to understanding each patients unique and individual needs. At Aligned Hospice we know that you or your loved one are more than your terminal diagnosis. We strive to know our patients, their family, and caregivers on a personal level. Making the decision to sign on to hospice is never an easy one; we are hear to support, respect, and honor your decisions during this sacred time.

One of the greatest differentiators for Aligned Hospice is that our (HMD) Hospice Medical Directors (the MD overseeing the care) will attend an in home visit. Our HMD will stay involved directly with the family and even share their direct cell phone number. 

The HMD at most hospice agencies typically play a supervisory and consultative role, rather than visiting patients in person. 

Nationally, the HMD public utilization data reflects between 0–5% of all hospice patients receive these HMD visits directly. More common is utilization of an Nurse Practitioner (NP) for visits reserved specifically for Recertification.

Medicare does not mandate a specific number of HMD visits, this is an enhancement we choose to provide due to our local experience in San Diego County.
