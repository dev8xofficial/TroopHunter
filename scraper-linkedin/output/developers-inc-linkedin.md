This week, we inaugurated Devsinc’s Daycare Center and Girls Common Room.
For me, this is not just another facility. It’s something I’ve wanted to do from the very beginning.

Since day one, my intention has been simple: if someone is going to spend eight or more hours of their day, five days a week at work, they should feel safe, comfortable, and respected here. Work should not feel like a place you’re forced to be at. It should feel like a space where you can breathe.

Pakistan has nearly 117 million women, almost half of our population. When half of a country exists as women, you cannot build systems that ignore their realities, especially in a work environment.

When Devsinc started, we had very little female representation. Over time, that changed, and honestly, it changed us for the better. Today, I feel proud knowing that women trust Devsinc enough to give us their time, energy, and careers.

Creating a safe space for women has always been close to my heart. That’s why we started with door-to-door pick and drop, so they could travel without fear, no matter what time they left the office. And now, with the daycare and resting area, working mothers don’t have to choose between being present at work and being present for their children.

Independence shouldn’t come at the cost of anxiety. And ambition shouldn’t mean guilt.

This is a small step, but it’s a sincere one.
Here’s to building workplaces where people, especially women, feel safe, seen, and supported.

For me, this is not just another facility. It’s something I’ve wanted to do from the very beginning.

Since day one, my intention has been simple: if someone is going to spend eight or more hours of their day, five days a week at work, they should feel safe, comfortable, and respected here. Work should not feel like a place you’re forced to be at. It should feel like a space where you can breathe.

Pakistan has nearly 117 million women, almost half of our population. When half of a country exists as women, you cannot build systems that ignore their realities, especially in a work environment.

When Devsinc started, we had very little female representation. Over time, that changed, and honestly, it changed us for the better. Today, I feel proud knowing that women trust Devsinc enough to give us their time, energy, and careers.

Creating a safe space for women has always been close to my heart. That’s why we started with door-to-door pick and drop, so they could travel without fear, no matter what time they left the office. And now, with the daycare and resting area, working mothers don’t have to choose between being present at work and being present for their children.

Independence shouldn’t come at the cost of anxiety. And ambition shouldn’t mean guilt.
