# The End Goal

This document defines the ratified target state for the Autonomous Design Engine (ADE). It is split into two sections: the **Bounded Goal** (what we are actually building toward now) and the **North Star** (the aspirational clauses and their required preconditions).

## The Bounded Goal

*The ratified near-horizon target, defined by contract.*

ADE autonomously produces multi-section marketing artifacts from a brief under frozen brand law: measurably improving within each run **beyond matched-compute resampling**, consistent without monotony, rated good-or-close by its human at a steady majority **and periodically bench-marked against external work**; its judge's agreement with its human is measured per boundary and per difficulty stratum, and human gates relax only where that evidence - plus standing audits - say so; its compounding assets (verdict corpus, golden core, strategy-decision data, own-client memory) are model-independent and survive substrate succession; and its weaknesses are hunted by a named, running self-investigation mechanism.

**Scope Adjustment (D2):** ADE's target is strictly **composition/UI-design intelligence** (layout, typography, color, componentry under brand law). Copy, imagery/art-direction, iconography, and motion authoring are **out of scope**. Complex design workflows (multi-page, forms, email, dashboards) are outside the current architecture.

**"Good Enough" Threshold (The Brake):**
The threshold at which the owner would ship ADE output on a real section instead of hand-building it. A section is considered "good enough to actually use for real work" when:
1. It passes the deterministic floor unconditionally.
2. The UI composition requires zero to trivial layout fixes to look professional.
3. The brand identity is accurately and safely applied without hallucinations.

## The North Star

*The aspirational capabilities, and the conditions under which they become achievable.*

- **Strategic Autonomy (EG-2)**: Requires funding the strategy layer (M15/E2.4) and a real, empirical research capability.
- **Complete Solutions & World-Class Org (EG-3/EG-9)**: Under the default scope (D2), these remain permanently unreachable. Achieving them requires funding explicit unmet preconditions — the "missing chairs" (copy co-optimization, imagery & art direction, iconography, and motion authoring) — alongside obtaining external-anchor evidence and establishing signal economics beyond one sole rater.
- **Style Durability (EG-7)**: Requires substrate-succession discipline exercised over years.
- **Cross-Artifact Orchestration (EG-8)**: Requires a workflow architecture that does not currently exist.
- **Compound Intelligence (EG-11)**: The *system's* intelligence compounds through data, anchors, and substrate succession, even though no single model weight improves itself.
