Purpose

This document defines the standard structure that every research project must follow.

Rather than inventing a new process for each investigation, every research effort begins from a common template. This ensures consistency, comparability, traceability, and methodological rigor across all research conducted within ADE.

The template captures not only the conclusions of a study, but also the reasoning, evidence, disagreements, assumptions, and architectural implications that led to those conclusions.

Objectives

The research template exists to:

Standardize every research investigation.
Ensure all research follows the same methodology.
Improve reproducibility.
Make research easier to review.
Preserve architectural knowledge.
Support future comparison between research projects.
Standard Research Structure

Every research project should contain the following sections:

Research Definition

Defines the problem being investigated.

Includes:

research title
objective
motivation
architectural context
expected contribution
Background

Explains the current system.

Documents:

existing architecture
existing assumptions
previous decisions
related research
known limitations
Research Questions

Clearly defines the questions the investigation attempts to answer.

Questions should remain open-ended rather than leading toward predetermined conclusions.

Investigation

Contains the actual research process.

May include:

architectural analysis
literature review
system comparison
case studies
thought experiments
failure analysis
hypothesis testing
cross-domain investigation
Multi-Agent Analysis

Documents the contributions from each research role.

Examples include:

Architect
Advocate
Skeptic
Cross-Domain Researcher
Synthesizer

Each role should independently contribute evidence before synthesis occurs.

Findings

Summarizes the major discoveries.

Findings should distinguish between:

confirmed knowledge
probable conclusions
unresolved questions
unexpected observations
Recommendations

Transforms findings into architectural recommendations.

Each recommendation should explain:

what should change
why
expected benefits
risks
implementation complexity
Architectural Impact

Explains how the recommendations affect ADE.

Possible impacts include:

architecture
orchestration
evaluation
learning
autonomy
scalability
reliability
usability
Evidence

Lists all evidence supporting the conclusions.

Evidence may include:

architectural reasoning
benchmarks
experiments
literature
comparative systems
engineering principles
Open Questions

Research rarely answers everything.

This section records:

unanswered questions
future investigations
assumptions requiring validation
potential research directions
Final Synthesis

Provides the final integrated conclusion.

This section reconciles disagreements between research roles and presents the final architectural assessment.

Quality Requirements

A completed research project should:

remain evidence-driven
avoid unsupported speculation
document uncertainty honestly
separate facts from opinions
clearly identify assumptions
justify recommendations
remain reproducible
Outputs

Every completed research project should produce:

Research Report
Findings Summary
Architectural Recommendations
Evidence Record
Open Questions
Integration Proposal

These outputs become inputs to the Research Integration process.

Continuous Learning

Each completed research project contributes to improving:

the research methodology,
future investigations,
architectural understanding,
and the long-term evolution of ADE.

Research projects are therefore cumulative, with each investigation building upon the knowledge established by previous work.

This template becomes the standard blueprint for all future investigations—for example:

Architect Research
Generator Research
Critic Research
Memory Research
Learning System Research
Autonomy Research
Multi-Agent Research
Evaluation Research
Design Constitution Research
Outer Loop Research

Every one of these can follow the same structure, making the entire research program consistent and easier to maintain.
