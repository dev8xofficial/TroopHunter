# Investigations Backlog

Permanent, append-mostly, ranked open questions and deferred strategic items for the Autonomous Design Engine (ADE).
Items are ranked by **EVI (Expected Value of Investigation) = (Impact × Probability × Reversibility) / Cost**.

## 1. The Missing Chairs (EG-3/EG-9)
**Context**: Reaching "complete" and "world-class" requires adding capabilities for copy, imagery/art-direction, iconography, and motion authoring. Currently out of scope.
**EVI**: High (High Impact, High Probability, Low Cost to specify).
**Status**: Deferred. Requires funding as named phases (e.g., R15-class) and the M15/E2.4 strategy layer.

## 2. Complex Design Workflows (EG-8)
**Context**: Extending ADE to handle multi-page, forms, email, dashboards, and cross-artifact orchestration. Currently outside the architecture bounds by decision (CA-14 / D2).
**EVI**: Medium (High Impact, High Cost).
**Status**: Deferred. Reversing this requires a new contract and must pass the per-surface capability checklist.

## 3. Global Search (R7)
**Context**: Searching across domains/surfaces for novel combinations (EG-11).
**EVI**: Medium (Medium Impact, High Cost).
**Status**: Deferred.

## 4. Aesthetic Drift (R17)
**Context**: The risk that the system's design style drifts over time (EG-7).
**EVI**: Low (Unknown Probability, High Cost to measure long-term).
**Status**: Deferred. Mentioned as a residual risk without a chunk.
