## 0. Verdict

**The End Goal, as written, is not achievable with the system as built.** Not because the architecture is wrong — its floor (gates, loop integrity, authority model, storage, governance posture) is the strongest part of the corpus and survived every attack — but because:

1. **Four End-Goal clauses have no owning mechanism anywhere in the system** (independent strategy formation; self-directed capability growth; adaptation across industries/technologies; complex multi-artifact workflows). They are not under-built — they are **unbuilt and mostly unscheduled**, parked in Tier-3/4 research bets the roadmap itself says "may never be reached at solo scale."
2. **Two clauses are unverifiable by the system's own measurement design** ("world-class / best human design teams" has no external referent anywhere in the evaluation stack; and the go/no-go H1 experiment is statistically confounded as pre-registered — see CF-1, the single most urgent finding in this document, because it sits under everything else).
3. **One clause is impossible by construction under the current substrate assumption** ("continuously increase its own design intelligence… beyond its original capabilities"): every learning mechanism in ADE — Library, calibration, constitution, reward model — changes *context and selection*, never *capability*. With frozen model weights, the generative ceiling is the frontier model of the day, better elicited. Self-transcendence is not in the physics of this design (CF-2).
4. **The binding resource ceiling is already known and unresolved:** taste-signal starvation at solo scale (AI-F1) caps calibration, the reward model, and every rung of the autonomy ladder. The End Goal multiplies the demand on exactly that scarce signal.
5. **As of HEAD, the system has *no* self-improvement mechanism at all:** commit `c2be66d0` deleted the entire Research Engine. The End Goal clause "identify weaknesses in its own performance, challenge its assumptions" currently has **no owner in the repository** (CF-9 — established).

**However: a bounded, staged form of the End Goal is genuinely achievable**, and the distance between "as built" and "achievable" is enumerable. §5 defines the bounded goal; §4 lists the 19 contract amendments (CA-1…CA-19) that close the closable gaps and honestly fence the unclosable ones. The deepest truth is unchanged from the prior investigation and bears repeating: **nothing has ever run.** Every claim in this contract — including its pessimistic ones — is cheaper to test than to keep debating.

## 2. Findings

Format per finding: **What breaks → Root cause → What must change.** Severity: **BLOCKING** (End Goal unreachable while open) · **STRUCTURAL** (caps a clause) · **SIGNIFICANT** (degrades or delays) · **HYGIENE** (contract grounding). ### Seat: Evaluation Scientist / Statistician

#### CF-1 — The H1 gate is confounded: as pre-registered, it cannot distinguish "seeing helps" from "sampling more helps" — **BLOCKING**
- **What breaks.** H1 is the go/no-go for the entire project: *"an agent that sees its own render produces a measurably better section on iteration N+1 than N."* The pre-registered pass metric (`spec/08 §2, §5`) is: (A) final Critic score > iter-0 score in ≥70% of ≥10 runs; (B) humans prefer final over iter-0 in ≥70% of blind pairs. Both prongs are inflated by **selection over more samples**, independent of whether critique feedback contributes anything:
  - The loop runs "explore early (≈3 candidates), polish late (1)" over up to 6 iterations (plan Appendix B/D) — the final best-so-far is the maximum over ~8 draws; iter-0's score is the maximum over ~3. Under the **null hypothesis of zero learning** (candidate quality i.i.d. across iterations), the probability that the run's best appears after iteration 0 — making prong A true — is approximately (8−3)/8 ≈ **0.63**. The pre-registered bar is 0.70. The gate's pass bar sits within noise of its own null.
  - The sample size compounds it: with n=10 runs, observing ≥7/10 under a 50% null has P ≈ 0.17; under the *actual* ~0.63 null, passing is closer to a coin flip. The experiment as designed has essentially no power to reject "the Eyes add nothing."
  - Prong B (human blind pairs) inherits the same confound: the final candidate was *selected by the Critic over ~2.7× more draws*; if Critic score correlates with human preference at all, humans will prefer the final even if critique feedback never improved a single candidate.
  - `spec/07 §8` criterion 2 ("iteration N+1 demonstrably addresses iteration N's feedback") is honest qualitative causal evidence and partially mitigates — but it is not in the quantitative gate, and "addresses the feedback" ≠ "is better than resampling would have been."
- **Root cause.** H1 was framed as *before/after within a run* rather than *loop vs. matched-compute control*. The confound is invisible in the framing because best-so-far retention (I4, correct engineering) guarantees monotonicity — which is precisely what makes the trend measure tautological.
- **What must change (CA-1).** Add a **matched-compute control arm** to the Phase-0 exit gate: for each brief, also generate the same total number of candidates *blind* (no critique feedback, diversity via temperature only), select the best by the same Critic, and define **H1-pass = loop output beats matched-compute blind best-of-N** (human blind preference, at significance). Additionally: raise n to ≥20–25 briefs or report exact binomial CIs and treat 10 as a pilot; and log per-iteration score deltas conditioned on feedback (loop *gain*) so the saturation curve comes free. A cheap in-run variant: interleave one feedback-conditioned and one fresh candidate in the same iteration and compare their win-rates — the causal question answered inside the existing budget.
- **Why this is the most urgent finding:** every downstream decision — the entire phase sequence, the kill rule, the credibility of the method — keys off H1. Running 20 weeks of Phase 0 and "passing" a gate that a null system would also pass would be **measurement theater (F-SPEC-05) at the foundation**, worse than no gate because it would license Phase 1–4 investment on noise.
- **Evidence:** T0 arithmetic on the spec's own pre-registered numbers + T1 (self-refinement literature: gains frequently attributable to selection; known winner's-curse bias when the selector and the scorer are the same model). **Confidence: Medium-High** for the confound's existence; the *magnitude* is measurable only by running it.
- **Falsifier:** if the loop beats matched-compute blind best-of-N cleanly, this finding retires and H1 is *stronger* than currently claimed.

#### CF-3 — "World-class" and "best human design teams" have no external referent anywhere in the system — **BLOCKING for EG-9/EG-11's quality bar**
- **What breaks.** Every quality signal in ADE terminates in one place: the owner's taste (verdicts, golden-core ratings, constitution ratification, exemplar scoring). The benchmark measures *Critic↔owner agreement* and *iter-0→final gain* — never *ADE vs. the outside world*. A system can climb this entire measurement stack monotonically and remain mediocre by world standards; F-HUM-02 (reviewer-taste overfitting) is catalogued as a failure but it is actually the **design**: there is nothing else to overfit to. The End Goal's bar — "comparable to the best human design teams" — is not merely unmet; it is **unmeasurable by the instruments as specified**. `spec/13 §3` requires multi-rater ratings (already unmeetable solo, AI-F1); even satisfied, multiple raters from the same circle measure consensus, not world-class.
- **Root cause.** The evaluation charter was designed to anchor *self-improvement* (stop reward-hacking of the self-loop) — the correct first job. It was never extended to anchor *absolute quality*, because the End Goal's bar post-dates the charter.
- **What must change (CA-2).** Add an **external-anchor track** to the charter: (a) a curated set of world-class reference works (rated exemplars from top studios/awards, refreshed annually) that golden-core outputs are blind-compared against; (b) periodic **blind side-by-sides vs. competitor tools** (v0, Lovable, Framer AI) and, where affordable, vs. commissioned human professional work on the same briefs; (c) report "distance from external anchor" as a standing metric beside Critic↔human agreement. This also operationalizes open-question #2 (build vs. buy) with data instead of debate.
- **Evidence:** T0 structural + established (no external comparison exists anywhere in spec/plan). **Confidence: High** that the referent is absent; Medium that the proposed track is sufficient.
- **Falsifier:** none needed — the absence is observable. The remedy's adequacy is falsified if blind external comparisons prove too noisy/expensive to sustain solo (then EG-9's bar must be rescoped, CA-17).

#### CF-4 — Autonomy-ladder relaxation lacks difficulty stratification and post-relaxation audit — **STRUCTURAL**
- **What breaks.** Gates relax when a boundary's Critic↔human agreement clears a threshold (`spec/09 §2`, C3.2). Agreement is measured on the *reviewed distribution* — dominated by ordinary cases. Trustworthiness on the tail (hard, novel, adversarial cases) is what unattended operation actually requires, and tail agreement is systematically lower than average agreement. The chat history (§12, cluster 18) named this exact risk ("agreement on easy cases isn't trustworthiness on hard ones"); **no mechanism landed** — C3.2 specifies threshold + drop-back-on-complaints, nothing else. Drop-back triggered by "quality complaints" means the failure has already shipped.
- **Root cause.** The ladder was specified before the benchmark design; stratification requires the benchmark to tag case difficulty, which nothing does.
- **What must change (CA-3).** (a) Difficulty/novelty strata in the golden core and frontier (the system-proposed adversarial cases, `spec/13 §5`, are the natural hard stratum); (b) rung promotion requires clearing the threshold **per stratum, including the hard stratum**; (c) every relaxed gate carries a **standing random-audit sample** (a fixed % of unattended passes re-reviewed by the human, forever) whose measured miss-rate — not complaints — is the drop-back trigger.
- **Evidence:** T0–T1. **Confidence: Medium.** **Falsifier:** empirical stratified agreement showing negligible easy/hard gap.

### Seat: Learning-Systems Scientist

#### CF-2 — No mechanism in ADE increases design *capability*; "beyond original capabilities" is impossible by construction as stated — **BLOCKING for EG-11 (as worded)**
- **What breaks.** Inventory every learning mechanism in the corpus: Library write-back (retrieved context), Critic calibration (prompt/rubric/exemplar tuning), constitution amendments (judging context), R4 reward model (a better *selector*), R2 channel (better *signal capture*). Every one changes **what the frozen base model is shown or how its outputs are filtered**. None changes what the model *can do*. The generative ceiling is therefore the latent design ability of the frontier model of the day, better elicited — a real and worthwhile gain, but **bounded and externally owned**. The End Goal's "continuously increase its own design intelligence… improve beyond its original capabilities" describes capability growth ADE cannot produce from inside: when elicitation saturates (and self-refinement gains empirically saturate within a few iterations — T1), the curve flattens until Anthropic ships a better model. ADE's improvement curve is a **staircase whose steps are cut by someone else**; ADE controls only how fully each step is exploited and how fast the next step is absorbed.
- **Root cause.** The Constitutional-AI analogy was imported minus its engine: Anthropic's outer loop terminates in *weight updates* (RLHF/RLAIF training). ADE's outer loop terminates in *prompts and selectors*. The analogy holds for governance; it silently fails for capability.
- **What must change.** Two honest paths, not mutually exclusive:
  - **(CA-4) Reframe and engineer for substrate succession.** Accept the staircase: ADE's *own* compounding assets are the model-independent ones — the verdict corpus, the golden core, the brief/strategy data, the deterministic gates (this is AI-F2/AI-F5, now with the End-Goal consequence drawn). Build the **model-succession subsystem** as a first-class capability: what survives a model swap (data, benchmark, library payloads), what must be re-earned (prompt calibration, thresholds, reward-model retrain), and the absorption playbook (re-baseline → recalibrate → re-verify gates). Today this exists only as "pin the id and re-baseline" (F-MOD-05) — a flinch, not a subsystem. Under this reframing, "continuously increase its design intelligence" becomes true *of the system* (data + anchors + succession) while false *of any frozen component* — and that wording change belongs in the End-Goal document itself (CA-17).
  - **(CA-5) Open a capability-training path where it is actually possible (prod-only, Phase 4+):** distillation/fine-tuning of the R4 judge (a small learned reward model *is* weight-level learning, on the judging side), and — far future, if volume ever justifies — generator fine-tuning on accumulated approved-work + preference data. Note honestly: both require the API/paid path and data volumes that intersect AI-F1.
- **Evidence:** T0 (mechanism inventory — exhaustive against the corpus) + T1. **Confidence: High** for the inventory claim (it is checkable by enumeration); Medium for saturation timing.
- **Falsifier:** demonstration of durable, compounding *capability* gain (not selection gain) from context/selection mechanisms alone across model-held-constant conditions — e.g., H6 ablation showing quality gains that grow with Library size without bound. (The finding predicts plateau.)

#### CF-8 — Design knowledge is stored as prose; the compounding asset is text about pictures — **SIGNIFICANT**
- **What breaks.** Library entries embed/store natural-language lessons (`intent`, `construction[]`, `rationale[]` — `spec/03 §2`). Visual/spatial knowledge is lossy in both directions: distillation discards what made the artifact work beyond words; regeneration re-interprets the words through the model's priors — which already *contain* most articulable design prose ("editorial heroes avoid gradient overlays" is in every frontier model). The marginal value of a text pattern over the model's prior may be ~zero, which is exactly the H6 failure mode (F-LRN-01) — and the current design guarantees H6 is tested on the *weakest possible representation* of accumulated knowledge. Meanwhile the one store of visual ground truth the system will accumulate — approved screenshots with verdicts — is deliberately excluded from generation context (exemplars held out; approved work only re-entered as text).
- **Root cause.** De-identification (I7, correct) was conflated with de-visualization: stripping client identity was implemented by reducing artifacts to abstract prose, because text is what the de-id gate knows how to scan.
- **What must change (CA-6).** Make the H6 ablation **three-armed**: Library-off vs. text-Library vs. **multimodal entries** (de-identified/redacted thumbnail + structural payload + text), so the representation question is answered with the same experiment that tests compounding. Define de-id rules for images (client marks/copy redaction) before arm three. If AI-F2's rescoping (own-client memory first) is adopted, own-client visual retrieval needs no de-id at all — the cheapest test of visual memory arrives essentially free.
- **Evidence:** T0–T1. **Confidence: Medium.** **Falsifier:** text-arm ≈ multimodal-arm in the ablation.

#### CF-10 — The substrate is non-stationary and the plan treats it as fixed — **STRUCTURAL for EG-7**
- **What breaks.** Over the multi-year horizon the End Goal implies, *everything under ADE moves*: models deprecate (F-OPS-05), prompts get tuned, the constitution amends, embeddings change (F-MEM-03), aesthetics drift (L1), even the owner's taste drifts (AI-F8). Each drift is individually catalogued; the **joint dynamics are unmanaged**: verdicts collected on model-M1 outputs partially mistrain a reward model judging M2's distribution; calibration thresholds earned on last year's output distribution silently expire; benchmark ratings age (F-SPEC-06 knows this for the core, but the *verdict corpus* has no aging model at all). "Adapt to new technologies/tools" (EG-7) additionally collides with the hard-committed output representation (React+Tailwind, `.tsx` single-file contract) — a reasonable scoped choice, but adaptation to a new target stack is a Generator-contract + harness + gate rewrite that nothing in the plan owns.
- **Root cause.** The outer loop was specified as if measuring a stationary process; non-stationarity was handled point-wise (pinning, re-baselining) rather than as a property of the whole learning system.
- **What must change (CA-7).** Add a **non-stationarity section to spec/13** (what ages, at what rate, what re-earning it costs): distribution-tag every verdict (model id + system version at collection — the trace already has the fields; carry them into the verdict schema); define reward-model retraining triggers on distribution shift; give the output-representation commitment an explicit revisit trigger (new dominant stack). New failure entries: verdict-distribution staleness; calibration non-transfer across model swaps (distinct from F-LRN-02's domain non-transfer).
- **Evidence:** T0–T1. **Confidence: Medium.**

#### CF-6 — The output space is a strict subset of design; a "design organization" authors what ADE can only arrange — **STRUCTURAL for EG-3/EG-9**

#### CF-13 — The taste-anchoring paradox: novelty the anchor doesn't recognize is selected against — **SIGNIFICANT for EG-11**
- **What breaks.** EG-11 wants the system to "discover better design approaches." ADE's selection stack — Critic pass gates, human verdicts, Library confidence, constitution — all reward what the anchor (ultimately, the owner's taste, itself calibrated by the same loop, AI-F8) *already recognizes as good*. A genuinely better approach that reads as wrong to the anchor is filtered at every layer; the P4/P8 distinctiveness principles push against the mean but are still *judged by the same anchor*. This is not a flaw to eliminate (an unanchored system is F-JDG-02 reward-hacking) — it is a trade-off to manage; but as built, nothing manages it: no protected exploration budget, no mechanism by which a rejected-but-interesting direction survives, and the only true escape (external *outcome* signal — did the design work in the world, R16) is Tier-4 "far future."
- **What must change (CA-12).** (a) A small **protected exploration quota** (one of N candidates per early iteration exempt from best-so-far replacement pressure, logged for later human review — cheap); (b) rejected-with-interest verdicts as a first-class label in the R2 channel (feeds R13 trajectory learning); (c) pull a *coarse* outcome signal forward from R16 where trivially available (deploy analytics on the owner's own shipped projects) rather than waiting for the full Tier-4 apparatus.
- **Evidence:** T0–T1 (exploration/exploitation literature). **Confidence: Medium.**

### Seat: Systems Architect

#### CF-7 — Every judge in the system shares one set of priors; "fresh context" decorrelates state, not blind spots — **STRUCTURAL** *(sharpens G3 + the deleted engine's own doctrine)*
- **What breaks.** Generator, Critic, Phase-Exit Reviews (brand/PDS/library), whole-artifact QA, brief comprehension — one model family. I2's fresh context removes *conversational* contamination (real, valuable) but every gate that "independently checks" an artifact checks it with the same aesthetic priors, the same systematic biases (F-JDG-07), the same failure modes that produced it. The now-deleted Research Engine stated the principle exactly ("agreement between same-substrate perspectives is ~zero evidence") — **for itself**; ADE's runtime never received the same medicine. Consequence for the End Goal: as human gates relax (EG-10), the correlated-blind-spot risk *grows*, because the human was the only decorrelated judge in the system.
- **What must change (CA-13).** (a) High-stakes boundaries (brand approval pre-filter, PDS freeze, rung-promotion evaluations) get a **second judge from a different model family** — the `local`/Ollama provider slot makes this possible key-free in dev, imperfect but decorrelated; (b) a **bias-probe suite** in the benchmark (order swaps, verbosity variations, style-transfer probes) with measured verdict stability, per C3.1 but promoted from Phase 3 to the benchmark's birth (C1.13); (c) report same-model agreement and cross-model agreement separately — the gap between them is the measured size of this problem.
- **Evidence:** T1. **Confidence: Medium-High.**

### Seat: Security / Red Team

#### CF-15 — The sandbox contradicts its own harness: deny-all egress vs. runtime CDN dependencies — **SIGNIFICANT, cheap to fix**
- **What breaks.** C0.4/`spec/07 §6.2` mandate deny-by-default network egress for the render sandbox, in the same breath as Tailwind via **Play CDN** (`<script src="https://cdn.tailwindcss.com">`) and Google-Fonts loading (chat-history §3 harness hardening) — the render *cannot complete* under its own network policy. An implementing agent hits this contradiction on day one and resolves it ad hoc (probably by weakening egress). Adjacent: font substitution (Canela→serif fallback) is recorded but still means the Critic grades brand typography that isn't the brand's — a standing distortion of `brand_adherence` no gate accounts for.
- **What must change (CA-15).** **Self-host** the pinned Play-CDN script and all fonts inside the harness (removes the live third-party dependency, satisfies deny-all, strengthens F-OPS-07 pinning); where a commercial face is unavailable, the substitution note must reach the **Critic prompt** as an explicit caveat ("grade layout/weight, not letterforms") — not just the trace.

## 5. The Bounded Goal (what "achievable" honestly means)

With the contract applied, the achievable system — call it the **Bounded Goal**, proposed as the ratified near-horizon objective (CA-17):

> ADE autonomously produces multi-section marketing artifacts from a brief under frozen brand law: measurably improving within each run **beyond matched-compute resampling** (repaired H1), consistent without monotony (H4), rated good-or-close by its human at a steady majority (H2) **and periodically bench-marked against external work** (CA-2); its judge's agreement with its human is measured per boundary and per difficulty stratum, and human gates relax only where that evidence — plus standing audits — say so; its compounding assets (verdict corpus, golden core, strategy-decision data, own-client memory) are model-independent and survive substrate succession; and its weaknesses are hunted by a named, running self-investigation mechanism.

That is EG-1 (partial), EG-4, EG-6, EG-10, and honest fractions of EG-3/EG-9 — a system worth building, describable without a single hedge.

**What remains North Star, with its price tags:** EG-2 (strategy — CA-8, real research capability, and calendar time); EG-3/EG-9 in full ("complete solutions," "world-class org" — the missing chairs, CA-11(b), plus external-anchor evidence, plus signal economics beyond one rater); EG-7 (substrate-succession discipline exercised over years); EG-8 (a workflow architecture that doesn't exist); EG-11 as worded (**cannot be met by any frozen-weight system as designed** — met only in the reframed sense of CA-4/CA-5: the *system's* intelligence compounds through data, anchors, and substrate succession, even though no component improves itself).

## 6. Bottom line

The question was whether the End Goal is achievable with the system as built. The surgical answer:

- **As built and as operated today: no.** Four clauses have no mechanism, two are unmeasurable by the system's own instruments, one is impossible by construction as worded, the binding signal ceiling is unresolved, the self-improvement subsystem was deleted, and — underneath everything — **zero experiments have ever run**, so even the achievable clauses are currently claims on paper.
- **The architecture is not the problem.** Nothing found here (or in the prior investigation) breaks the skeleton: gates, loop, authority model, storage, governance posture all survived full-force attack from every seat. Every blocker lives in **signal, scope, measurement design, and follow-through** — all of which are choices.
- **The contract is the path.** CA-21→20→18 this week; CA-1 before measurement; CA-9/15 folded into the build; the rest at their named phases; CA-11/14/17 are owner decisions that cost a paragraph each and change the truth-value of the entire End Goal.
- **And the oldest finding still dominates:** the single highest-leverage act available is unchanged — restore the spike, build Phase 0, and let evidence start flowing. This contract exists so that when that evidence arrives, it lands on a measurement design that can actually bear its weight.
